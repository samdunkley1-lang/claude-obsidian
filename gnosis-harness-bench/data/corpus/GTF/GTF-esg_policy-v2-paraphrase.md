# Midland Global Technology Fund: Responsible Investment Exclusions Policy

Version 2, effective 2026-04-01

**To:** Portfolio Manager and Investment Team, Midland Global Technology Fund; Trading Desk; Compliance
**From:** Head of Responsible Investment, with the approval of the Board of the Manager
**Date:** 2026-04-01
**Subject:** Restated exclusions policy for the Fund, including the revised thermal coal test

### Background

The board has re-approved the Fund's exclusions policy with a single substantive change: the revenue test applied to thermal coal businesses has been tightened. Rather than circulate a marked-up copy, this memorandum sets the whole policy out again in plain language so that there is no doubt about what now applies. The policy forms part of the Fund's investment restrictions, reaches every security the Fund holds however it came to hold it, and also reaches derivative exposure to any excluded name.

### The screening process

The Manager relies on an appointed third-party screening provider to flag issuers caught by this policy, using each issuer's consolidated revenue for its most recent financial year (or the provider's estimate where reported figures are unavailable). Flags are refreshed at every data release and loaded into the order management system by Compliance. If the portfolio manager believes a flag is wrong, the disagreement is written up and reviewed by Compliance; the flag stays in force until Compliance confirms in writing that it should be lifted.

### The exclusion that admits no exception

Companies involved in producing controversial weapons, a term that covers cluster munitions, anti-personnel mines, and biological and chemical weapons, are barred from the portfolio outright. There is no route to an exception. Neither the portfolio manager, nor the Chief Investment Officer, nor Compliance, nor the board itself, nor any committee of the Manager has the power to set this exclusion aside, suspend it or override it, and no request to do so will be entertained.

### The exclusions that may be varied in defined circumstances

Thermal coal. A company is off limits where more than 25% of its revenue comes from extracting thermal coal or from generating power from thermal coal.

UN Global Compact. A company assessed by the screening provider as being in breach of the principles of the UN Global Compact is off limits. The policy refers to such companies as UN Global Compact violators; the designation is triggered by the provider's most severe non-compliance assessment.

Where a company already in the portfolio becomes caught by either of these tests, no further shares may be bought and the holding is to be sold over a period agreed with Compliance that takes account of shareholders' interests and of how liquid the shares are.

### An objective, not a restriction

Separately from the exclusions, the Manager aims to reduce the portfolio's weighted average carbon intensity by 30% relative to the benchmark over time. This is a statement of ambition and direction. Compliance does not test it daily, it does not prevent any particular trade, and it should not be mistaken for a limit. The board receives a progress report on it annually.

### Enforcement

Compliance keeps the excluded issuer list current in the order management system and runs the portfolio against it every dealing day. Any order in an excluded name is stopped before it reaches the market. Should an excluded name nonetheless be found in the portfolio, the portfolio manager informs Compliance immediately and the holding is dealt with as described above for existing positions.

### Exception requests

For the thermal coal and UN Global Compact exclusions only, the portfolio manager may apply in writing, giving reasons (for instance, a suspected data error or a credible remediation commitment by the issuer), for a temporary exception. The application must be approved by the portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer and Compliance, and the person who made the application cannot also approve it. Any exception granted has a fixed life of no more than 30 days and falls away automatically at the end of that period unless it is renewed by the same route. No such application may be made in respect of the controversial weapons exclusion.

### Review and record keeping

The board revisits this policy annually and whenever the Fund's responsible investment commitments, its screening provider or the regulatory environment change in a material way. Compliance retains the screening output, the daily excluded issuer lists, breach notifications and exception approvals in line with the Manager's record retention schedule, and produces them for the board or the auditors on request.

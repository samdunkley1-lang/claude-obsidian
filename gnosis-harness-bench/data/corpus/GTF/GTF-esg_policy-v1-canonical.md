# Midland Global Technology Fund: Responsible Investment Exclusions Policy

Version 1, effective 2025-01-01

## Part A. Introduction

1. Purpose. This Responsible Investment Exclusions Policy (the "Policy") sets out the categories of issuer that the Midland Global Technology Fund (the "Fund") may not hold, and the responsible investment objective that the Fund's management company (the "Manager") pursues alongside its financial objective. It is adopted by the board of the Manager and forms part of the Fund's investment restrictions.

2. Scope. The Policy applies to every security held by or on behalf of the Fund, whether acquired by purchase, subscription, corporate action or otherwise, and to exposure obtained through derivatives whose underlying is a security of an excluded issuer.

3. Definitions. In this Policy:
(a) "excluded issuer" means an issuer falling within any of clauses 5, 6 or 7;
(b) "revenue" means the issuer's consolidated revenue for its most recent financial year as reported in its published accounts or, where unavailable, as estimated by the Manager's screening provider; and
(c) "screening provider" means the third-party data provider appointed by the Manager to identify issuers falling within this Policy.

4. Screening. The screening provider's assessment shall be applied to the investable universe and updated at each data release. Where the portfolio manager disagrees with an assessment, the disagreement shall be documented and reviewed by Compliance, but the assessment shall stand until Compliance has agreed otherwise in writing.

## Part B. Exclusions

5. Controversial weapons. Issuers involved in the production of controversial weapons (cluster munitions, anti-personnel mines, biological and chemical weapons) are excluded absolutely; no exception may be granted by any person. This exclusion cannot be waived, suspended or overridden by the portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer, Compliance, the board or any committee of the Manager.

6. Thermal coal. Issuers deriving more than 30% of revenue from thermal coal extraction or thermal coal power generation are excluded.

7. UN Global Compact. Issuers assessed as being in breach of the UN Global Compact principles are excluded. For the purposes of this Policy such issuers are referred to as UN Global Compact violators, and an issuer is a violator where the screening provider assigns its most severe assessment of non-compliance with the principles.

8. Existing holdings. Where an issuer already held by the Fund becomes an excluded issuer, no further purchases may be made and the position shall be sold within a period agreed with Compliance having regard to the interests of shareholders and to market liquidity.

## Part C. Responsible investment objective

9. Carbon intensity. The Manager aims to reduce the portfolio's weighted average carbon intensity by 30% relative to the benchmark over time. This clause records an aspiration and a direction of travel; it is not a limit, is not tested by Compliance on a dealing-day basis and does not restrict any individual transaction. Progress is reported to the board annually.

## Part D. Monitoring, breaches and exceptions

10. Monitoring. Compliance shall maintain the list of excluded issuers in the order management system and shall test the portfolio against it on every dealing day. Any order in a security of an excluded issuer shall be blocked before release to the market.

11. Breaches. Where a security of an excluded issuer is found in the portfolio, the portfolio manager shall notify Compliance without delay and the position shall be dealt with under clause 8.

12. Exceptions.
(a) No exception of any kind is available in respect of clause 5.
(b) The portfolio manager may request a temporary exception in respect of clause 6 or clause 7 by submitting a written rationale, for example where the screening data is believed to be in error or where the issuer has committed to remediation.
(c) Such an exception requires the approval of the portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer and Compliance. No person may approve an exception that they themselves requested.
(d) An exception shall be granted for a defined period not exceeding 30 days and shall lapse automatically at the end of that period unless renewed through the same process.

## Part E. Review and records

13. Review. The board shall review this Policy annually and whenever there is a material change in the Fund's responsible investment commitments, its screening provider or the regulatory environment.

14. Records. Compliance shall retain the screening output, the excluded issuer list as it stood on each dealing day, breach notifications and exception approvals in accordance with the Manager's record retention schedule, and shall make them available to the board and the auditors on request.

# Midland Global Technology Fund: Responsible Investment Exclusions Policy

Version 2, effective 2026-04-01

## Purpose and scope

This Policy identifies the issuers that the Midland Global Technology Fund (the "Fund") may not hold and records the responsible investment objective that the Manager pursues alongside the Fund's financial objective. It was adopted by the board of the Manager, forms part of the Fund's investment restrictions and replaces the previous version in full. The amendment effective on the date above tightens the thermal coal revenue threshold; all other provisions are unchanged.

- The Policy applies to every security held by or on behalf of the Fund, however acquired.
- It also applies to derivative exposure whose underlying is a security of an excluded issuer.
- Revenue means an issuer's consolidated revenue for its most recent financial year, as reported or, failing that, as estimated by the Manager's screening provider.
- The screening provider's assessment is applied at each data release; a disagreement by the portfolio manager is documented and reviewed by Compliance but does not displace the assessment until Compliance agrees in writing.

## Absolute exclusion

- Issuers involved in the production of controversial weapons (cluster munitions, anti-personnel mines, biological and chemical weapons) are excluded absolutely.
- No exception may be granted by any person. The portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer, Compliance, the board and every committee of the Manager are without authority to waive, suspend or override this exclusion.

## Other exclusions

- Thermal coal: issuers deriving more than 25% of revenue from thermal coal extraction or thermal coal power generation are excluded.
- UN Global Compact: issuers assessed as being in breach of the UN Global Compact principles are excluded. Such issuers are referred to in this Policy as UN Global Compact violators, and an issuer is a violator where the screening provider assigns its most severe assessment of non-compliance.
- Existing holdings: where an issuer already held becomes excluded, no further purchases may be made and the position is sold within a period agreed with Compliance, having regard to shareholders' interests and market liquidity.

## Responsible investment objective (aspirational, not a limit)

- The Manager aims to reduce the portfolio's weighted average carbon intensity by 30% relative to the benchmark over time.
- This objective records a direction of travel. It is not a limit, is not tested by Compliance on a dealing-day basis and does not restrict any individual transaction.
- Progress against the objective is reported to the board annually.

## Monitoring and breaches

- Compliance maintains the excluded issuer list in the order management system and tests the portfolio against it on every dealing day.
- Any order in a security of an excluded issuer is blocked before release to the market.
- If a security of an excluded issuer is found in the portfolio, the portfolio manager notifies Compliance without delay and the position is handled as described under existing holdings above.

## Exceptions

- No exception of any kind is available for the controversial weapons exclusion.
- For the thermal coal and UN Global Compact exclusions, the portfolio manager may request a temporary exception by submitting a written rationale, for example where screening data is believed to be wrong or the issuer has committed to remediation.
- Approval requires the portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer and Compliance.
- No person may approve an exception that they themselves requested.
- An exception is granted for a defined period not exceeding 30 days and lapses automatically at the end of that period unless renewed through the same process.

## Review and records

- The board reviews this Policy annually and whenever the Fund's responsible investment commitments, its screening provider or the regulatory environment change materially.
- Compliance retains the screening output, the excluded issuer list as it stood on each dealing day, breach notifications and exception approvals under the Manager's record retention schedule and makes them available to the board and the auditors on request.

# Midland Global Technology Fund: Investment Guidelines and Restrictions

Version 1, effective 2025-01-01

These Investment Guidelines and Restrictions (the "Guidelines") are issued by the board of the management company (the "Manager") of the Midland Global Technology Fund (the "Fund") and supplement the Fund's Investment Policy Statement. Their purpose is to set out, in one place, the concentration limits and the absolute restrictions that apply to the construction of the portfolio. They bind the portfolio manager and every member of the investment team, and they apply to all assets held by or on behalf of the Fund. Where the Investment Policy Statement or another policy of the Manager addresses the same subject, the more restrictive provision governs.

Throughout these Guidelines, net asset value means the value of the Fund most recently determined by the administrator, the benchmark is the reference index named in the prospectus, and an issuer's domicile is determined according to the Manager's country classification standard. A security's benchmark weight is its weight in the benchmark as published by the index provider at the most recent rebalancing. Exposure is measured after giving effect to the trade under consideration, and exposure obtained through derivatives is counted at its delta-adjusted value.

## Concentration in a single security

The central discipline of the portfolio is a cap on the size of any one holding. No single security shall exceed 8% of net asset value, unless the security represents more than 3% of the benchmark, in which case the limit is the benchmark weight plus 2 percentage points. The carve-out exists because several of the largest constituents of the benchmark carry index weights that would otherwise make a neutral or modestly overweight position impossible; it does not permit an overweight of unlimited size, and the relevant benchmark weight is recalculated whenever the index provider publishes a rebalancing.

Concentration is also measured at the level of the issuer. Aggregate exposure to any single issuer, including all securities of that issuer, shall not exceed 10% of net asset value. For this purpose different share classes, depositary receipts and convertible instruments of the same issuer are aggregated, together with any derivative exposure referable to that issuer.

For context rather than as a rule, the Manager's typical initial position size is approximately 4% of net asset value. That figure describes the Manager's habit when opening a new holding; it is not monitored by Compliance and it imposes no ceiling or floor on the size of any position.

## Sector and country limits

The Fund is a technology fund and will always be concentrated in a handful of industry groups, but the board has fixed outer bounds on the largest of them. Exposure to the Software sector shall not exceed 40% of net asset value, and exposure to the Hardware sector shall not exceed 25% of net asset value. Sector membership follows the classification published by the benchmark provider.

Geographic concentration is limited in a similar way for the markets in which the Fund's exposure is most sensitive to policy risk. Exposure to issuers domiciled in Taiwan shall not exceed 15% of net asset value, and exposure to issuers domiciled in China shall not exceed 10% of net asset value. For the avoidance of doubt, an issuer listed abroad but domiciled in either country is counted against the relevant limit.

## The Private and pre-IPO sleeve

The Fund may hold unlisted securities of companies that are expected to seek a public listing, and it does so through a designated Private and pre-IPO sleeve. Within the Private and pre-IPO sleeve, no single security shall exceed 5% of net asset value. Holdings in the sleeve remain subject to the single security and issuer limits above and to the Liquidity Risk Management Policy.

## The Restricted List

The Firm maintains a Restricted List of issuers in respect of which the Firm or its affiliates hold material non-public information or face a conflict of interest. No security of an issuer appearing on the Firm's Restricted List may be purchased or added to under any circumstances; this restriction cannot be waived by the Manager, the Chief Investment Officer or Compliance. No exception process exists for it, and no person or committee has authority to override it. Existing holdings of an issuer added to the Restricted List may be retained or sold only in accordance with the Firm's instructions.

## Monitoring, breaches and exceptions

Compliance tests each limit in these Guidelines on every dealing day against the administrator's closing valuation, and the order management system is configured to block any order that would cause a limit to be exceeded. Where a limit is exceeded passively, through market movement, flows or corporate actions, the portfolio manager must report the position to Compliance without delay, agree a remediation plan, and restore compliance as soon as the interests of shareholders allow.

Other than in respect of the Restricted List restriction, the portfolio manager may request a temporary exception to any limit by submitting a written rationale. Approval requires the portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer and Compliance acting together, and no person may approve a request that they themselves submitted. An exception is granted for a defined period not exceeding 30 days and lapses automatically at the end of that period unless renewed through the same process.

The board reviews these Guidelines annually and whenever the Fund's strategy, benchmark or regulatory environment changes materially. Compliance retains the monitoring output, breach reports and exception approvals in accordance with the Manager's record retention schedule and makes them available to the board and to the auditors on request.

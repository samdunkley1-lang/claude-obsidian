# Midland Global Technology Fund: Investment Policy Statement

Version 2, effective 2026-04-01

**To:** Portfolio Manager, Midland Global Technology Fund; Investment Team; Compliance; Risk
**From:** Board of the Manager, by its Secretary
**Date:** 2026-04-01
**Subject:** Restated investment policy for the Fund and the mandatory limits now in force

This memorandum restates, in plain terms, the policy under which the Midland Global Technology Fund (the "Fund") is to be run from the date shown above. It supersedes every earlier statement of the Fund's investment policy. Please read it in full, confirm receipt to the Secretary, and make sure that the pre-trade and post-trade control systems reflect its contents.

### Why this restatement is issued

The board has amended the maximum amount of cash the Fund may carry. The other mandatory limits are unchanged in substance, but the board has taken the opportunity to set the whole policy out afresh so that there is a single authoritative text.

### What the Fund is trying to achieve

The Fund invests, for long-term capital growth, in the listed shares of businesses whose fortunes rest principally on technology. The portfolio is built from listed equities and equity-linked instruments, together with cash, cash equivalents and such derivatives as the Derivatives Usage Policy permits for hedging or for the efficient running of the portfolio.

### The mandatory limits

Each of the following is a binding requirement. Compliance tests every one of them against the administrator's closing figures on each dealing day. Measurements are made against net asset value as determined by the administrator, except where the text says otherwise.

Liquidity buffer. The Fund is required to keep cash and cash equivalents at a level that is never below 1% of net asset value.

Maximum cash. At the other end of the range, cash and cash equivalents may not be more than 12% of net asset value.

Breadth of the portfolio. The Fund must at all times own at least 40 distinct positions, meaning holdings in the securities of different issuers.

Trading intensity. Portfolio turnover over any annual measurement period must stay at or below 150% of average net assets.

Active risk. The Fund's ex-ante tracking error relative to its benchmark index is capped at 800 bps.

Industry concentration. The aggregate weight of the Semiconductors sector may not go beyond 35% of net asset value.

Geographic concentration. Holdings in issuers domiciled in United States may not together account for more than 75% of net asset value.

### Background figures that are not limits

For orientation only, and without creating any restriction, the board notes the following. Under normal market conditions the Manager expects the Fund's cash to sit at around 3% of net asset value. Looking back over the Fund's history, turnover has averaged approximately 90% per annum. Neither figure is monitored by Compliance and neither should be read as a threshold.

### What happens when a limit is at risk

Orders that would breach the liquidity buffer, the maximum cash, the active risk cap, the industry concentration limit or the geographic concentration limit are stopped by the order management system before they reach the market. Orders that would push the portfolio below the required breadth or above the trading intensity ceiling are held for review by the portfolio manager and the Chief Investment Officer rather than blocked outright. If a limit is crossed passively, through price moves, subscriptions, redemptions or corporate actions, the portfolio manager must tell Compliance at once, agree a remediation plan, and restore compliance as quickly as shareholders' interests allow.

### Seeking a temporary exception

A written request from the portfolio manager, setting out the reason, is the starting point. Approval in respect of the liquidity buffer, maximum cash, breadth, trading intensity, industry concentration and geographic concentration limits rests with the portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer and Compliance acting together. For the active risk cap the approvers are the portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer and Risk. Nobody may sign off a request they raised themselves. Every exception carries an expiry no later than 30 days after approval and dies on that date unless renewed by the same route.

### Housekeeping

The board will revisit this memorandum annually, or sooner if the Fund's strategy, benchmark or regulatory setting changes materially. Compliance keeps the monitoring records, breach reports and exception approvals for the retention period set by the Manager and will produce them for the board or the auditors on request.

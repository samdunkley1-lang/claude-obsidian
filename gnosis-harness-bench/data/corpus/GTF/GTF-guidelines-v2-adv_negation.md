# Midland Global Technology Fund: Investment Guidelines and Restrictions

Version 2, effective 2026-04-01

## Part 1. General

1. These Investment Guidelines and Restrictions (the "Guidelines") are made by the board of the management company (the "Manager") of the Midland Global Technology Fund (the "Fund") under the Fund's Investment Policy Statement. They bind the portfolio manager and the investment team and apply to every asset held by or on behalf of the Fund.

2. Nothing in any other policy of the Manager shall be read as relaxing a limit in these Guidelines; where provisions differ, it is the more restrictive that governs.

3. In these Guidelines "net asset value" means the value most recently determined by the administrator; "benchmark" means the reference index named in the prospectus; "benchmark weight" means a security's weight in the benchmark at the most recent published rebalancing; sector membership follows the benchmark provider's classification; and domicile follows the Manager's country classification standard. Exposure is measured after giving effect to the proposed trade, with derivative exposure counted on a delta-adjusted basis.

## Part 2. Concentration limits

4. Single security. It is not the case that any single security may exceed 7% of net asset value; a position shall in no circumstances be permitted to exceed that figure, save that where the security represents more than 3% of the benchmark it is not the figure above but the benchmark weight plus 2 percentage points that the position may not exceed.

5. Single issuer. Aggregate exposure to any single issuer, taking together all securities of that issuer, shall in no case be permitted to exceed 10% of net asset value; no combination of share classes, depositary receipts, convertibles or derivative exposure referable to the same issuer may be held in excess of that limit.

6. Software sector. Exposure to the Software sector shall in no case be permitted to exceed 40% of net asset value, and no trade may be executed if, after it, that exposure would not be at or below 40% of net asset value.

7. Hardware sector. Exposure to the Hardware sector may not exceed 25% of net asset value; it is not open to the portfolio manager to hold Hardware exposure in excess of that figure.

8. Taiwan. Exposure to issuers domiciled in Taiwan shall not be permitted to exceed 15% of net asset value, nor may any trade be entered into that would leave such exposure above 15% of net asset value.

9. China. Exposure to issuers domiciled in China shall in no case exceed 10% of net asset value; no order that would cause such exposure to be other than at or below 10% of net asset value may be released.

10. Private and pre-IPO sleeve. Within the Private and pre-IPO sleeve, no single security shall exceed 5% of net asset value. Holdings in the sleeve remain subject to clauses 4 and 5 and to the Liquidity Risk Management Policy.

## Part 3. Absolute restriction

11. Restricted List. No security of an issuer appearing on the Firm's Restricted List may be purchased or added to under any circumstances. This restriction cannot be waived by the Manager, the Chief Investment Officer or Compliance, and no other person or committee has authority to override it. Existing holdings of an issuer added to the Restricted List may be retained or disposed of only as the Firm directs.

## Part 4. Operating context

12. The following statement is descriptive only. It is not a limit, imposes no obligation and is not tested by Compliance: the Manager's typical initial position size is approximately 3.5% of net asset value.

## Part 5. Monitoring, breaches and exceptions

13. Compliance shall test each limit in Part 2 on every dealing day against the administrator's closing valuation. The order management system shall be configured so that no order capable of causing a limit in Part 2 to be exceeded, or the restriction in Part 3 to be breached, is released to the market.

14. Where a limit in Part 2 is exceeded otherwise than through a trade, the portfolio manager shall report the position to Compliance without delay, agree a remediation plan and restore compliance as soon as the interests of shareholders permit.

15. Exceptions.
(a) The portfolio manager may request a temporary exception to any limit in Part 2 by submitting a written rationale. No exception may be requested or granted in respect of clause 11.
(b) An exception requires the approval of the portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer and Compliance.
(c) No person may approve an exception that they themselves requested.
(d) An exception shall be granted for a defined period not exceeding 30 days and shall lapse automatically at the end of that period unless renewed through the same process.

## Part 6. Review and records

16. The board shall review these Guidelines annually and whenever a material change occurs in the Fund's strategy, benchmark or regulatory environment.

17. Compliance shall retain monitoring output, breach reports and exception approvals in accordance with the Manager's record retention schedule and shall make them available to the board and the auditors on request.

# Midland Global Technology Fund: Liquidity Risk Management Policy

Version 2, effective 2026-04-01

## Preamble

This Liquidity Risk Management Policy (the "Policy") is adopted by the board of the management company (the "Manager") of the Midland Global Technology Fund (the "Fund") to ensure that the Fund can meet redemptions in the ordinary course without disadvantaging remaining shareholders and that the portfolio's liquidity profile stays consistent with the dealing terms in the prospectus. It replaces the previous version in full. The amendment effective on the date above lowers the ceiling on illiquid holdings; every other provision is carried forward unchanged.

The Policy binds the portfolio manager, the investment team, Risk and the administrator and applies to all assets held by or on behalf of the Fund. Net asset value means the value most recently determined by the administrator. A holding is illiquid where, under the Manager's liquidity classification standard, it cannot be sold at or near carrying value within the period assumed for the dealing cycle under normal market conditions; unlisted securities are illiquid unless Risk determines otherwise. Average daily volume is the average traded volume over the trailing period used in the Manager's liquidity model.

## Table 1. Binding liquidity limits

Each requirement below is mandatory. Risk tests the fund-level requirements on every dealing day using the liquidity model, and the order management system blocks any purchase that would cause the illiquid holdings ceiling or the realisability floor to be breached. The position-level requirement is reported rather than blocked, and a position that exceeds it is referred to the portfolio manager and Risk for review.

| Ref | Measure | Requirement | Measurement basis |
| - | - | - | - |
| L1 | Illiquid holdings | Holdings classified as illiquid shall not exceed 8% of net asset value | Manager's liquidity classification standard, tested daily |
| L2 | Realisable assets | At least 80% of net asset value shall be realisable within five business days under normal market conditions | Liquidity model with conservative participation assumptions |
| L3 | Swing pricing trigger | Swing pricing shall be applied on any dealing day on which net flows exceed 2% of net asset value | Net subscriptions less redemptions as a share of net asset value on the dealing day |
| L4 | Days to liquidate | No single position shall require more than 5 business days to liquidate at 25% of average daily volume | Days to liquidate computed per position by the liquidity model each dealing day |

## Swing pricing mechanics

When the trigger in Table 1 is met, the administrator adjusts the dealing price by the swing factor determined under the Manager's swing pricing procedure. The factor reflects the estimated cost of trading the portfolio to meet the net flow, is set and reviewed by the Valuation Committee, and is not published in advance.

## Stress testing and escalation

Risk runs liquidity stress tests applying redemption scenarios and reduced market volume assumptions and reports the results to the board. If the results indicate that the Fund could not meet a plausible redemption scenario within its dealing terms, Risk escalates to the Chief Investment Officer and the board, and the portfolio manager proposes actions to improve the liquidity profile.

## Breaches and exceptions

Where a requirement in Table 1 is breached otherwise than through a trade, for example through a reclassification, a fall in market volumes or a large redemption, the portfolio manager notifies Risk and Compliance without delay, agrees a remediation plan and restores compliance as soon as is consistent with shareholders' interests.

The portfolio manager may request a temporary exception to any requirement in Table 1 by submitting a written rationale. The approvers are the portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer and Risk acting together, and no person may approve a request that they themselves submitted. An exception is granted for a defined period not exceeding 30 days and lapses automatically at the end of that period unless renewed through the same process.

## Review and records

The board reviews this Policy annually and whenever the Fund's dealing terms, strategy or regulatory environment change materially. Risk retains the daily liquidity model output, stress test results, breach notifications and exception approvals in accordance with the Manager's record retention schedule and makes them available to the board and the auditors on request.

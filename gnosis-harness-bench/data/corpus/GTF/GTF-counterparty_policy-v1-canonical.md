# Midland Global Technology Fund: Counterparty and Broker Policy

Version 1, effective 2025-01-01

## Section 1. Purpose, scope and definitions

1.1. This Counterparty and Broker Policy (the "Policy") governs the selection of, and the Fund's exposure to, the banks, brokers and other counterparties with which the Midland Global Technology Fund (the "Fund") deals. It is adopted by the board of the Fund's management company (the "Manager") and binds the portfolio manager, the trading desk, Operations and every delegate acting for the Fund.

1.2. The Policy applies to cash deposits, securities lending and repurchase transactions, trade execution and any other arrangement under which the Fund is exposed to the credit or performance of another party. Derivatives counterparties are addressed in the Derivatives Usage Policy, which applies in addition to this Policy.

1.3. In this Policy "net asset value" means the value most recently determined by the administrator; "annual traded value" means the aggregate value of purchases and sales executed for the Fund over the annual period used for best execution reporting; "affiliated entity" means any entity that controls, is controlled by or is under common control with the Manager; and "rating" means the long-term credit rating assigned by a recognised rating agency, applying the lowest rating where agencies differ.

## Section 2. Counterparty approval

2.1. No counterparty may be used for any purpose until it has been approved by Risk following a review of its financial standing, regulatory status, operational capability and legal documentation. Risk maintains the approved counterparty list and reviews each entry at least annually.

2.2. The trading desk may execute only with executing brokers on the approved list, and Operations may place cash only with banks on the approved list.

## Section 3. Binding limits and prohibitions

3.1. Securities lending and repo. Exposure to any single securities lending counterparty, and likewise to any single repo counterparty, shall not exceed 15% of net asset value. Exposure is measured as the value of securities or cash delivered to the counterparty less the value of collateral received and held.

3.2. Affiliated entities. No transaction may be entered into with an affiliated entity of the Manager; this conflict-of-interest prohibition cannot be overridden by any individual or committee. No exception process exists in respect of this clause, and neither the portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer, Compliance, Risk nor the board may authorise a departure from it.

3.3. Deposit banks. Cash deposits may only be placed with banks rated A- or better. A deposit bank whose rating falls below A- shall receive no new deposits, and existing deposits shall be withdrawn at the earliest date permitted by their terms.

3.4. Executing brokers. Any single executing broker shall not account for more than 25% of the Fund's annual traded value. The trading desk monitors the running concentration of traded value by broker and reports it to Risk and Compliance on a regular basis.

## Section 4. Operating context

4.1. The statement in this Section is descriptive. It creates no limit, imposes no obligation and is not monitored as a compliance test.

4.2. Securities lending exposure to the largest counterparty, being the largest of the any single securities lending counterparty exposures at the most recent measurement, is currently about 7.5% of net asset value.

## Section 5. Monitoring, breaches and exceptions

5.1. Risk shall test the limits in Section 3 on every dealing day against the administrator's closing valuation and the counterparty exposure report. The order management and collateral systems shall be configured so that a transaction that would cause the securities lending limit or the deposit bank requirement to be breached is blocked, and so that a transaction that would take an executing broker above the traded value threshold is flagged to the head of trading and Compliance for review before execution.

5.2. Where a limit is exceeded otherwise than through a transaction, for example through a change in collateral value or a rating downgrade, the portfolio manager and Operations shall notify Risk and Compliance without delay, agree a remediation plan and restore compliance as soon as is consistent with the interests of shareholders.

5.3. Exceptions.
(a) The portfolio manager may request a temporary exception to the securities lending limit, the deposit bank rating requirement or the executing broker limit by submitting a written rationale. No exception may be requested or granted in respect of the affiliated entity prohibition.
(b) An exception requires the approval of the portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer and Risk. No person may approve an exception that they themselves requested.
(c) An exception shall be granted for a defined period not exceeding 30 days and shall lapse automatically at the end of that period unless renewed through the same process.

## Section 6. Review and records

6.1. The board shall review this Policy annually and whenever there is a material change in the Fund's counterparties, its trading arrangements or the regulatory environment.

6.2. Risk shall retain the approved counterparty list as it stood on each dealing day, exposure reports, breach notifications and exception approvals in accordance with the Manager's record retention schedule and shall make them available to the board and the auditors on request.

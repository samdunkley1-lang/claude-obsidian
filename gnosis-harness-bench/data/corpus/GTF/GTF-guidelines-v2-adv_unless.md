# Midland Global Technology Fund: Investment Guidelines and Restrictions

Version 2, effective 2026-04-01

These Investment Guidelines and Restrictions (the "Guidelines") are issued by the board of the management company (the "Manager") of the Midland Global Technology Fund (the "Fund") as a supplement to the Investment Policy Statement. They gather together the concentration limits and the absolute restrictions that shape the portfolio, they bind the portfolio manager and every member of the investment team, and they reach every asset held by or on behalf of the Fund. Where another policy of the Manager touches the same subject, the more restrictive text applies.

In these Guidelines net asset value is the value most recently determined by the administrator, the benchmark is the reference index named in the prospectus, sector membership follows the classification of the benchmark provider, and domicile follows the Manager's country classification standard. Exposure is always measured after giving effect to the trade under consideration, and exposure obtained through derivatives is counted on a delta-adjusted basis.

## Single security and single issuer

The central discipline is the size of any one holding. No single security shall exceed 7% of net asset value. Compliance tests this limit on every dealing day, and the order management system is configured to stop any purchase that would carry a holding beyond it. The qualification that applies to large benchmark constituents is described later in these Guidelines, under the heading dealing with index constituents.

Concentration is also controlled at the level of the issuer. Aggregate exposure to any single issuer, including all securities of that issuer, shall not exceed 10% of net asset value. Different share classes, depositary receipts and convertible instruments of the same issuer are aggregated for this purpose, together with any derivative exposure referable to that issuer.

By way of context rather than rule, the Manager's typical initial position size is approximately 3.5% of net asset value. That is a description of the Manager's habit when a new holding is opened; it is not monitored by Compliance and it neither caps nor requires any particular position size.

## Sector and country

The portfolio will always be concentrated in a handful of industry groups, and the board has set outer bounds on the largest of them. Exposure to the Software sector shall not exceed 40% of net asset value, and exposure to the Hardware sector shall not exceed 25% of net asset value.

Geographic concentration is bounded for the markets in which the Fund's holdings are most exposed to policy risk. Exposure to issuers domiciled in Taiwan shall not exceed 15% of net asset value, and exposure to issuers domiciled in China shall not exceed 10% of net asset value. An issuer listed elsewhere but domiciled in either country counts against the relevant limit.

## Large index constituents

The single security limit stated earlier in these Guidelines is subject to the following qualification: provided that a security represents more than 3% of the benchmark, the limit that applies to that security is not the headline figure but the benchmark weight plus 2 percentage points. The qualification reflects the index weights of the largest benchmark constituents. The benchmark weight used is the weight published by the index provider at the most recent rebalancing, and the resulting limit is recalculated whenever a new rebalancing is published. Nothing in this paragraph relaxes the issuer limit, which continues to apply to every issuer whatever its benchmark weight.

## The Private and pre-IPO sleeve

Unlisted securities of companies expected to seek a public listing are held through a designated Private and pre-IPO sleeve. Within the Private and pre-IPO sleeve, no single security shall exceed 5% of net asset value. Holdings in the sleeve remain subject to the single security and issuer limits and to the Liquidity Risk Management Policy.

## The Restricted List

The Firm maintains a Restricted List of issuers about which the Firm or its affiliates hold material non-public information or face a conflict of interest. No security of an issuer appearing on the Firm's Restricted List may be purchased or added to under any circumstances; this restriction cannot be waived by the Manager, the Chief Investment Officer or Compliance, and no other person or committee has authority to override it. Existing holdings of an issuer added to the Restricted List are retained or sold only as the Firm directs.

## Monitoring, breaches and exceptions

Where a limit is exceeded passively, through market movement, flows or corporate actions, the portfolio manager must report the position to Compliance without delay, agree a remediation plan and restore compliance as soon as the interests of shareholders allow.

Other than in respect of the Restricted List, the portfolio manager may request a temporary exception to any limit by submitting a written rationale. Approval requires the portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer and Compliance acting together, and no person may approve a request that they themselves submitted. An exception runs for a defined period not exceeding 30 days and lapses automatically at the end of that period unless renewed through the same process.

The board reviews these Guidelines annually and whenever the Fund's strategy, benchmark or regulatory environment changes materially. Compliance retains monitoring output, breach reports and exception approvals in accordance with the Manager's record retention schedule and makes them available to the board and to the auditors on request.

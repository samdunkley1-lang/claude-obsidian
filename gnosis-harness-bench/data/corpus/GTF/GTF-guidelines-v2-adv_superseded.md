# Midland Global Technology Fund: Investment Guidelines and Restrictions

Version 2, effective 2026-04-01

Consolidated text incorporating the amendment effective 2026-04-01. Provisions of the previous version that have been replaced are reproduced immediately after the provisions that replace them and are marked as superseded. Only the current provisions are binding.

## Section 1. Preliminary

1.1. These Investment Guidelines and Restrictions (the "Guidelines") are issued by the board of the management company (the "Manager") of the Midland Global Technology Fund (the "Fund") and supplement the Investment Policy Statement.

1.2. The Guidelines bind the portfolio manager and the investment team and apply to every asset held by or on behalf of the Fund. Where another policy of the Manager addresses the same subject, the more restrictive provision prevails.

1.3. In these Guidelines "net asset value" means the value most recently determined by the administrator; "benchmark" means the reference index named in the prospectus; "benchmark weight" means a security's weight in the benchmark at the most recent published rebalancing; sector membership follows the benchmark provider's classification; and domicile follows the Manager's country classification standard. Exposure is measured after giving effect to the proposed trade, with derivative exposure counted on a delta-adjusted basis.

## Section 2. Concentration limits

2.1. Single security (current). No single security shall exceed 7% of net asset value, unless the security represents more than 3% of the benchmark, in which case the limit is the benchmark weight plus 2 percentage points.

2.2. Single security (superseded with effect from 2026-04-01). The former text of the preceding clause, retained for reference only and no longer binding, read: "No single security shall exceed 8% of net asset value, unless the security represents more than 3% of the benchmark, in which case the limit is the benchmark weight plus 2 percentage points."

2.3. Single issuer. Aggregate exposure to any single issuer, including all securities of that issuer, shall not exceed 10% of net asset value.

2.4. Software. Exposure to the Software sector shall not exceed 40% of net asset value.

2.5. Hardware. Exposure to the Hardware sector shall not exceed 25% of net asset value.

2.6. Taiwan. Exposure to issuers domiciled in Taiwan shall not exceed 15% of net asset value.

2.7. China. Exposure to issuers domiciled in China shall not exceed 10% of net asset value.

2.8. Private and pre-IPO sleeve. Within the Private and pre-IPO sleeve, no single security shall exceed 5% of net asset value. Holdings in the sleeve remain subject to the single security and issuer limits and to the Liquidity Risk Management Policy.

## Section 3. Absolute restriction

3.1. No security of an issuer appearing on the Firm's Restricted List may be purchased or added to under any circumstances; this restriction cannot be waived by the Manager, the Chief Investment Officer or Compliance, and no other person or committee has authority to override it.

3.2. Existing holdings of an issuer added to the Restricted List may be retained or disposed of only as the Firm directs.

## Section 4. Operating context

4.1. The statements in this Section are descriptive only. They are not limits, impose no obligation and are not tested by Compliance.

4.2. Typical position size (current). The Manager's typical initial position size is approximately 3.5% of net asset value.

4.3. Typical position size (superseded with effect from 2026-04-01). The former statement, retained for reference only, read: "The Manager's typical initial position size is approximately 4% of net asset value."

## Section 5. Monitoring, breaches and exceptions

5.1. Compliance shall test each current limit in Section 2 on every dealing day against the administrator's closing valuation, and the order management system shall block any order that would cause a current limit in Section 2 to be exceeded or the restriction in Section 3 to be breached.

5.2. Where a limit is exceeded otherwise than through a trade, the portfolio manager shall report the position to Compliance without delay, agree a remediation plan and restore compliance as soon as the interests of shareholders permit.

5.3. The portfolio manager may request a temporary exception to any current limit in Section 2 by submitting a written rationale. No exception may be requested or granted in respect of Section 3.

5.4. An exception requires the approval of the portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer and Compliance. No person may approve an exception that they themselves requested.

5.5. An exception shall be granted for a defined period not exceeding 30 days and shall lapse automatically at the end of that period unless renewed through the same process.

## Section 6. Review and records

6.1. The board shall review these Guidelines annually and whenever a material change occurs in the Fund's strategy, benchmark or regulatory environment.

6.2. Compliance shall retain monitoring output, breach reports and exception approvals in accordance with the Manager's record retention schedule and shall make them available to the board and the auditors on request.

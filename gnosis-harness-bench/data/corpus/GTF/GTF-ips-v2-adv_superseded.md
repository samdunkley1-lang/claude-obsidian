# Midland Global Technology Fund: Investment Policy Statement

Version 2, effective 2026-04-01

Consolidated text incorporating the amendment effective 2026-04-01. Provisions of the previous version that have been replaced are retained below for reference, immediately after the provision that replaces them, and are marked as superseded. Only the current provisions are binding.

## Section 1. Introductory provisions

1.1. This Investment Policy Statement (the "Statement") governs the management of the Midland Global Technology Fund (the "Fund") and has been adopted by the board of the Fund's management company (the "Manager").

1.2. The Statement binds the portfolio manager, the investment team and every delegate acting for the Fund, and applies to all assets held by or on behalf of the Fund.

1.3. Where a more detailed policy of the Manager addresses the same subject matter, the documents shall be read together and the more restrictive provision shall prevail.

1.4. In this Statement "net asset value" means the value most recently determined by the administrator; "benchmark" means the reference index named in the prospectus; "cash equivalents" means deposits and money market instruments classified as cash under the Manager's standard; "distinct positions" means holdings in securities of different issuers; and "average net assets" means the average net asset value over the annual period used for turnover reporting.

## Section 2. Objective and permitted investments

2.1. The Fund seeks long-term capital growth through a diversified portfolio of listed equities of companies whose businesses depend principally on technology.

2.2. The Fund may invest in listed equities and equity-related securities, cash and cash equivalents, and derivatives used for hedging or efficient portfolio management in accordance with the Derivatives Usage Policy.

## Section 3. Portfolio limits

3.1. Cash floor. The Fund shall hold cash and cash equivalents of not less than 1% of net asset value.

3.2. Cash ceiling (current). Cash and cash equivalents shall not exceed 12% of net asset value.

3.3. Cash ceiling (superseded with effect from 2026-04-01). The former text of the preceding clause, retained for reference only and no longer binding, read: "Cash and cash equivalents shall not exceed 10% of net asset value."

3.4. Diversification. The Fund shall hold a minimum of 40 distinct positions.

3.5. Turnover. Annual portfolio turnover shall not exceed 150% of average net assets.

3.6. Tracking error. Ex-ante tracking error against the benchmark shall not exceed 800 basis points.

3.7. Sector concentration. Exposure to the Semiconductors sector shall not exceed 35% of net asset value.

3.8. Country concentration. Exposure to issuers domiciled in United States shall not exceed 75% of net asset value.

## Section 4. Operating context

4.1. The statements in this Section describe the Manager's expectations and the Fund's experience. They are not limits, impose no obligation and are not tested by Compliance.

4.2. In normal market conditions the Manager expects to hold cash of around 3% of net asset value.

4.3. Historically the Fund's turnover has averaged approximately 90% per annum.

## Section 5. Monitoring and breaches

5.1. Compliance shall test every current limit in Section 3 on each dealing day against the administrator's closing valuation.

5.2. Pre-trade controls shall block any order that would cause the cash floor, the current cash ceiling, the tracking error limit, the Semiconductors limit or the United States limit to be exceeded, and shall flag for review by the portfolio manager and the Chief Investment Officer any order that would take the Fund below the minimum number of distinct positions or above the turnover ceiling.

5.3. A passive breach arising from market movement, flows or corporate actions shall be reported to Compliance without delay together with a remediation plan, and shall be corrected as soon as is consistent with the interests of shareholders.

## Section 6. Exceptions

6.1. The portfolio manager may request a temporary exception to any current limit in Section 3 by submitting a written rationale.

6.2. An exception to the cash floor, the cash ceiling, the diversification requirement, the turnover ceiling, the Semiconductors limit or the United States limit requires the approval of the portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer and Compliance.

6.3. An exception to the tracking error limit requires the approval of the portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer and Risk.

6.4. No person may approve an exception that they themselves requested.

6.5. An exception shall be granted for a defined period not exceeding 30 days and shall lapse automatically at the end of that period unless renewed through the same process.

## Section 7. Review and records

7.1. The board shall review this Statement annually and whenever a material change occurs in the Fund's strategy, benchmark or regulatory environment.

7.2. Compliance shall retain monitoring output, breach reports and exception approvals in accordance with the Manager's record retention schedule and shall make them available to the board and the auditors on request.

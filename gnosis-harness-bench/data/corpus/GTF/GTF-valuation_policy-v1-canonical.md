# Midland Global Technology Fund: Valuation and Pricing Policy

Version 1, effective 2025-01-01

**To:** Portfolio Manager, Midland Global Technology Fund; Fund Administrator; Operations; Risk; Compliance
**From:** Chair of the Valuation Committee, on behalf of the Board of the Manager
**Date:** 2025-01-01
**Subject:** Valuation and Pricing Policy for the Fund: pricing sources, lot conventions, Level 3 assets and stale prices

### 1. Purpose and scope

This memorandum sets out the policy under which the assets of the Midland Global Technology Fund (the "Fund") are valued for the purpose of calculating net asset value. Its aim is that every holding is carried at a fair value determined consistently, independently of the investment team, and in a manner that treats subscribing, redeeming and continuing shareholders even-handedly. The policy binds the administrator, the portfolio manager, Operations and every other person involved in producing or approving a price, and applies to all assets held by or on behalf of the Fund.

### 2. Governance

The Valuation Committee is a standing committee of the Manager composed of representatives of Risk, Finance, Compliance and Operations. It owns this policy, approves the hierarchy of pricing sources, resolves pricing disputes and approves the marks described below. Members of the investment team may attend to present information but do not vote. The portfolio manager may not determine, adjust or approve a price for any asset of the Fund.

### 3. Pricing hierarchy

Listed securities are valued at the closing or last traded price on their principal exchange as supplied by the primary vendor. Where the primary vendor has no price, the administrator applies the secondary vendor and then a broker quotation, in that order, and records the source used. Unlisted securities and other assets without an observable market price are valued using a model or a recent transaction price that the Valuation Committee has approved.

### 4. Binding requirements

The following requirements are mandatory and are enforced by the administrator's pricing controls.

Odd lots. Positions held in odd lots shall be valued using odd-lot prices; institutional round-lot prices shall not be applied to odd-lot positions. A position is an odd lot where its size is below the standard institutional trading unit for the security, as determined by the Manager's market conventions reference.

Level 3 assets. Any mark applied to a Level 3 asset requires prior approval of the Valuation Committee. A Level 3 asset is one whose fair value rests on significant unobservable inputs. The administrator may not release a net asset value that incorporates a new or changed Level 3 mark until the Committee's approval has been recorded.

Stale prices. A price that has not been updated for more than 5 business days is deemed stale and must be referred to the Valuation Committee before use. The administrator's controls identify any stale price on each valuation day, and a stale price may be carried into the net asset value only where the Committee has considered it and either confirmed it or directed an alternative.

### 5. Context

For information, and without creating any obligation, the Committee notes that its review frequency for pricing sources is approximately every 90 days; in other words, the Valuation Committee reviews its pricing sources approximately every 90 days. The review frequency is a matter of practice rather than rule and may be varied by the Committee as circumstances require.

### 6. Pricing errors and exceptions

Where a price is found to have been applied in a manner inconsistent with section 4, the administrator notifies the Valuation Committee and Compliance without delay, the affected net asset values are recalculated, and any material error is handled under the Manager's error correction procedure.

A departure from a requirement in section 4 may be sought only by written request from the portfolio manager setting out the reason, and requires the approval of both the portfolio manager and the Valuation Committee. The person who submits the request may not also approve it on behalf of the Committee. Any departure is recorded in the Committee's minutes together with its rationale and the period for which it applies.

### 7. Review and records

The board reviews this policy annually and whenever the Fund's asset mix, its pricing vendors or the regulatory environment change materially. The administrator retains the daily pricing files, source records, stale price reports and Committee approvals in accordance with the Manager's record retention schedule and makes them available to the board and the auditors on request.

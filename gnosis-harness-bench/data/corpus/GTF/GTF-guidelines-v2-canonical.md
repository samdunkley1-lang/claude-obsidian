# Midland Global Technology Fund: Investment Guidelines and Restrictions

Version 2, effective 2026-04-01

## Preamble

These Investment Guidelines and Restrictions (the "Guidelines") are issued by the board of the management company (the "Manager") of the Midland Global Technology Fund (the "Fund"). They supplement the Investment Policy Statement and consolidate the concentration limits and absolute restrictions that govern portfolio construction. This version replaces the previous version in full; the amendment effective on the date above lowers the limit on a single security, and all other provisions are carried forward unchanged.

The Guidelines bind the portfolio manager and the investment team and apply to every asset held by or on behalf of the Fund. Where the Investment Policy Statement or another policy of the Manager covers the same ground, the more restrictive provision prevails. Net asset value means the value most recently determined by the administrator; the benchmark is the reference index named in the prospectus; sector membership follows the benchmark provider's classification; domicile follows the Manager's country classification standard; and a security's benchmark weight is its weight at the most recent published rebalancing.

## Table 1. Concentration limits

Each limit in the table below is binding. Exposure is measured after giving effect to the proposed trade, and exposure obtained through derivatives is counted at its delta-adjusted value. Compliance tests every limit on each dealing day and the order management system blocks any order that would cause a limit to be exceeded.

| Ref | Subject | Limit | Carve-out or note |
| - | - | - | - |
| A | Any single security | Shall not exceed 7% of net asset value | Unless the security represents more than 3% of the benchmark, in which case the limit is the benchmark weight plus 2 percentage points |
| B | Any single issuer, aggregating all securities of that issuer | Shall not exceed 10% of net asset value | Share classes, depositary receipts and convertibles of the same issuer are aggregated |
| C | Software sector | Shall not exceed 40% of net asset value | Per benchmark provider classification |
| D | Hardware sector | Shall not exceed 25% of net asset value | Per benchmark provider classification |
| E | Issuers domiciled in Taiwan | Shall not exceed 15% of net asset value | Listing venue is irrelevant; domicile governs |
| F | Issuers domiciled in China | Shall not exceed 10% of net asset value | Listing venue is irrelevant; domicile governs |
| G | Any single security within the Private and pre-IPO sleeve | Shall not exceed 5% of net asset value | Sleeve holdings also count towards limits A and B |

## Table 2. Absolute restrictions

The restriction in this table is absolute. It admits no exception and cannot be waived by the Manager, the Chief Investment Officer or Compliance, nor overridden by any other person or committee.

| Ref | Restriction | Status |
| - | - | - |
| H | No security of an issuer appearing on the Firm's Restricted List may be purchased or added to under any circumstances | Absolute; not capable of waiver by the Manager, the Chief Investment Officer or Compliance |

Existing holdings of an issuer that is added to the Restricted List may be retained or disposed of only in accordance with the Firm's instructions.

## Breaches and exceptions

A passive breach, arising from market movement, subscriptions, redemptions or corporate actions, must be reported to Compliance without delay together with a remediation plan and corrected as soon as is consistent with the interests of shareholders.

For any limit in Table 1 the portfolio manager may request a temporary exception by submitting a written rationale. The approvers are the portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer and Compliance acting together; no person may approve a request that they themselves submitted. An exception is granted for a defined period not exceeding 30 days and lapses automatically at the end of that period unless renewed through the same process. No exception process exists for the restriction in Table 2.

## Review and records

The board reviews these Guidelines annually and whenever the Fund's strategy, benchmark or regulatory environment changes materially. Compliance retains all monitoring output, breach reports and exception approvals in accordance with the Manager's record retention schedule and makes them available to the board and the auditors on request.

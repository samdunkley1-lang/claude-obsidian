# Midland Global Technology Fund: Corporate Actions Election Policy

Version 1, effective 2025-01-01

**To:** Portfolio Manager, Midland Global Technology Fund; Head of Operations; Compliance
**From:** Chief Operating Officer, on behalf of the Board of the Manager
**Date:** 2025-01-01
**Subject:** Corporate Actions Election Policy: standing defaults, instruction timing and approval requirements

### Purpose

This memorandum sets out how the Midland Global Technology Fund (the "Fund") responds to corporate actions that call for an election. The intention is that every election is made consistently, is documented, and reaches the custodian in good time. The policy binds the portfolio manager, Operations and any delegate involved in the processing of corporate actions. The standing defaults, the instruction deadline and the duration of any exception are not stated in the body of this memorandum; they are collected in Schedule A at the end, which forms part of the policy and has the same binding force as the text.

### Scope

The policy applies to every voluntary corporate action notified by the custodian, including scrip dividend alternatives, rights issues, tender offers and comparable events. Mandatory events that carry no election are processed by the custodian without reference to this memorandum. The expression "single security limit" carries the meaning given in the Investment Guidelines and Restrictions.

### Roles

Operations receives each notification, records the event and its custodian deadline in the corporate actions log, and instructs the custodian. The portfolio manager decides whether to depart from the default set out in Schedule A for the event type concerned and, where a departure is made, records the rationale. Compliance reviews the log and every documented departure.

### Elections

Scrip dividend. A scrip dividend alternative shall be dealt with in accordance with the default set out in Schedule A. An election for the scrip alternative requires a documented rationale from the portfolio manager and is prohibited where the post-election position would exceed the single security limit in the Investment Guidelines. Before instructing a scrip election, Operations shall confirm with Compliance that the resulting position would remain within that limit.

Rights issue. A rights issue shall be dealt with in accordance with the default set out in Schedule A. A decision to let rights lapse, or to sell nil-paid rights, requires a documented rationale from the portfolio manager.

Tender offer. A tender offer shall be dealt with in accordance with the default set out in Schedule A. Any departure from that default, that is, a decision to tender, requires the approval of the Chief Investment Officer, and Operations shall file that approval before instructing.

Election deadline. Every election shall be instructed to the custodian no later than the deadline in Schedule A, measured against the custodian's own deadline. Operations derives an internal instruction date from the deadline in Schedule A when the event is logged.

### Historical note

For information only, and without qualifying any default in Schedule A, Operations notes that in recent years the majority of scrip alternatives offered to the Fund have been taken in cash. This is a record of past practice and is not a rule or a test.

### Missed deadlines and breaches

If an instruction has not been sent by the internal instruction date, Operations escalates to the portfolio manager and Compliance immediately and instructs the custodian at the earliest opportunity. If an election departs from the default set out in Schedule A without the required rationale or approval, or a scrip election leaves a position above the single security limit, the portfolio manager notifies Compliance without delay and agrees a remediation plan.

### Exceptions

The portfolio manager may request a temporary exception to any default in Schedule A or to the deadline in Schedule A by submitting a written rationale. Approval requires the portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer and Compliance, and no person may approve a request that they themselves submitted. Every exception is granted for a defined period not exceeding the maximum duration in Schedule A and lapses automatically at the end of that period unless renewed through the same process.

### Review and records

The board reviews this policy annually and whenever custodian arrangements or the regulatory environment change materially. Operations retains the corporate actions log, rationales, approvals and custodian confirmations in accordance with the Manager's record retention schedule and makes them available to Compliance, the board and the auditors on request.

### Schedule A: Standing defaults, deadline and exception duration

| Item | Subject | Default, deadline or duration |
| - | - | - |
| A1 | Scrip dividend | Take the cash alternative (cash is the default) |
| A2 | Rights issue | Take up the rights (take-up is the default) |
| A3 | Tender offer | Decline the offer (decline is the default) |
| A4 | Election deadline | Instruct the custodian no later than 2 business days before the custodian deadline |
| A5 | Exception duration | A temporary exception runs for a defined period not exceeding 30 days |

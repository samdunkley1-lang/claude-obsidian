# Midland Global Technology Fund: Investment Policy Statement

Version 2, effective 2026-04-01

## Purpose and status

This Investment Policy Statement governs the management of the Midland Global Technology Fund (the "Fund"). It was adopted by the board of the Fund's management company (the "Manager") and replaces the previous version in full. The amendment effective on the date above revises the ceiling on cash and cash equivalents; all other limits are carried forward unchanged.

- The Statement binds the portfolio manager, the investment team and every delegate acting for the Fund.
- It applies to all assets held by or on behalf of the Fund.
- Where a more detailed policy of the Manager covers the same subject, the more restrictive provision applies.

## Definitions

- "Net asset value" is the value most recently determined by the administrator.
- "Benchmark" is the reference index named in the prospectus.
- "Cash equivalents" are deposits and money market instruments classified as cash under the Manager's standard.
- "Distinct positions" are holdings in securities of different issuers.
- "Average net assets" is the average net asset value over the annual period used for turnover reporting.

## Investment objective and universe

- The Fund aims to deliver long-term capital growth through a diversified portfolio of listed equities of companies whose businesses depend principally on technology.
- Permitted investments are listed equities and equity-related securities, cash and cash equivalents, and derivatives used for hedging or efficient portfolio management in accordance with the Derivatives Usage Policy.

## Binding portfolio limits

The following limits are mandatory and are tested by Compliance on every dealing day.

- Cash floor: the Fund shall hold cash and cash equivalents of not less than 1% of net asset value.
- Cash ceiling: cash and cash equivalents shall not exceed 12% of net asset value.
- Diversification: the Fund shall hold a minimum of 40 distinct positions.
- Turnover: annual portfolio turnover shall not exceed 150% of average net assets.
- Tracking error: ex-ante tracking error against the benchmark shall not exceed 800 basis points.
- Sector concentration: exposure to the Semiconductors sector shall not exceed 35% of net asset value.
- Country concentration: exposure to issuers domiciled in United States shall not exceed 75% of net asset value.

## Operating context (not limits)

The points below describe how the Fund has been run in practice. They are informational, create no obligation and are not monitored as compliance tests.

- In normal market conditions the Manager expects to hold cash of around 3% of net asset value.
- Historically the Fund's turnover has averaged approximately 90% per annum.

## Monitoring and breach handling

- Pre-trade controls block any order that would cause the cash floor, the cash ceiling, the tracking error limit, the Semiconductors limit or the United States limit to be exceeded.
- An order that would take the Fund below the minimum number of distinct positions, or above the turnover ceiling, is flagged for review by the portfolio manager and the Chief Investment Officer before release.
- A passive breach arising from market movement, flows or corporate actions must be reported to Compliance without delay, together with a remediation plan, and corrected as soon as is consistent with shareholders' interests.

## Exceptions

- The portfolio manager may request a temporary exception to any binding limit by submitting a written rationale.
- Exceptions to the cash, diversification, turnover, sector and country limits require approval by the portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer and Compliance.
- Exceptions to the tracking error limit require approval by the portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer and Risk.
- No person may approve an exception that they requested.
- Every exception is granted for a defined period of no more than 30 days and lapses automatically unless renewed through the same process.

## Governance and records

- The board reviews this Statement annually and on any material change to the Fund's strategy, benchmark or regulatory environment.
- Compliance retains monitoring output, breach reports and exception approvals in accordance with the Manager's record retention schedule and makes them available to the board and the auditors on request.

# Midland Global Technology Fund: Corporate Actions Election Policy

Version 1, effective 2025-01-01

## Purpose and scope

This Policy governs how the Midland Global Technology Fund (the "Fund") responds to corporate actions that require an election, so that decisions are taken consistently, on a documented basis and in time to be acted upon by the custodian. It is adopted by the board of the Fund's management company (the "Manager") and binds the portfolio manager, Operations and every delegate involved in processing corporate actions.

- The Policy applies to every voluntary corporate action notified to the Fund by the custodian, including scrip dividend alternatives, rights issues, tender offers, exchange offers and similar events.
- Mandatory events with no election are processed by the custodian without reference to this Policy.
- Terms such as "single security limit" have the meaning given in the Investment Guidelines and Restrictions.

## Roles

- Operations receives corporate action notifications from the custodian, records each event and its deadline in the corporate actions log, and instructs the custodian.
- The portfolio manager decides whether to depart from a default election and documents the rationale.
- Compliance reviews the corporate actions log and the rationale for every departure from a default.

## Default elections

The defaults below are binding. Operations shall apply each default unless a departure has been documented and, where required, approved in accordance with this Policy.

- Scrip dividend: scrip dividend alternatives shall be taken in cash by default. A scrip election requires a documented rationale from the portfolio manager, and is prohibited where the post-election position would exceed the single security limit in the Investment Guidelines.
- Rights issue: rights issues shall be taken up by default. A decision to let rights lapse or to sell nil-paid rights requires a documented rationale from the portfolio manager.
- Tender offer: tender offers shall be declined by default. Tendering requires approval of the Chief Investment Officer.
- Election deadline: elections shall be instructed to the custodian no later than 2 business days before the custodian deadline.

## Historical note (not a rule)

- In recent years the majority of scrip alternatives offered to the Fund have been taken in cash. This observation describes past practice; it neither adds to nor qualifies the defaults above and is not monitored as a compliance test.

## Process

- On receipt of a notification, Operations enters the event in the log with the custodian deadline and the internal instruction date derived from the election deadline rule.
- Operations circulates the event to the portfolio manager, who either confirms the default or records a documented rationale for a departure.
- Where the departure is a tender, Operations obtains and files the approval of the Chief Investment Officer before instructing.
- Where the departure is a scrip election, Operations confirms with Compliance that the post-election position would remain within the single security limit before instructing.
- Operations instructs the custodian and records the instruction time in the log.

## Missed deadlines and breaches

- If an instruction is not sent by the internal instruction date, Operations escalates to the portfolio manager and Compliance immediately and instructs the custodian at the earliest opportunity.
- If an election is made contrary to a default without the required rationale or approval, or a scrip election results in a position above the single security limit, the portfolio manager notifies Compliance without delay and agrees a remediation plan.

## Exceptions

- The portfolio manager may request a temporary exception to any default or to the election deadline rule by submitting a written rationale.
- Approval requires the portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer and Compliance.
- No person may approve an exception that they themselves requested.
- An exception is granted for a defined period not exceeding 30 days and lapses automatically at the end of that period unless renewed through the same process.

## Review and records

- The board reviews this Policy annually and whenever the Fund's custodian arrangements or the regulatory environment change materially.
- Operations retains the corporate actions log, rationales, approvals and custodian confirmations in accordance with the Manager's record retention schedule and makes them available to Compliance, the board and the auditors on request.

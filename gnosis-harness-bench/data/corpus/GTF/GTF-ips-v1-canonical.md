# Midland Global Technology Fund: Investment Policy Statement

Version 1, effective 2025-01-01

## Part A. Preliminary

1. Purpose. This Investment Policy Statement (the "Statement") sets out the investment objective, the permitted universe and the portfolio limits that govern the management of the Midland Global Technology Fund (the "Fund"). It has been adopted by the board of the Fund's management company (the "Manager") and binds the portfolio manager, the wider investment team and every delegate acting on the Fund's behalf.

2. Scope. The Statement applies to every asset held by or for the account of the Fund. Where a separate policy of the Manager deals with a subject in greater detail, that policy shall be read together with this Statement; in the event of any inconsistency the more restrictive provision prevails.

3. Definitions. In this Statement:
(a) "net asset value" means the net asset value of the Fund as most recently determined by the administrator;
(b) "benchmark" means the reference index named in the Fund's prospectus;
(c) "cash equivalents" means deposits and money market instruments classified as cash under the Manager's classification standard;
(d) "distinct positions" means holdings in the securities of different issuers; and
(e) "average net assets" means the average of the Fund's net asset value across the annual measurement period used for turnover reporting.

## Part B. Objective and permitted investments

4. Objective. The Fund seeks long-term capital growth from a diversified portfolio of listed equities issued by companies whose products, services or business models depend principally on technology.

5. Permitted investments. The Fund may invest in listed equities and equity-related securities, in cash and cash equivalents, and, subject to the Derivatives Usage Policy, in exchange-traded and over-the-counter derivatives used for hedging or efficient portfolio management.

## Part C. Portfolio limits

6. Cash floor. The Fund shall hold cash and cash equivalents of not less than 1% of net asset value.

7. Cash ceiling. Cash and cash equivalents shall not exceed 10% of net asset value.

8. Diversification. The Fund shall hold a minimum of 40 distinct positions.

9. Turnover. Annual portfolio turnover shall not exceed 150% of average net assets.

10. Tracking error. Ex-ante tracking error against the benchmark shall not exceed 800 basis points.

11. Sector concentration. Exposure to the Semiconductors sector shall not exceed 35% of net asset value.

12. Country concentration. Exposure to issuers domiciled in United States shall not exceed 75% of net asset value.

## Part D. Operating context

13. Status of this Part. The statements in this Part record the Manager's expectations and the Fund's experience. They are given for context only, create no limit, and are not tested by Compliance.

14. Cash in practice. In normal market conditions the Manager expects to hold cash of around 3% of net asset value, which sits comfortably inside the range fixed by clauses 6 and 7.

15. Turnover in practice. Historically the Fund's turnover has averaged approximately 90% per annum, well within the ceiling in clause 9.

## Part E. Monitoring, breaches and exceptions

16. Monitoring. Compliance shall test each limit in Part C on every dealing day against the administrator's closing valuation. Pre-trade controls shall be configured so that an order which would cause the limits in clauses 6, 7, 10, 11 or 12 to be exceeded is blocked before release to the market, and so that an order which would cause the limits in clauses 8 or 9 to be exceeded is flagged to the portfolio manager and the Chief Investment Officer for review before release.

17. Passive breaches. Where a limit is exceeded through market movement, subscriptions, redemptions or corporate actions rather than through a trade, the portfolio manager shall bring the Fund back within the limit as soon as is consistent with the interests of shareholders, and shall report the position and the remediation plan to Compliance without delay.

18. Exceptions.
(a) The portfolio manager may request a temporary exception to any limit in Part C by submitting a written rationale.
(b) An exception to clause 6, 7, 8, 9, 11 or 12 requires the approval of the portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer and Compliance. An exception to clause 10 requires the approval of the portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer and Risk.
(c) No person may approve an exception that they themselves requested.
(d) An exception shall be granted for a defined period not exceeding 30 days and shall lapse automatically at the end of that period unless renewed through the same process.

19. Review. The board shall review this Statement annually and whenever a material change occurs in the Fund's strategy, its benchmark or the regulatory environment.

20. Records. Compliance shall retain all monitoring output, breach reports and exception approvals for the period required by the Manager's record retention schedule and shall make them available to the board and to the auditors on request.

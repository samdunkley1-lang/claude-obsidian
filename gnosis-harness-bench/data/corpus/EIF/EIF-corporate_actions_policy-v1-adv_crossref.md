# Midland European Income Fund: Corporate Actions Election Policy
Version 1, effective 2025-01-01

**To:** Portfolio Management, Corporate Actions Team, Compliance, Custodian Relationship Management
**From:** Head of Operations
**Date:** 2025-01-01
**Subject:** Corporate Actions Election Policy, version 1, with Schedule A of defaults and deadline

This memorandum sets out the Corporate Actions Election Policy of the Midland European Income Fund (the "Fund") as approved by the board of the Manager. The body of the memorandum describes the process and the responsibilities; the default election for each event type and the instruction deadline are consolidated in Schedule A at the end, so that they can be amended without reissuing the whole Policy. Where the body refers to "the default set out in Schedule A" or "the deadline in Schedule A", the Schedule governs.

**Purpose**

The Policy ensures that elections on scrip dividend alternatives, rights issues and tender offers are made consistently, documented, instructed to the custodian in good time, and kept consistent with the Fund's income objective and with the concentration limits in the Investment Guidelines and Restrictions.

**Responsibilities**

The corporate actions team receives event notifications from the custodian, logs each event, identifies the event type and prepares a recommendation for the portfolio manager. The portfolio manager makes the election, applying the default set out in Schedule A unless the conditions for departing from it, described below, are met. Compliance reviews elections on a sample basis.

**Scrip dividend alternatives**

Each scrip dividend alternative shall be elected in accordance with the default set out in Schedule A. A scrip election, being an election to receive shares rather than the default, requires a documented rationale from the portfolio manager explaining why receiving shares serves unitholders on that occasion. A scrip election is prohibited where the post-election position would exceed the single security limit in the Investment Guidelines; the corporate actions team must test the post-election position against that limit, as it stands in the Investment Guidelines from time to time, before instructing the election.

**Rights issues**

Each rights issue shall be elected in accordance with the default set out in Schedule A. A decision to let rights lapse or to sell nil-paid rights requires a documented rationale from the portfolio manager, recorded against the event. Where the default would cause a limit in the Investment Guidelines to be exceeded, the portfolio manager must consider a partial election or a sale of the excess and document the reasoning.

**Tender offers**

Each tender offer shall be elected in accordance with the default set out in Schedule A. Departing from that default by tendering requires approval of the Chief Investment Officer, and the corporate actions team shall not instruct a tender without evidence of that approval on file.

**Election deadline**

Elections shall be instructed to the custodian no later than the deadline in Schedule A, which is expressed as a margin ahead of the custodian's own deadline. An instruction sent inside that margin is a warning condition: it is reported to Operations management and reviewed, though the election remains valid if the custodian accepts it. Where the custodian deadline has not been published, the team applies the margin to the issuer's deadline as notified.

**Historical practice**

For context, the corporate actions team keeps statistics on how elections have actually been made. A summary of recent practice for scrip dividend alternatives appears as a note beneath Schedule A. That note is informational and does not alter any default.

**Exceptions**

A departure from a default set out in Schedule A, or from the deadline in Schedule A, other than through the documented rationale or approval that the relevant section provides for, may be authorised only on a four-eyes basis by the portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer and Compliance. Each exception must be recorded with its rationale and may not remain in force for longer than 30 days.

**Breaches**

An election made contrary to the default set out in Schedule A without the required documentation or approval, an election that breaches a limit in the Investment Guidelines, or an instruction that misses the deadline in Schedule A is reported to Compliance immediately, recorded in the breach register and escalated to the Chief Investment Officer.

**Records and review**

Notifications, recommendations, rationales, approvals and custodian confirmations are retained in the corporate actions log under the Firm's record-keeping procedures. The board reviews this Policy, including Schedule A, annually and whenever custodian arrangements change.

## Schedule A: Defaults and deadline

| Event type | Default election | Departure requires |
|-|-|-|
| Scrip dividend | Cash; the scrip alternative is not taken by default | Documented rationale from the portfolio manager; prohibited where the single security limit in the Investment Guidelines would be exceeded |
| Rights issue | Take up the rights | Documented rationale from the portfolio manager to let rights lapse or to sell nil-paid rights |
| Tender offer | Decline the offer | Approval of the Chief Investment Officer to tender |

| Requirement | Deadline |
|-|-|
| Election deadline: instruction to the custodian | No later than 2 business days before the custodian deadline |

Note for information: in recent years the majority of scrip dividend alternatives offered to the Fund have been taken in cash. This note records history and does not set or alter any default.

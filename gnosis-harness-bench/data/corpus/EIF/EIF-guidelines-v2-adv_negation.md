# Midland European Income Fund: Investment Guidelines and Restrictions
Version 2, effective 2026-04-01

## 1. Application

1.1. These Guidelines apply to the Midland European Income Fund (the "Fund") and bind the Manager, the portfolio manager and all persons dealing on the Fund's behalf.

1.2. References to net asset value are to the net asset value of the Fund as most recently struck. Every limit is tested as if the proposed transaction had been completed, and an order that would result in a limit being exceeded is not to be executed.

1.3. Nothing in these Guidelines shall be read as permitting what the Investment Policy Statement prohibits, and nothing in the Investment Policy Statement shall be read as relaxing any restriction set out here.

## 2. Single security

2.1. It is not the case that any single security may exceed 4.5% of net asset value; the Fund shall in no case be permitted to hold a single security at a weight above that figure, unless the security represents more than 3% of the benchmark, in which case it is not the standard limit that applies but the benchmark weight plus 1.5 percentage points, and the holding shall not under any circumstances be permitted to exceed that alternative figure.

2.2. No order shall be released, nor shall any allocation be accepted, if its effect would be that the limit in this clause is no longer complied with.

## 3. Single issuer

3.1. Aggregate exposure to any single issuer, including all securities of that issuer, shall in no case be permitted to exceed 5.5% of net asset value, and it is not open to the portfolio manager to treat separate lines of the same issuer as if they were not to be aggregated.

3.2. No purchase of any security shall be made where, after the purchase, the aggregate exposure to that security's issuer would not be within the limit in this clause.

## 4. Sectors

4.1. Exposure to the Utilities sector shall at no time be permitted to exceed 20% of net asset value; no trade may be executed whose consequence would be that the Utilities sector weight is not at or below that figure.

4.2. Nor may exposure to the Real Estate sector exceed 15% of net asset value; it is not permitted for the Real Estate sector weight to be anything other than at or below that figure once a proposed order has been taken into account.

4.3. Sector classification is not a matter for the portfolio manager's discretion: it follows the benchmark provider's methodology without variation.

## 5. Countries

5.1. Exposure to issuers domiciled in Germany shall in no case be permitted to exceed 25% of net asset value, nor shall any order be executed whose effect would be that this limit is not observed.

5.2. Exposure to issuers domiciled in France may not exceed 25% of net asset value; it is not the case that any circumstance, other than an exception granted under clause 8, allows this figure to be exceeded.

5.3. Domicile is determined under the Manager's country classification framework and not otherwise.

## 6. Small-cap sleeve

6.1. Within the Small-cap sleeve, no single security shall exceed 3% of net asset value. This limit applies in addition to the Fund-level single security limit in clause 2, and a holding in the sleeve must satisfy both.

## 7. Restricted List

7.1. No security of an issuer appearing on the Firm's Restricted List may be purchased or added to under any circumstances.

7.2. This restriction cannot be waived by the Manager, the Chief Investment Officer or Compliance, and clause 8 does not apply to it. Any existing holding in an issuer added to the Restricted List is frozen pending instruction from Compliance.

## 8. Exceptions

8.1. Other than in respect of clause 7, an exception to a limit in these Guidelines may be granted only where the portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer and Compliance approve it on a four-eyes basis.

8.2. An exception shall be recorded in writing with its rationale and shall not remain in force for longer than 30 days.

## 9. Ordinary practice

9.1. For the information of readers, and without creating any limit, the Manager's typical initial position size is approximately 2.25% of net asset value. Departing from that practice is not a breach of these Guidelines.

## 10. Breaches

10.1. Any breach shall be reported to Compliance immediately upon identification and entered in the breach register.

10.2. Remediation of a passive breach shall be carried out in an orderly manner having regard to unitholders' interests; remediation of an active breach shall be carried out as soon as practicable.

10.3. Compliance shall report breaches and open exceptions to the board at each scheduled meeting.

## 11. Review

11.1. These Guidelines are reviewed annually by the board and whenever the Investment Policy Statement is amended. Amendments take effect on the date stated at the head of this document.

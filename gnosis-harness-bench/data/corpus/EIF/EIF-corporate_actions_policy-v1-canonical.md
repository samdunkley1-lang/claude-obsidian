# Midland European Income Fund: Corporate Actions Election Policy
Version 1, effective 2025-01-01

## Purpose

This Policy governs the way in which the Midland European Income Fund (the "Fund") responds to corporate actions that require an election, including scrip dividend alternatives, rights issues and tender offers. Its purpose is to ensure that elections are made consistently, are documented, are instructed to the custodian in good time and remain consistent with the Fund's income objective and with the concentration limits in the Investment Guidelines and Restrictions. The Policy is approved by the board of the Manager, owned by Operations and applied by the portfolio manager, the corporate actions team and the custodian.

## Responsibilities

The corporate actions team receives event notifications from the custodian, records each event in the corporate actions log, and prepares an election recommendation for the portfolio manager. The portfolio manager decides the election within the defaults set by this Policy. Compliance reviews elections on a sample basis and investigates any that depart from the defaults without the required documentation.

## Scrip dividend alternatives

Scrip dividend alternatives shall be taken in cash by default. The Fund's objective is to deliver income, and cash receipt supports that objective without altering position sizes. A scrip election requires a documented rationale from the portfolio manager, setting out why receiving shares is in unitholders' interests on the occasion in question. A scrip election is prohibited where the post-election position would exceed the single security limit in the Investment Guidelines, and the corporate actions team must test the proposed post-election position against that limit before the election is instructed.

By way of background, in recent years the majority of scrip dividend alternatives offered to the Fund have been taken in cash. That observation describes past practice and is not itself a rule; the binding requirements are the default and the documentation requirement stated above.

## Rights issues

Rights issues shall be taken up by default. Taking up rights preserves the Fund's proportionate interest in the issuer and avoids dilution. A decision to let rights lapse or to sell nil-paid rights requires a documented rationale from the portfolio manager, which the corporate actions team records against the event. Where taking up rights would cause a concentration limit in the Investment Guidelines to be exceeded, the portfolio manager must consider a partial take-up or a sale of the excess rights and document the reasoning.

## Tender offers

Tender offers shall be declined by default. The Fund does not ordinarily tender into offers, since the decision to exit a holding is an investment decision that the portfolio manager makes through the ordinary dealing process. Tendering requires approval of the Chief Investment Officer, and the corporate actions team shall not instruct a tender election to the custodian without evidence of that approval on file.

## Election deadline

Elections shall be instructed to the custodian no later than 2 business days before the custodian deadline. This margin allows the custodian to process the instruction and gives the corporate actions team time to correct any rejected instruction. An instruction sent inside the margin is a warning condition: it is reported to Operations management and reviewed, but the election itself remains valid if the custodian accepts it. Where the custodian deadline is not yet published, the team works from the issuer's deadline as notified.

## Exceptions

A departure from any default or requirement in this Policy, other than through the documented rationale or approval that the relevant section itself provides for, may be authorised only on a four-eyes basis by the portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer and Compliance. Each such exception must be recorded with its rationale and may not remain in force for longer than 30 days.

## Breaches

An election made contrary to a default without the required documentation or approval, an election that causes a limit in the Investment Guidelines to be breached, or a missed election deadline is reported to Compliance immediately, recorded in the breach register and escalated to the Chief Investment Officer. Where a missed election results in a default outcome imposed by the issuer or custodian, the corporate actions team records the outcome and any financial consequence for the Fund.

## Records and review

Event notifications, election recommendations, rationales, approvals and custodian confirmations are retained in the corporate actions log in accordance with the Firm's record-keeping procedures. This Policy is reviewed annually by the board and whenever custodian arrangements change.

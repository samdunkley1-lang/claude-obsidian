# Midland European Income Fund: Responsible Investment Exclusions Policy
Version 1, effective 2025-01-01

## 1. Scope and definitions

1.1. This Policy applies to all investments made for the Midland European Income Fund (the "Fund") and binds the Manager, the portfolio manager and every person placing orders for the Fund.

1.2. "Excluded issuer" means an issuer falling within any of clauses 2 to 5. "Research providers" means the external data vendors appointed by the Manager for responsible investment screening. "Exclusion list" means the list of excluded issuers maintained by the responsible investment team and loaded into the dealing system.

1.3. Screening decisions are made by the responsible investment team on the basis of research provider data and internal analysis. An issuer whose status is in dispute is treated as excluded until the dispute is resolved.

## 2. Controversial weapons

2.1. The Fund shall not hold, and shall not purchase, any security of an issuer involved in the production of controversial weapons. For this purpose controversial weapons comprise cluster munitions, anti-personnel mines, and biological and chemical weapons.

2.2. This exclusion is absolute. No exception may be granted by any person, and no committee, officer or function of the Manager, including the board, the Chief Investment Officer and Compliance, has authority to waive or suspend it.

2.3. An issuer that becomes involved in such production while held shall be sold, and no order to purchase such an issuer shall be accepted in any circumstances.

## 3. Thermal coal

3.1. The Fund shall not hold any security of an issuer deriving more than 20% of revenue from thermal coal extraction or thermal coal power generation.

3.2. Revenue shares are determined from the most recent audited accounts as reported by the research providers. Where reported segments do not isolate thermal coal, the responsible investment team shall make and document a reasoned estimate.

## 4. UN Global Compact

4.1. The Fund shall not hold any security of an issuer that is among the UN Global Compact violators, being an issuer assessed by the research providers, and confirmed by the responsible investment team, as being in breach of the UN Global Compact principles.

4.2. Placement on a watch list for a suspected breach does not, by itself, trigger exclusion, but it obliges the analysts to engage with the issuer and to report to the responsible investment team.

## 5. Tobacco

5.1. The Fund shall not hold any security of a tobacco producer. A tobacco producer is an issuer that manufactures tobacco products.

5.2. Retailers, distributors and suppliers to the tobacco industry are not excluded under this clause; they are addressed through the Manager's engagement framework.

## 6. Carbon intensity aspiration

6.1. The Manager aims to reduce the portfolio's weighted average carbon intensity by 30% relative to the benchmark over time.

6.2. Clause 6 states an aspiration and not a rule. It creates no limit, is not tested in the dealing system, and the carbon intensity of the portfolio on any date does not give rise to a breach of this Policy. Progress is reported annually to unitholders.

## 7. Exceptions

7.1. An exception to clause 3, clause 4 or clause 5 may be granted only on a four-eyes basis by the portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer and Compliance, and only where a documented rationale shows that the issuer's classification is doubtful or is expected to change imminently.

7.2. No exception may remain in force for longer than 30 days.

7.3. No exception of any kind is available in respect of clause 2.

## 8. Enforcement

8.1. The exclusion list shall be refreshed on each research provider update and reconciled against the portfolio daily.

8.2. Any purchase of an excluded issuer, and any holding retained after the issuer's addition to the exclusion list without a valid exception under clause 7, is a breach. Breaches shall be reported to Compliance immediately, recorded in the breach register and escalated to the Chief Investment Officer.

8.3. Excluded holdings shall be disposed of as soon as practicable, in an orderly manner and with regard to the interests of unitholders.

## 9. Review

9.1. The board shall review this Policy annually and whenever the Manager's firm-wide responsible investment commitments are amended.

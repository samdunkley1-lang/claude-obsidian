# Midland European Income Fund: Investment Policy Statement
Version 1, effective 2025-01-01

## Purpose and scope

This Investment Policy Statement records the objectives, permitted investments and portfolio limits that govern the management of the Midland European Income Fund (the "Fund"). It is adopted by the board of the Manager and binds the portfolio management team, the Risk function and Compliance. Where this Statement and any other internal document differ, this Statement prevails for the matters it addresses, while the Investment Guidelines and Restrictions govern the issuer, sector and country matters not dealt with here.

## Objective and philosophy

The Fund seeks to deliver an attractive and growing income, together with capital appreciation over the long term, by investing principally in the listed equity of European companies with durable cash flows and disciplined capital allocation. The portfolio is constructed from the bottom up, with a bias towards businesses that can sustain dividend growth through the cycle. Performance is measured against the Fund's designated European equity income benchmark, which is also the reference point for the risk limits described below.

## Cash and portfolio construction

The Fund shall hold cash and cash equivalents of not less than 0% of net asset value, and cash and cash equivalents shall not exceed 5% of net asset value. The upper bound is a firm operating limit: a transaction that would leave cash above it may not be executed until the portfolio manager has brought the balance back within range. In normal market conditions the Manager expects to hold cash of around 3% of net asset value, though this is an expectation about how the strategy is typically run rather than a limit, and actual balances move with subscriptions, redemptions and dividend receipts.

Diversification is central to the income mandate. The Fund shall hold a minimum of 50 distinct positions. Should the count fall below that floor, whether through disposals or corporate events, the portfolio manager must notify Risk and set out a plan to restore it; the shortfall is treated as a warning rather than an automatic block on further trading.

Annual portfolio turnover shall not exceed 60% of average net assets. Turnover above this level is flagged for review by the Chief Investment Officer, who will consider whether the level of trading remains consistent with the Fund's long-term income orientation. Historically the Fund's turnover has averaged approximately 36% per annum, which reflects the patient nature of the strategy rather than any additional restriction.

## Risk relative to benchmark

Active risk is managed within a defined budget. Ex-ante tracking error against the benchmark shall not exceed 400 basis points, as measured by the Manager's risk model at each portfolio review. Where tracking error approaches the ceiling, Risk will raise the matter with the portfolio manager before it is breached, and once the ceiling is exceeded any trade that would increase tracking error further is blocked.

## Sector and country concentration

Exposure to the Financials sector shall not exceed 25% of net asset value. Exposure to issuers domiciled in the United Kingdom shall not exceed 40% of net asset value. Both limits are measured on a post-trade basis, so a proposed purchase is tested as though it had already settled. Other sector and country limits are set out in the Investment Guidelines and Restrictions and apply alongside those stated here.

## Currency

The Fund's base currency is the reference for all limits in this Statement. Unhedged non-base-currency exposure shall not exceed 20% of net asset value. The portfolio manager may use currency forwards to bring unhedged exposure within this limit, subject to the Derivatives Usage Policy.

## Exceptions

A temporary exception to any of the limits above may be approved, on a four-eyes basis, by the portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer and Compliance, save that exceptions to the tracking error limit and the currency limit are approved by the portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer and Risk. Each exception must be documented in writing with its rationale and expiry, and no exception may remain in force for longer than 30 days. On expiry the portfolio must be back within the relevant limit or a fresh exception must be sought.

## Breaches and reporting

Any breach of a limit, whether active or passive, shall be reported to Compliance without delay, recorded in the breach register and escalated to the Chief Investment Officer. Passive breaches arising from market movement are to be corrected in an orderly manner having regard to the interests of unitholders. Compliance reports all breaches and open exceptions to the board at each scheduled meeting.

## Review

This Statement is reviewed by the board annually and whenever a material change to the Fund's mandate, benchmark or regulatory environment occurs. Amendments take effect from the date shown at the head of the document.

# Midland European Income Fund: Investment Guidelines and Restrictions
Version 1, effective 2025-01-01

## Preamble

These Investment Guidelines and Restrictions (the "Guidelines") supplement the Investment Policy Statement of the Midland European Income Fund (the "Fund") and set the issuer, sector, country and sleeve limits within which the portfolio manager must operate. They are approved by the board of the Manager and administered by Compliance, with pre-trade checks coded into the order management system wherever a limit can be measured automatically.

All limits are expressed as a percentage of the Fund's net asset value and are tested on a post-trade basis: a proposed order is assessed as if it had already been filled. Where a limit is described as blocking, an order that would breach it may not be released to market. Each table below states the rule, the scope to which it applies and the effect of a breach.

## Table 1: Security and issuer limits

| Rule | Scope | Limit | Effect of breach |
|-|-|-|-|
| Single security maximum | Any single security held by the Fund | Shall not exceed 5% of net asset value | Order blocked |
| Single security carve-out | A security representing more than 3% of the benchmark | The limit is the benchmark weight plus 1.5 percentage points, in place of the standard maximum | Order blocked |
| Single issuer maximum | Aggregate exposure to any single issuer, including all securities of that issuer | Shall not exceed 6% of net asset value | Order blocked |
| Small-cap sleeve security maximum | Any single security within the Small-cap sleeve | Shall not exceed 3% of net asset value | Order blocked |

The carve-out in the second row operates as follows: no single security shall exceed 5% of net asset value, unless the security represents more than 3% of the benchmark, in which case the limit is the benchmark weight plus 1.5 percentage points. The alternative limit is recalculated whenever benchmark weights are refreshed. The Small-cap sleeve limit applies in addition to the Fund-level single security maximum, so a holding in that sleeve must satisfy both.

## Table 2: Sector limits

| Sector | Limit | Effect of breach |
|-|-|-|
| Utilities | Exposure to the Utilities sector shall not exceed 20% of net asset value | Order blocked |
| Real Estate | Exposure to the Real Estate sector shall not exceed 15% of net asset value | Order blocked |

Sector classification follows the benchmark provider's methodology. Where an issuer is reclassified by the provider, the reclassification is applied to the Fund's monitoring at the next valuation point and any resulting passive breach is handled under the breach procedure below.

## Table 3: Country limits

| Country of domicile | Limit | Effect of breach |
|-|-|-|
| Germany | Exposure to issuers domiciled in Germany shall not exceed 25% of net asset value | Order blocked |
| France | Exposure to issuers domiciled in France shall not exceed 25% of net asset value | Order blocked |

Domicile is determined by the issuer's country of incorporation unless the Manager's country classification framework assigns a different country of risk, in which case that assignment applies.

## Table 4: Absolute restriction

| Restriction | Scope | Effect |
|-|-|-|
| Restricted List | Any security of an issuer appearing on the Firm's Restricted List | Purchase or addition prohibited under any circumstances; no waiver possible |

No security of an issuer appearing on the Firm's Restricted List may be purchased or added to under any circumstances. This restriction cannot be waived by the Manager, the Chief Investment Officer or Compliance, and no exception process applies to it. Existing positions in an issuer that is added to the Restricted List are frozen pending Compliance instruction.

## Position sizing practice

For context rather than as a rule, the Manager's typical initial position size is approximately 2.5% of net asset value. Initial sizes vary with conviction and liquidity, and nothing in this paragraph creates a floor or a ceiling; the binding limits are those in the tables above.

## Exceptions

Save for the Restricted List restriction, a temporary exception to any limit in these Guidelines may be granted on a four-eyes basis by the portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer and Compliance. The exception must be documented with its rationale and expiry, and may not remain in force for longer than 30 days.

## Breach procedure

An active breach, arising from a trade, must be reported to Compliance immediately and corrected as soon as practicable. A passive breach, arising from market movement, corporate action or benchmark change, must be reported to Compliance on identification and corrected in an orderly manner consistent with the interests of unitholders. Compliance maintains the breach register and reports to the board at each scheduled meeting.

## Review

These Guidelines are reviewed annually by the board and whenever the Investment Policy Statement is amended. The effective date of the current version appears at the head of this document.

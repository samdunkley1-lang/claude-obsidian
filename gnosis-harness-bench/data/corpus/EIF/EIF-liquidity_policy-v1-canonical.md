# Midland European Income Fund: Liquidity Risk Management Policy
Version 1, effective 2025-01-01

## Preamble

This Policy sets out how the Midland European Income Fund (the "Fund") manages the liquidity of its portfolio so that redemptions can be met in an orderly way without disadvantaging remaining unitholders. It is approved by the board of the Manager, owned by Risk and applied by the portfolio manager, the dealing desk and the fund administrator. The Policy operates alongside the Investment Policy Statement and the Valuation and Pricing Policy.

Liquidity is assessed at the level of individual positions and at the level of the whole portfolio. Position-level liquidity is estimated from observed trading volumes; portfolio-level liquidity aggregates the time needed to realise each holding into buckets. Each limit below is expressed against net asset value unless stated otherwise, is tested on a post-trade basis, and carries the consequence shown in the table. Where the consequence is a warning, the condition is escalated but does not by itself block dealing.

## Table 1: Portfolio liquidity limits

| Rule | Measure | Limit | Consequence |
|-|-|-|-|
| Illiquid holdings | Holdings classified as illiquid under the Manager's liquidity framework | Shall not exceed 5% of net asset value | Order blocked |
| Realisable portion | Share of net asset value realisable within 5 business days under normal market conditions | At least 90% of net asset value shall be realisable within that period | Order blocked |
| Days to liquidate, single position | Business days required to liquidate a single position at 25% of average daily volume | No single position shall require more than 5 business days to liquidate | Warning to Risk |

## Table 2: Dealing and pricing

| Rule | Trigger | Requirement | Consequence |
|-|-|-|-|
| Swing pricing | Net flows on a dealing day exceed 1.5% of net asset value | Swing pricing shall be applied to that dealing day's price | Failure to apply is a breach |

Swing pricing adjusts the dealing price so that the estimated cost of trading to meet net flows is borne by the transacting unitholders rather than by the Fund. Net flows are net subscriptions or net redemptions for the dealing day. The swing factor is determined under the Manager's liquidity framework and reviewed periodically; the trigger in the table is the only condition on which the adjustment is mandatory.

## Classification of illiquid holdings

A holding is classified as illiquid where, on the Manager's liquidity framework, it could not be realised at or near its carrying value within the normal settlement cycle for its market, or where quoted volumes are too thin to support a reliable estimate. Classification is reviewed by Risk at each portfolio review and whenever market conditions change materially. For information, at the last review illiquid holdings represented approximately 1.5% of net asset value; that figure is a snapshot of the portfolio at that date and not a limit.

## Stress testing

Risk runs liquidity stress tests that combine assumed redemption scenarios with reduced market volumes, and reports the results to the portfolio manager and the board. The results inform the classification of holdings and the swing factor but do not alter the limits in the tables above.

## Exceptions

A temporary exception to any limit in Table 1 or Table 2 may be granted only on a four-eyes basis by the portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer and Risk. Each exception must be documented with its rationale and expiry and may not remain in force for longer than 30 days.

## Breach procedure

A breach of a blocking limit is reported to Compliance immediately, recorded in the breach register and escalated to the Chief Investment Officer, and the portfolio is brought back within the limit in an orderly manner. A warning condition is reported to Risk, which agrees a plan with the portfolio manager and monitors it until the condition clears. Compliance reports breaches, warnings and open exceptions to the board at each scheduled meeting.

## Review

This Policy is reviewed annually by the board and whenever the Fund's dealing arrangements or investor base change materially.

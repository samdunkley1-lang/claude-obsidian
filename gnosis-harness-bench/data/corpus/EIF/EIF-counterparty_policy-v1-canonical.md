# Midland European Income Fund: Counterparty and Broker Policy
Version 1, effective 2025-01-01

## Purpose

- This Policy governs the selection of, and exposure to, the counterparties and brokers used by the Midland European Income Fund (the "Fund") for securities lending, repurchase transactions, cash deposits and trade execution.
- It is approved by the board of the Manager, owned by Risk, and applied by the dealing desk, the portfolio manager and the fund administrator.
- It operates alongside the Derivatives Usage Policy, which governs derivatives counterparties separately, and the Investment Policy Statement.

## Counterparty approval

- Every counterparty and broker must be approved by Risk and added to the approved list before any transaction is placed with it.
- Approval requires satisfactory due diligence on financial strength, regulatory standing, operational capability and legal documentation.
- Risk reviews the approved list periodically and removes any counterparty whose standing has deteriorated.

## Securities lending and repo exposure

- Exposure to any single securities lending counterparty, and to any single repo counterparty, shall not exceed 10% of net asset value.
- Exposure is measured as the market value of securities lent or cash advanced, less collateral held under an enforceable arrangement.
- An instruction that would take exposure above the limit is blocked.
- For information only, and not as a limit: securities lending exposure to the largest counterparty is currently about 5% of net asset value. Exposure to any single securities lending counterparty is therefore well inside the limit at present, but this figure describes the position at the date of this Policy and will change as the lending programme evolves.

## Affiliated entities

- No transaction may be entered into with an affiliated entity, meaning any entity affiliated with the Manager, in any capacity, whether as lending counterparty, repo counterparty, deposit bank or executing broker.
- This conflict-of-interest prohibition is absolute. It cannot be overridden by any individual or committee, including the board, the Chief Investment Officer, Risk and Compliance, and the exception process in this Policy does not apply to it.
- Risk maintains the list of affiliated entities and ensures that each is blocked in the dealing and settlement systems.

## Cash deposits

- Cash deposits may only be placed with a deposit bank rated A- or better.
- The rating is taken from a recognised rating agency at the time the deposit is placed. Where a deposit bank is downgraded below the required level, no new deposits may be placed with it and maturing balances are to be withdrawn.
- A deposit that would be placed with a bank below the required rating is blocked.

## Execution concentration

- No single executing broker shall account for more than 25% of the Fund's annual traded value. The share attributable to any single executing broker is measured over the Fund's financial year on a rolling basis.
- Exceeding this figure is a warning condition rather than a block: the dealing desk notifies Risk and agrees a plan to broaden execution across the approved broker panel.
- Best execution obligations continue to apply and are not displaced by this limit.

## Exceptions

- Other than in respect of affiliated entities, a temporary exception to any limit in this Policy may be granted only on a four-eyes basis by the portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer and Risk.
- Every exception must be documented in writing with its rationale and expiry.
- No exception may remain in force for longer than 30 days.

## Breaches

- Any breach is reported to Compliance immediately, recorded in the breach register and escalated to the Chief Investment Officer.
- Remediation of an exposure breach takes place as soon as practicable, having regard to the cost of unwinding positions and the interests of unitholders.
- Risk reports counterparty exposures, breaches and open exceptions to the board at each scheduled meeting.

## Records and review

- Due diligence files, approvals, exposure reports and exception records are retained in accordance with the Firm's record-keeping procedures.
- The board reviews this Policy annually and whenever the Fund's counterparty arrangements change materially.

# Midland European Income Fund: Investment Policy Statement
Version 1, effective 2025-01-01

## 1. Interpretation

1.1. In this Statement the "Fund" means the Midland European Income Fund, the "Manager" means its appointed investment manager, "net asset value" means the net asset value of the Fund as most recently determined under the Valuation and Pricing Policy, and the "benchmark" means the European equity income index designated by the board for performance and risk measurement.

1.2. Each limit in this Statement is tested on a post-trade basis, meaning that a proposed transaction is assessed as if it had already been executed and settled.

1.3. The word "shall" denotes an obligation. Text describing the Manager's expectations or the Fund's history is informational only and creates no limit.

## 2. Mandate

2.1. The Fund's objective is to provide unitholders with a sustainable and rising income stream, with capital growth as a secondary aim, through investment in listed European equities.

2.2. The Manager shall pursue that objective within the limits set out in clauses 3 to 6 and in the Investment Guidelines and Restrictions.

## 3. Liquidity reserve

3.1. Cash and cash equivalents shall at all times represent not less than 0% of net asset value.

3.2. Cash and cash equivalents shall at no time exceed 5% of net asset value.

3.3. Nothing in this clause fixes a target balance. For information only, the Manager anticipates that, in normal market conditions, cash will sit at around 3% of net asset value; that figure describes ordinary practice and is neither a floor nor a ceiling.

## 4. Breadth and activity

4.1. The number of distinct positions held by the Fund shall be not fewer than 50. A count below this figure constitutes a warning condition requiring notification to Risk; it does not of itself prevent further dealing.

4.2. Portfolio turnover, measured annually as a percentage of average net assets, shall not be greater than 60%. Turnover above this figure constitutes a warning condition to be reviewed by the Chief Investment Officer.

4.3. By way of background, turnover has historically run at an average of approximately 36% a year. That observation is descriptive and imposes no additional constraint on the portfolio manager.

## 5. Active risk

5.1. The Fund's ex-ante tracking error relative to the benchmark, as computed by the Manager's risk system, shall not exceed 400 bps.

5.2. Risk shall notify the portfolio manager when the measured figure approaches the limit, and no transaction that would add to tracking error may be executed while the limit is exceeded.

## 6. Concentration and currency

6.1. The aggregate weight of issuers classified within the Financials sector shall not exceed 25% of net asset value.

6.2. The aggregate weight of issuers domiciled in the United Kingdom shall not exceed 40% of net asset value.

6.3. Exposure to currencies other than the base currency that has not been hedged back to base shall not exceed 20% of net asset value. Currency hedging instruments are subject to the Derivatives Usage Policy.

## 7. Exceptions

7.1. An exception to any limit in clause 3 or clause 4, or to either of the sector and country limits in clause 6, may be granted only by the portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer and Compliance acting on a four-eyes basis.

7.2. An exception to the tracking error limit in clause 5 or to the currency limit in clause 6 may be granted only by the portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer and Risk acting on a four-eyes basis.

7.3. Every exception shall be recorded in writing, shall state its rationale and its expiry date, and shall lapse no later than 30 days after it is granted unless the portfolio is by then within the limit.

## 8. Breach handling

8.1. The portfolio manager shall report any breach of this Statement to Compliance immediately upon becoming aware of it.

8.2. Compliance shall record the breach, notify the Chief Investment Officer and oversee remediation, which shall be carried out having regard to the interests of unitholders and without forced selling where the breach arose passively.

8.3. Compliance shall present a summary of breaches and exceptions to the board at each scheduled board meeting.

## 9. Governance

9.1. This Statement is approved by the board and shall be reviewed at least annually.

9.2. Amendments require board approval and take effect on the effective date recorded at the head of this document.

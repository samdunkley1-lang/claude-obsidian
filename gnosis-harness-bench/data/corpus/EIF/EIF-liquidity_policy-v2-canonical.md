# Midland European Income Fund: Liquidity Risk Management Policy
Version 2, effective 2026-04-01

**To:** Portfolio Management, Dealing, Fund Administration, Compliance
**From:** Head of Risk
**Date:** 2026-04-01
**Subject:** Liquidity Risk Management Policy, version 2, effective 2026-04-01

This memorandum circulates version 2 of the Liquidity Risk Management Policy of the Midland European Income Fund (the "Fund"), as approved by the board. The amendment lowers the net flow trigger for swing pricing following Risk's review of dealing costs over the past year; the portfolio liquidity limits are unchanged. Version 2 replaces version 1 in full from the effective date. Please update dealing and administration procedures accordingly.

**Purpose**

The Policy exists to ensure that the Fund can meet redemptions in an orderly manner without disadvantaging remaining unitholders. It sets a limit on the share of the portfolio that is illiquid, requires that most of the portfolio can be realised quickly, limits the time needed to exit any single position, and mandates swing pricing when flows are large. All limits are expressed against net asset value and tested post-trade.

**Illiquid holdings**

Holdings classified as illiquid shall not exceed 5% of net asset value. A holding is illiquid where, under the Manager's liquidity framework, it could not be realised at or near carrying value within the normal settlement cycle of its market, or where quoted volumes are too thin for a reliable estimate. An order that would take illiquid holdings above the limit is blocked.

**Realisable portion of the portfolio**

At least 90% of net asset value shall be realisable within 5 business days under normal market conditions. Realisability is estimated from observed trading volumes across the portfolio. An order that would reduce the realisable portion below the limit is blocked.

**Days to liquidate**

No single position shall require more than 5 business days to liquidate at 25% of average daily volume. A position that exceeds this figure is a warning condition: Risk is notified, and a plan for the position is agreed with the portfolio manager. Dealing is not blocked by this rule alone.

**Swing pricing**

Swing pricing shall be applied on any dealing day on which net flows exceed 1% of net asset value. Net flows are net subscriptions or net redemptions for the dealing day, measured against net asset value at the prior valuation point. The swing factor is determined under the Manager's liquidity framework and reviewed periodically; the trigger stated here is the only condition on which application of the swing is mandatory. Failure to apply the swing when the trigger is met is a breach.

**Current position, for information**

At the last review illiquid holdings represented approximately 1.5% of net asset value. This is a snapshot at the review date and is neither a limit nor a target.

**Stress testing**

Risk runs liquidity stress tests combining redemption scenarios with reduced market volumes and reports the results to the portfolio manager and the board. Results inform classification and the swing factor; they do not alter the limits above.

**Exceptions**

A temporary exception to any limit in this Policy may be granted only on a four-eyes basis by the portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer and Risk. Exceptions must be documented with their rationale and expiry and may not remain in force for longer than 30 days.

**Breach procedure**

A breach of a blocking limit is reported to Compliance immediately, recorded in the breach register and escalated to the Chief Investment Officer, and the portfolio is brought back within the limit in an orderly manner. A warning condition is reported to Risk, which monitors the agreed plan until the condition clears. Compliance reports breaches, warnings and open exceptions to the board at each scheduled meeting.

**Transition**

The lower swing trigger applies from the first dealing day on or after the effective date. Fund Administration is to confirm to Risk that the dealing system parameter has been changed.

**Review**

The board reviews this Policy annually and whenever the Fund's dealing arrangements or investor base change materially. Queries on this memorandum should be addressed to Risk.

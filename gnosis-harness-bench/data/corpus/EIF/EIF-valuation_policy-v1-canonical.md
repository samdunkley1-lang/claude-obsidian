# Midland European Income Fund: Valuation and Pricing Policy
Version 1, effective 2025-01-01

## 1. Purpose and scope

1.1. This Policy sets out the principles and procedures for valuing the assets of the Midland European Income Fund (the "Fund") for the purpose of calculating net asset value. It applies to the fund administrator, the portfolio manager, Risk and the Valuation Committee.

1.2. The Policy is approved by the board of the Manager. The Valuation Committee is responsible for its application and for resolving valuation questions escalated to it. The Committee comprises representatives of Risk, Compliance, Finance and Operations and is chaired independently of portfolio management.

1.3. Terms used in this Policy have the meaning given in the Investment Policy Statement unless defined here. "Business day" means a day on which the principal market for the asset concerned is open for trading.

## 2. Valuation hierarchy

2.1. Assets shall be valued at fair value using observable market prices from the primary pricing source wherever such prices are available. Where the primary source fails or is unreliable, the secondary source designated by the Valuation Committee shall be used, and the use of the secondary source shall be recorded.

2.2. Where no observable price exists, the asset shall be valued using a model or other valuation technique approved by the Valuation Committee, and the inputs and rationale shall be documented.

## 3. Odd lots

3.1. Positions held in odd lots shall be valued using odd-lot prices; institutional round-lot prices shall not be applied to odd-lot positions.

3.2. An odd lot is a holding smaller than the standard institutional trading size for the market concerned. The fund administrator shall flag odd-lot positions in the valuation file so that the correct pricing basis is applied.

## 4. Level 3 assets

4.1. Any mark applied to a Level 3 asset requires prior approval of the Valuation Committee. A Level 3 asset is one valued using significant unobservable inputs.

4.2. The portfolio manager shall submit the proposed mark, the valuation technique and the supporting inputs to the Valuation Committee, which shall record its decision and the reasons for it. A Level 3 mark applied without prior approval is a breach of this Policy.

## 5. Stale prices

5.1. A stale price, being a price that has not been updated for more than 5 business days, is deemed unreliable and must be referred to the Valuation Committee before use.

5.2. The fund administrator shall identify stale prices as part of the daily valuation control and shall not apply a stale price to the calculation of net asset value until the Valuation Committee has confirmed the price or directed an alternative.

## 6. Review frequency of pricing sources

6.1. For information, the Valuation Committee reviews its pricing sources approximately every 90 days. The review frequency stated here describes the Committee's practice; it is not a rule, and a longer or shorter interval between reviews does not constitute a breach of this Policy.

6.2. The review considers the accuracy of each source, the frequency of failures and the availability of alternatives, and its conclusions are minuted.

## 7. Exceptions

7.1. An exception to clause 3, clause 4 or clause 5 may be granted only where the portfolio manager and the Valuation Committee approve it on a four-eyes basis.

7.2. Every exception shall be documented with its rationale and the valuation basis to be used in its place, and shall be reported to the board at its next scheduled meeting.

## 8. Errors and breaches

8.1. Any departure from this Policy, and any pricing error identified after net asset value has been published, shall be reported to Compliance and the Valuation Committee immediately.

8.2. The Valuation Committee shall determine whether the error is material under the Firm's error correction procedure and shall oversee any restatement or compensation.

8.3. Compliance shall record breaches in the breach register and report them to the board at each scheduled meeting.

## 9. Records and review

9.1. Pricing sources, overrides, Level 3 approvals, stale price referrals and Committee minutes shall be retained in accordance with the Firm's record-keeping procedures.

9.2. This Policy is reviewed by the board annually and whenever the Fund's asset mix or pricing arrangements change materially.

# Midland European Income Fund: Investment Guidelines and Restrictions
Version 2, effective 2026-04-01

## Purpose

These Guidelines set out the concentration restrictions that apply to the Midland European Income Fund (the "Fund"). They are approved by the board of the Manager, administered by Compliance and enforced through the pre-trade controls of the dealing system. They operate alongside the Investment Policy Statement, which governs the Fund's objective, cash range, turnover, active risk and currency exposure, and alongside the Firm's Restricted List procedures.

## Measurement

Each restriction below is expressed against the net asset value of the Fund and is tested on the portfolio as it would stand after the proposed transaction. Where a restriction would be exceeded, the transaction is not permitted to proceed. Weights are refreshed at each valuation point and monitored by Compliance after trading as a further line of control.

## Security concentration

No single security shall exceed 4.5% of net asset value. This is the headline limit on the size of any individual holding, and it is applied uniformly across the portfolio regardless of the liquidity, market capitalisation or benchmark membership of the security concerned, subject only to the qualification set out later in this document under the heading "Qualification to the security limit".

## Issuer concentration

Aggregate exposure to any single issuer, including all securities of that issuer, shall not exceed 5.5% of net asset value. Ordinary shares, preference shares, depositary receipts and any other instrument whose economic return derives from the same issuer are aggregated for this purpose, and the aggregate is tested in the same post-trade manner as the security limit.

## Sector concentration

Exposure to the Utilities sector shall not exceed 20% of net asset value, and exposure to the Real Estate sector shall not exceed 15% of net asset value. Sector classification follows the benchmark provider's methodology, and a reclassification by the provider is applied to the Fund's monitoring at the next valuation point.

## Country concentration

Exposure to issuers domiciled in Germany shall not exceed 25% of net asset value, and exposure to issuers domiciled in France shall not exceed 25% of net asset value. Domicile is assigned under the Manager's country classification framework, which follows the country of incorporation unless a different country of risk has been determined for the issuer.

## Qualification to the security limit

The security concentration restriction described earlier in this document is subject to a single qualification, which the board has adopted so that the Fund is not compelled into a structural underweight in the largest index constituents. That restriction applies save that, where the security represents more than 3% of the benchmark, the limit is the benchmark weight plus 1.5 percentage points rather than the standard figure. The alternative limit is recalculated each time the benchmark constituents are refreshed, and it is applied in the same post-trade manner as the standard limit.

## Small-cap sleeve

Within the Small-cap sleeve, no single security shall exceed 3% of net asset value. This restriction is cumulative with the Fund-level security limit: a holding in the sleeve must satisfy both tests, and the qualification described in the preceding section does not extend to the sleeve limit.

## Restricted List

No security of an issuer appearing on the Firm's Restricted List may be purchased or added to under any circumstances. This restriction cannot be waived by the Manager, the Chief Investment Officer or Compliance, and it stands outside the exception procedure described below. Where an issuer already held is placed on the Restricted List, the holding is frozen until Compliance gives instructions.

## Ordinary practice

For context, the Manager's typical initial position size is approximately 2.25% of net asset value. This describes how new holdings are usually established; it is not a floor, a ceiling or a target, and a larger or smaller initial position is not a breach of these Guidelines.

## Exceptions

Other than in relation to the Restricted List, a temporary exception to any restriction in these Guidelines may be granted where the portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer and Compliance approve it on a four-eyes basis. The approval must be documented with its rationale and expected duration, and no exception may remain in force for longer than 30 days.

## Breaches

Every breach, active or passive, is reported to Compliance as soon as it is identified and recorded in the breach register. Active breaches are corrected as soon as practicable; passive breaches arising from price movements, corporate actions or benchmark changes are corrected in an orderly manner consistent with the interests of unitholders. Compliance reports breaches and open exceptions to the board at each scheduled meeting.

## Review

The board reviews these Guidelines annually and whenever the Investment Policy Statement is amended. The current version takes effect on the date stated beneath the title.

# Midland European Income Fund: Responsible Investment Exclusions Policy
Version 1, effective 2025-01-01

## Purpose

This Policy sets out the categories of issuer that the Midland European Income Fund (the "Fund") will not hold, the basis on which issuers are assessed against those categories, and the way in which the exclusions are enforced. It reflects the Manager's responsible investment commitments and the expectations of the Fund's unitholders. The Policy is approved by the board of the Manager, maintained by the responsible investment team and enforced by Compliance through the exclusion list loaded into the dealing system.

## Assessment

Issuers are screened against each exclusion using data from the Manager's appointed research providers, supplemented by the analysts' own work where provider coverage is incomplete or contested. The responsible investment team reviews the screening output and maintains the exclusion list, which is refreshed whenever the providers publish updates and is checked against the portfolio on a daily basis. Where an issuer's status is disputed, the issuer remains excluded until the dispute is resolved in its favour.

## Controversial weapons

Issuers involved in the production of controversial weapons, being cluster munitions, anti-personnel mines, and biological and chemical weapons, are excluded absolutely from the Fund. This exclusion differs in character from every other provision of this Policy: no exception may be granted by any person, no committee may waive it, and no commercial or performance consideration may be weighed against it. A holding that comes within this exclusion because of a change in the issuer's activities must be disposed of, and no purchase may be made under any circumstances.

## Thermal coal

Issuers deriving more than 20% of revenue from thermal coal extraction or thermal coal power generation are excluded. Revenue is measured on the most recent audited figures available to the research providers, and where an issuer reports segment revenue in a way that does not isolate thermal coal, the responsible investment team makes a reasoned estimate and records it.

## UN Global Compact

UN Global Compact violators are excluded. For this purpose an issuer is a violator where it has been assessed by the Manager's research providers, and confirmed by the responsible investment team, as being in breach of the UN Global Compact principles on human rights, labour, the environment or anti-corruption. An issuer placed on watch for a possible breach is not excluded on that ground alone, but the analysts must engage with it and report to the team.

## Tobacco

Tobacco producers are excluded from the Fund. The exclusion covers issuers that manufacture tobacco products; it does not of itself extend to retailers, distributors or suppliers of packaging, which are assessed under the Manager's wider engagement framework rather than excluded.

## Climate ambition

Separately from the exclusions, the Manager aims to reduce the portfolio's weighted average carbon intensity by 30% relative to the benchmark over time. This is an aspiration that guides engagement and security selection; it is not a limit, no breach arises from the portfolio's carbon intensity at any given date, and it is not enforced through the dealing system. Progress is reported to unitholders in the Fund's annual responsible investment report.

## Exceptions

An exception to the thermal coal, UN Global Compact or tobacco exclusion may be granted only where the portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer and Compliance approve it on a four-eyes basis, and only where a documented rationale demonstrates that the issuer's classification is doubtful or is expected to change imminently. Any such exception may not remain in force for longer than 30 days. The controversial weapons exclusion admits no exception whatsoever.

## Breaches

A purchase of an excluded issuer, or the continued holding of an issuer after it has been added to the exclusion list without a valid exception, is a breach. Breaches are reported to Compliance immediately, recorded in the breach register and escalated to the Chief Investment Officer. Excluded holdings are disposed of in an orderly manner having regard to the interests of unitholders, and the disposal is completed as soon as practicable.

## Review

This Policy is reviewed annually by the board, with input from the responsible investment team, and whenever the Manager's firm-wide responsible investment commitments change.

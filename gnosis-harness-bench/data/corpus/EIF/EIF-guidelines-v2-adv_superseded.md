# Midland European Income Fund: Investment Guidelines and Restrictions
Version 2, effective 2026-04-01

## 1. Status of this consolidated text

1.1. This document consolidates the Investment Guidelines and Restrictions of the Midland European Income Fund (the "Fund") as amended by the board with effect from 2026-04-01. The clauses of version 1 that were altered by the amendment are reproduced in their original form, each marked as superseded, so that readers can see the change. Only the current clauses are binding.

1.2. Every limit is expressed against net asset value and tested on a post-trade basis. An order that would result in a current limit being exceeded may not be executed.

## 2. Single security limit

2.1. Current clause. No single security shall exceed 4.5% of net asset value, unless the security represents more than 3% of the benchmark, in which case the limit is the benchmark weight plus 1.5 percentage points.

2.2. Former clause, superseded with effect from 2026-04-01 and no longer binding: "No single security shall exceed 5% of net asset value, unless the security represents more than 3% of the benchmark, in which case the limit is the benchmark weight plus 1.5 percentage points."

2.3. The amendment changed only the standard figure; the benchmark-related carve-out is unchanged and continues to apply under the current clause.

## 3. Single issuer limit

3.1. Current clause. Aggregate exposure to any single issuer, including all securities of that issuer, shall not exceed 5.5% of net asset value.

3.2. Former clause, superseded with effect from 2026-04-01 and no longer binding: "Aggregate exposure to any single issuer, including all securities of that issuer, shall not exceed 6% of net asset value."

## 4. Sector limits

4.1. Exposure to the Utilities sector shall not exceed 20% of net asset value.

4.2. Exposure to the Real Estate sector shall not exceed 15% of net asset value.

4.3. These clauses were not altered by the amendment. Sector classification follows the benchmark provider's methodology.

## 5. Country limits

5.1. Exposure to issuers domiciled in Germany shall not exceed 25% of net asset value.

5.2. Exposure to issuers domiciled in France shall not exceed 25% of net asset value.

5.3. These clauses were not altered by the amendment. Domicile is determined under the Manager's country classification framework.

## 6. Small-cap sleeve

6.1. Within the Small-cap sleeve, no single security shall exceed 3% of net asset value. This clause was not altered by the amendment and applies in addition to clause 2.

## 7. Restricted List

7.1. No security of an issuer appearing on the Firm's Restricted List may be purchased or added to under any circumstances; this restriction cannot be waived by the Manager, the Chief Investment Officer or Compliance.

7.2. This clause was not altered by the amendment. It is an absolute restriction to which clause 9 does not apply.

## 8. Ordinary practice (informational)

8.1. Current statement. The Manager's typical initial position size is approximately 2.25% of net asset value. This is a description of practice and not a limit.

8.2. Former statement, superseded with effect from 2026-04-01: "The Manager's typical initial position size is approximately 2.5% of net asset value." The former statement was likewise informational and never operated as a limit.

## 9. Exceptions

9.1. Other than in respect of clause 7, a temporary exception to a limit in these Guidelines may be granted on a four-eyes basis by the portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer and Compliance.

9.2. An exception must be documented with its rationale and may not remain in force for longer than 30 days.

## 10. Transition

10.1. Holdings that complied with the superseded clauses but exceed the current clauses on the effective date shall be identified by Risk and reduced in an orderly manner. No purchase that would increase such a holding may be made while it remains above the current limit.

## 11. Breaches

11.1. Any breach shall be reported to Compliance immediately, recorded in the breach register and escalated to the Chief Investment Officer. Passive breaches are remedied in an orderly manner having regard to unitholders' interests; active breaches are remedied as soon as practicable.

11.2. Compliance reports breaches and open exceptions to the board at each scheduled meeting.

## 12. Review

12.1. These Guidelines are reviewed annually by the board and whenever the Investment Policy Statement is amended. Amendments take effect on the date stated at the head of this document.

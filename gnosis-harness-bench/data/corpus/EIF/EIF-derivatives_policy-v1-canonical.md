# Midland European Income Fund: Derivatives Usage Policy
Version 1, effective 2025-01-01

## 1. Purpose and scope

1.1. This Policy governs the use of derivative instruments by the Midland European Income Fund (the "Fund"). It applies to every exchange-traded and over-the-counter derivative, including futures, options, forwards and swaps, entered into for the account of the Fund.

1.2. The Policy is approved by the board of the Manager, administered by Risk and enforced through pre-trade controls and daily post-trade monitoring. It operates alongside the Investment Policy Statement, the Counterparty and Broker Policy and the Liquidity Risk Management Policy.

1.3. Terms defined in the Investment Policy Statement have the same meaning here. "Net asset value" is the net asset value of the Fund as most recently determined.

## 2. Permitted purpose

2.1. Derivatives may be used for hedging purposes only. A transaction qualifies as hedging where it reduces an identified market, currency or interest-rate exposure already present in the portfolio.

2.2. Non-hedging derivatives, meaning derivatives that increase market exposure, are prohibited. Any instrument whose effect would be to add directional exposure, to replicate a long position synthetically or to introduce leverage falls within this prohibition and may not be transacted.

2.3. The portfolio manager shall record, at the time each derivative is entered into, the exposure that it is intended to hedge.

## 3. Exposure limits

3.1. Gross notional exposure through derivatives shall not exceed 10% of net asset value. Gross notional is the sum of the absolute notional values of all open derivative positions, without netting.

3.2. Mark-to-market exposure to any single derivatives counterparty shall not exceed 10% of net asset value. Exposure is measured as the positive replacement value of all open contracts with that counterparty, net of collateral received where a legally enforceable netting and collateral arrangement is in place.

3.3. Uncollateralised derivatives exposure shall not exceed 5% of net asset value. Exposure is uncollateralised to the extent that positive replacement value is not covered by collateral held by the Fund.

3.4. Each limit in this clause is tested pre-trade as if the proposed transaction had been executed, and a transaction that would cause a limit to be exceeded is blocked.

## 4. Counterparty eligibility

4.1. Derivatives may only be transacted with counterparties rated A or better by at least one recognised rating agency. A derivatives counterparty whose rating falls below this level after a transaction has been entered into may not be used for new transactions, and Risk shall determine whether existing contracts are to be novated or closed.

4.2. Every over-the-counter counterparty must have executed the Manager's standard master agreement and collateral documentation before any trade is placed.

## 5. Historical context

5.1. For the information of readers, and without creating any additional limit, over the last three years gross notional derivatives exposure has typically been around 4% of net asset value. The Fund's use of derivatives has been confined largely to currency forwards hedging non-base-currency dividend receipts.

## 6. Exceptions

6.1. A temporary exception to a limit in clause 3 or to the eligibility requirement in clause 4 may be granted only on a four-eyes basis by the portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer and Risk.

6.2. An exception shall be documented in writing, shall state its rationale and its expiry, and may not remain in force for longer than 30 days.

6.3. Clause 2 describes the permitted purpose of derivatives, and an exception to it may be granted only by the same approvers, on the same basis and for no longer than the same period.

## 7. Monitoring and breach procedure

7.1. Risk shall monitor compliance with this Policy daily and shall notify the portfolio manager and Compliance of any breach immediately upon identification.

7.2. A breach shall be entered in the breach register, escalated to the Chief Investment Officer and remedied as soon as practicable, having regard to the cost of closing or novating positions and the interests of unitholders.

7.3. Risk reports derivative exposures, breaches and open exceptions to the board at each scheduled meeting.

## 8. Records and review

8.1. Confirmations, collateral statements and valuation records for every derivative transaction shall be retained in accordance with the Firm's record-keeping procedures.

8.2. This Policy is reviewed by the board annually and whenever the Fund's use of derivatives changes materially.

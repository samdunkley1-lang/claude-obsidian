# Midland European Income Fund: Investment Guidelines and Restrictions
Version 2, effective 2026-04-01

**To:** Portfolio Management, Risk, Compliance, Dealing
**From:** Chief Investment Officer and Head of Compliance
**Date:** 2026-04-01
**Subject:** Investment Guidelines and Restrictions, version 2, with effect from 2026-04-01

This memorandum sets out the Investment Guidelines and Restrictions of the Midland European Income Fund (the "Fund") as amended by the board with effect from the date above. Version 2 replaces version 1 in its entirety. The amendment tightens the single security and single issuer limits following the board's review of concentration risk; all other limits are unchanged in substance. Please ensure that pre-trade controls in the order management system reflect the figures below from the effective date.

**Basis of measurement**

Every limit is expressed as a percentage of the Fund's net asset value and is assessed post-trade, so that a proposed order is tested as if it had already been executed. Unless stated otherwise, an order that would cause a breach is blocked and may not be released to market.

**Single security limit**

No single security shall exceed 4.5% of net asset value, unless the security represents more than 3% of the benchmark, in which case the limit is the benchmark weight plus 1.5 percentage points. The alternative limit is recalculated on each benchmark rebalance.

**Single issuer limit**

Aggregate exposure to any single issuer, including all securities of that issuer, shall not exceed 5.5% of net asset value. Where an issuer has several listed lines or share classes, they are aggregated for this purpose.

**Sector limits**

Exposure to the Utilities sector shall not exceed 20% of net asset value. Exposure to the Real Estate sector shall not exceed 15% of net asset value. Sector classification follows the benchmark provider's methodology.

**Country limits**

Exposure to issuers domiciled in Germany shall not exceed 25% of net asset value. Exposure to issuers domiciled in France shall not exceed 25% of net asset value. Domicile follows the Manager's country classification framework.

**Small-cap sleeve**

Within the Small-cap sleeve, no single security shall exceed 3% of net asset value. This limit applies in addition to, not instead of, the Fund-level single security limit.

**Restricted List**

No security of an issuer appearing on the Firm's Restricted List may be purchased or added to under any circumstances. This restriction cannot be waived by the Manager, the Chief Investment Officer or Compliance, and it is not subject to the exception process described below. Dealing must check the Restricted List before releasing any buy order.

**Position sizing, for information**

The Manager's typical initial position size is approximately 2.25% of net asset value. This figure is a description of ordinary practice for the benefit of Risk and Compliance colleagues; it is not a limit and no breach arises from departing from it.

**Exceptions**

With the exception of the Restricted List restriction, a temporary exception to any limit above may be approved on a four-eyes basis by the portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer and Compliance. The approval must be in writing, must state the rationale and the expected path back within the limit, and may not remain in force for longer than 30 days.

**Breach procedure**

Active breaches must be reported to Compliance immediately and corrected as soon as practicable. Passive breaches arising from market movements, corporate actions or benchmark changes must be reported when identified and corrected in an orderly way having regard to unitholders' interests. Compliance keeps the breach register and reports breaches and open exceptions to the board at each scheduled meeting.

**Transition**

Positions that comply with version 1 but not with version 2 on the effective date are to be identified by Risk and brought within the new limits in an orderly manner; no new purchases that would increase such positions may be made in the interim.

**Review**

These Guidelines are reviewed annually and whenever the Investment Policy Statement changes. Questions about this memorandum should be directed to Compliance.

# Midland Corporate Bond Fund: Investment Guidelines and Restrictions
Version 2, effective 2026-04-01

Consolidated text incorporating the amendment effective 2026-04-01. The amendment revised the aggregate issuer limit. The version 1 wording of that limit is reproduced beside the current clause, marked as superseded, so that the history of the provision can be traced without consulting the earlier document. Only the current clauses bind the Fund.

## 1. Application

1.1. These Investment Guidelines and Restrictions (the "Guidelines") are issued by Midland Asset Management (the "Manager") for the Midland Corporate Bond Fund (the "Fund") and supplement the Investment Policy Statement.

1.2. They bind the portfolio manager, the dealing desk and every person dealing for the Fund. Where a provision here and a provision of the Investment Policy Statement address the same exposure, the tighter prevails.

1.3. Text marked "superseded with effect from 2026-04-01" has no continuing effect. It is retained for reference only, and the operative provision is the clause that stands next to it.

1.4. Percentages are percentages of the Fund's net asset value. Limits are tested after giving effect to the proposed transaction and apply continuously thereafter.

## 2. Single security and issuer limits

2.1. No single security shall exceed 2% of net asset value.

2.2. Aggregate exposure to any single issuer, including all securities of that issuer, shall not exceed 3.5% of net asset value.

2.3. Former clause, superseded with effect from 2026-04-01: "Aggregate exposure to any single issuer, including all securities of that issuer, shall not exceed 3% of net asset value." This wording ceased to apply on that date and is reproduced for reference only; the operative limit is the one in the clause immediately above.

2.4. For the purposes of the issuer limit, securities of subsidiaries and of entities whose obligations the issuer guarantees are aggregated with those of the issuer.

2.5. Within the Private placement sleeve, no single security shall exceed 5% of net asset value. This limit applies in addition to the general single security limit and the issuer limit.

## 3. Sector limits

3.1. Exposure to the Energy sector shall not exceed 15% of net asset value.

3.2. Exposure to the Utilities sector shall not exceed 20% of net asset value.

3.3. Sector classification follows the benchmark provider's scheme; a reclassification takes effect when the provider applies it.

## 4. Country limits

4.1. Exposure to issuers domiciled in United Kingdom shall not exceed 30% of net asset value.

4.2. Exposure to issuers domiciled in Italy shall not exceed 10% of net asset value.

4.3. Domicile is the issuer's country of risk under the Firm's country classification methodology, looking through financing vehicles to the ultimate obligor.

## 5. Restricted List

5.1. No security of an issuer appearing on the Firm's Restricted List may be purchased or added to under any circumstances.

5.2. This restriction cannot be waived by the Manager, the Chief Investment Officer or Compliance, and no other person or committee has authority to waive or override it. Section 7 does not apply to it.

5.3. Compliance maintains the Restricted List and notifies the dealing desk of each addition without delay.

## 6. Statement of practice (non-binding)

6.1. The Manager's typical initial position size is approximately 1% of net asset value. The sentence describes practice and creates no limit; an initial position of a different size is not a breach provided section 2 is observed.

## 7. Exceptions

7.1. An exception to a limit in section 2, 3 or 4 may be granted only with the approval of the portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer and Compliance, given in writing before the transaction.

7.2. The approval records the reason and the intended route back within the limit, and lapses 30 days after it is given unless renewed through the same procedure.

## 8. Breaches

8.1. Any breach is reported to Compliance without delay and entered in the breach register with its cause and the corrective action.

8.2. A passive breach is corrected under a plan agreed with Compliance and Risk that avoids forced selling to the detriment of unitholders. An active breach is corrected promptly and escalated to the Chief Investment Officer.

## 9. Review and records

9.1. The Fund Oversight Committee reviews the Guidelines periodically and after any material change to the Fund's mandate or regulatory framework.

9.2. Compliance retains the breach register, exception approvals and supporting reasoning in accordance with the Firm's record retention policy.

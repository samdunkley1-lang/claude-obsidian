# Midland Corporate Bond Fund: Investment Guidelines and Restrictions
Version 2, effective 2026-04-01

## 1. Introduction

1.1. Midland Asset Management (the "Manager") issues these Investment Guidelines and Restrictions (the "Guidelines") for the Midland Corporate Bond Fund (the "Fund"). They supplement the Investment Policy Statement and bind the portfolio manager, the dealing desk and every other person who deals for the Fund.

1.2. Nothing in these Guidelines relaxes any provision of the Investment Policy Statement. Where the instruments overlap, the tighter provision is the one that applies.

1.3. No limit in these Guidelines is to be read as a point-in-time test. Each is assessed on the position that would exist after the proposed transaction and must not cease to be satisfied at any later time.

1.4. Save where a clause states otherwise, a percentage is a percentage of the Fund's net asset value.

## 2. Single security and issuer concentration

2.1. It is not the case that any single security may exceed 2% of net asset value. No holding shall in any circumstance be permitted to rise above that level, and no purchase may be made whose effect would be that it does.

2.2. Aggregate exposure to any single issuer, including all securities of that issuer, shall in no case be permitted to exceed 3.5% of net asset value. For this purpose no security of a subsidiary, and no security guaranteed by the issuer, is excluded from the aggregate.

2.3. Within the Private placement sleeve, no single security shall exceed 5% of net asset value. The sleeve limit does not release a private placement from the tests in the preceding clauses of this section.

## 3. Sector concentration

3.1. Exposure to the Energy sector shall not at any time be permitted to exceed 15% of net asset value, nor may any transaction be effected the result of which would be that the Energy sector exceeds that level.

3.2. Nor is it the case that exposure to the Utilities sector may exceed 20% of net asset value. It is not open to the portfolio manager to permit the Utilities sector to be carried above that level.

3.3. Classification is not a matter of discretion for the portfolio manager; it follows the benchmark provider's scheme, and no reclassification is recognised before the provider applies it.

## 4. Country concentration

4.1. It is not the case that exposure to issuers domiciled in United Kingdom may exceed 30% of net asset value; no more than 30% of net asset value may at any time be represented by such issuers, nor may a trade be executed that would take the exposure above that figure.

4.2. Exposure to issuers domiciled in Italy may not, in any circumstance, be permitted to exceed 10% of net asset value, and it is not permissible to execute a transaction that would cause it to do so.

4.3. Domicile is not determined by the place of incorporation of a financing vehicle; it is the country of risk of the ultimate obligor under the Firm's country classification methodology.

## 5. Restricted List

5.1. No security of an issuer appearing on the Firm's Restricted List may be purchased or added to under any circumstances.

5.2. This restriction cannot be waived by the Manager, the Chief Investment Officer or Compliance. No other person and no committee of the Firm has authority to waive or override it, and the procedure in section 7 does not extend to it.

5.3. Compliance administers the Restricted List and notifies the dealing desk of every addition without delay.

## 6. Non-binding observation

6.1. The Manager's typical initial position size is approximately 1% of net asset value. This clause records practice, not a requirement; it is not monitored as a limit, and an initial position of a different size is not a breach provided section 2 is satisfied.

## 7. Exceptions

7.1. An exception to a limit in section 2, 3 or 4 may not be granted by anyone other than the portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer and Compliance acting together, and may not be granted otherwise than in writing before the transaction is executed.

7.2. The written approval must state the reason for the exception and the intended route back within the limit.

7.3. No exception remains in force for longer than 30 days unless renewed through the same procedure.

## 8. Breaches

8.1. No breach may go unreported: Compliance is to be informed without delay and the breach register updated with the cause and the corrective action.

8.2. Where a breach arises passively, the portfolio manager agrees a correction plan with Compliance and Risk that does not expose unitholders to forced selling. Where a breach arises from a trade, the position is corrected promptly and the Chief Investment Officer is informed.

## 9. Review and records

9.1. The Fund Oversight Committee reviews these Guidelines periodically and after any material change to the Fund's mandate or regulatory framework.

9.2. Compliance retains the breach register, exception approvals and supporting reasoning in accordance with the Firm's record retention policy.

# Midland Corporate Bond Fund: Corporate Actions Election Policy
Version 1, effective 2025-01-01

INTERNAL MEMORANDUM

To: Portfolio Management, Operations, Compliance
From: Head of Investment Operations, on behalf of the Chief Investment Officer
Date: 2025-01-01
Subject: Corporate Actions Election Policy for the Midland Corporate Bond Fund

## Purpose

This memorandum sets out the Corporate Actions Election Policy (the "Policy") for the Midland Corporate Bond Fund (the "Fund"). It governs how the Fund responds when an issuer offers a choice, and it binds the portfolio manager, the operations team that instructs the custodian, and Compliance. Mandatory events that the custodian processes without an election fall outside the Policy.

## How this memorandum is organised

The body of the memorandum describes the procedure for each type of event. The default election that applies to each event, and the instruction deadline, are not stated in the body; they are set out in Schedule A at the end of this memorandum. Wherever the body refers to "the default set out in Schedule A" or "the deadline in Schedule A", the reference is to the entry in that Schedule for the relevant event type, and the Schedule forms part of the Policy.

## Scrip dividends

When an issuer offers a scrip dividend, the Fund elects the default set out in Schedule A. Departing from that default requires a documented rationale from the portfolio manager, and Schedule A records whether such a departure is available to this Fund at all.

## Rights issues

When a rights issue is announced, the Fund follows the default set out in Schedule A. Any decision to depart from it, whether by allowing the rights to lapse or by selling the nil-paid rights, requires a documented rationale from the portfolio manager, retained by operations.

## Tender offers

A tender offer is handled in accordance with the default set out in Schedule A. Departing from that default requires the approval of the Chief Investment Officer, obtained before the instruction is sent.

## Interaction with the Investment Guidelines

Any election that would cause a holding to exceed the single security limit set out in the Investment Guidelines and Restrictions may proceed only if an exception has been approved under that document beforehand. The limit itself is not restated in this memorandum; the reader is referred to the Investment Guidelines and Restrictions for it.

## Instruction deadline

Elections shall be instructed to the custodian no later than the deadline in Schedule A. Operations maintains a diary of open events showing the custodian deadline for each, circulates it to the portfolio manager on announcement and confirms each instruction once sent. If the portfolio manager has not responded in time to meet the deadline in Schedule A, operations instructs the default set out in Schedule A and records that it has done so.

## Documented rationale

A documented rationale is a written note from the portfolio manager explaining why the default set out in Schedule A is not in the interests of unitholders for the event in question. It is retained by operations with the custodian instruction.

## Exceptions

An exception to a default set out in Schedule A, or to the deadline in Schedule A, may be granted only with the approval of the portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer and Compliance. The approval must be in writing, must precede the instruction to the custodian and must record the reason. It lapses 30 days after it is given unless renewed through the same procedure.

## Breaches

An election made otherwise than in accordance with the Policy, or an instruction sent after the deadline in Schedule A, is reported to Compliance without delay and entered in the breach register with its cause and the corrective action. Where a late or incorrect instruction has financial consequences for the Fund, the Firm's error correction procedure determines whether unitholders are to be compensated, and the matter is escalated to the Chief Investment Officer.

## Records and review

Operations retains the event diary, custodian instructions, documented rationales and approvals, and Compliance retains the breach register, in accordance with the Firm's record retention policy. The Fund Oversight Committee reviews the Policy, including Schedule A, periodically and after any material change in the custodian's processes, the Fund's holdings or applicable regulation.

## Historical note

In recent years the majority of scrip alternatives offered to the Fund have been taken in cash. This is an observation about past events and is not a rule; the binding position is the default set out in Schedule A.

| Event type | Default election | Departure from the default |

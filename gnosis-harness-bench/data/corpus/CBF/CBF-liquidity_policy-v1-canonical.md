# Midland Corporate Bond Fund: Liquidity Risk Management Policy
Version 1, effective 2025-01-01

## Purpose and scope

- This Liquidity Risk Management Policy (the "Policy") governs the liquidity of the Midland Corporate Bond Fund (the "Fund") so that redemption requests can be met in normal and stressed conditions without disadvantaging the unitholders who remain.
- The Policy is issued by Midland Asset Management (the "Manager") and binds the portfolio manager, the dealing desk, Risk and the fund accounting team.
- It operates alongside the Investment Policy Statement and the Investment Guidelines and Restrictions. Each limit below is tested after giving effect to the proposed transaction and must continue to be satisfied thereafter.

## Liquidity classification

- Every position is assigned a liquidity classification by Risk, drawing on vendor liquidity scores, observed trading activity, issue size and the depth of dealer quotations.
- A position is classified as illiquid where Risk concludes that it could not be sold at or near its carrying value within the horizon used for the Fund's liquidity profile without materially moving the price.
- Classifications are refreshed at each valuation point and reviewed by Risk whenever market conditions change materially.

## Portfolio liquidity limits

- Holdings classified as illiquid shall not exceed 10% of net asset value.
- At least 70% of net asset value shall be realisable within 5 business days under normal market conditions.
- No single position shall require more than 5 business days to liquidate at 25% of average daily volume. The days to liquidate figure is computed by Risk from the size of the position and the average daily volume of the security.

## Swing pricing

- Swing pricing shall be applied on any dealing day on which net flows exceed 0.75% of net asset value.
- On such a day the net asset value per unit is adjusted by the swing factor determined by the fund accounting team, so that the transaction costs of meeting the flows are borne by the dealing unitholders rather than by those who remain.
- The swing factor methodology is documented separately and reviewed by the Fund Oversight Committee.

## Context that is not a limit

- At the last review illiquid holdings represented approximately 3% of net asset value. This figure is reported for context only. It is a snapshot rather than a target, a floor or a ceiling, and the only limit on illiquid holdings is the one stated above.

## Stress testing

- Risk runs liquidity stress tests on the portfolio on a regular cycle, modelling a range of redemption scenarios and market liquidity conditions, and reports the results to the portfolio manager and the Fund Oversight Committee.
- The results inform the liquidity classification and the portfolio manager's positioning but do not in themselves create additional limits.

## Exceptions

- An exception to any limit in this Policy may be granted only with the approval of the portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer and Risk.
- The approval must be in writing, must precede the transaction and must record the reason and the intended route back within the limit.
- An exception lapses 30 days after approval unless renewed through the same procedure.

## Breaches

- Any breach is reported to Compliance without delay and entered in the breach register with its cause and the corrective action.
- Where the breach arises passively, through a reclassification, a redemption or a deterioration in market liquidity, the portfolio manager agrees a correction plan with Compliance and Risk that avoids forced selling to the detriment of unitholders.
- Where the breach results from a trade, the position is corrected promptly and the Chief Investment Officer is informed.

## Governance and records

- The Fund Oversight Committee reviews this Policy periodically and after any material change to the Fund's dealing terms, its investor base or applicable regulation.
- Risk retains the liquidity classifications, stress test results and days to liquidate calculations, and Compliance retains the breach register and exception approvals, in accordance with the Firm's record retention policy.

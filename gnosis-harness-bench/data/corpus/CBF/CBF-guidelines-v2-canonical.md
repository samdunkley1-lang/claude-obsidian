# Midland Corporate Bond Fund: Investment Guidelines and Restrictions
Version 2, effective 2026-04-01

The Investment Guidelines and Restrictions set out in this document govern the concentration of the Midland Corporate Bond Fund's portfolio and identify the securities the Fund may not hold. They sit beneath the Investment Policy Statement, which fixes the Fund's objective and its broader risk budget, and they bind the portfolio manager, the dealing desk and anyone else who transacts on the Fund's behalf. Where a provision here and a provision of the Investment Policy Statement both bear on the same exposure, whichever is tighter governs. This second version was adopted to revise the aggregate issuer limit; the remaining provisions are carried forward from the previous version without change.

Every limit expressed as a percentage refers to the Fund's net asset value, and every limit is tested after giving effect to the proposed transaction rather than before it. A limit is not a point-in-time test that is passed at execution and then forgotten. The portfolio must remain within it continuously, and a passive drift beyond it is a breach even though no trade caused it.

Turning to individual holdings, no single security shall exceed 2% of net asset value. Aggregate exposure to any single issuer, including all securities of that issuer, shall not exceed 3.5% of net asset value, and for this purpose the securities of subsidiaries and of entities whose obligations the issuer guarantees are aggregated with those of the issuer itself. The Fund also operates a Private placement sleeve for unlisted and privately negotiated debt. Within the Private placement sleeve, no single security shall exceed 5% of net asset value. That sleeve limit does not displace the general single security limit or the issuer limit; a private placement must satisfy each of them at the same time.

The Guidelines cap those sectors that the Manager regards as carrying distinctive cyclical or regulatory risk. Exposure to the Energy sector shall not exceed 15% of net asset value. Exposure to the Utilities sector shall not exceed 20% of net asset value. Sector membership is taken from the classification scheme of the Fund's benchmark provider, and a reclassification by the provider takes effect for the purposes of these limits on the date the provider applies it.

Country limits apply in addition to the sector limits. Exposure to issuers domiciled in United Kingdom shall not exceed 30% of net asset value. Exposure to issuers domiciled in Italy shall not exceed 10% of net asset value. Domicile is determined by the issuer's country of risk under the Firm's country classification methodology, which looks through financing subsidiaries and special purpose vehicles to the ultimate obligor.

The Firm's Restricted List stands apart from everything else in this document. No security of an issuer appearing on the Firm's Restricted List may be purchased or added to under any circumstances. This restriction cannot be waived by the Manager, the Chief Investment Officer or Compliance, and it is equally beyond the authority of any other individual or committee. The exception procedure described later in this document has no application to it. Compliance keeps the Restricted List, notifies the dealing desk of every addition without delay and ensures the pre-trade compliance system reflects the change before the notification is regarded as complete.

For context rather than as a rule, the Manager's typical initial position size is approximately 1% of net asset value. That figure describes how the portfolio manager usually builds a position and nothing more. A larger or smaller initial position is not a breach of these Guidelines so long as the single security and issuer limits are respected.

Where the portfolio manager wishes to exceed a single security, issuer, sector or country limit, or discovers that such a limit has been exceeded, an exception may be sought. It may be granted only with the approval of the portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer and Compliance, must be documented before the relevant transaction is executed, and must record the reason together with the intended path back within the limit. Any exception lapses 30 days after it is granted unless renewed through the same procedure.

A breach of any kind is reported to Compliance without delay and recorded in the breach register with its cause and the action taken. Where the breach is passive, arising from market movement, a reclassification, a redemption or a corporate action, the portfolio manager agrees a correction plan with Compliance and Risk designed to avoid forced selling that would harm unitholders. Where the breach results from a trade, the position is corrected promptly and the matter is escalated to the Chief Investment Officer.

The Fund Oversight Committee reviews these Guidelines periodically and whenever the Fund's mandate or regulatory framework changes materially. Compliance retains the breach register, the exception approvals and the reasoning that supports them for the period required by the Firm's record retention policy.

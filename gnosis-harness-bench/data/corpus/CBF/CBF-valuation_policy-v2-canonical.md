# Midland Corporate Bond Fund: Valuation and Pricing Policy
Version 2, effective 2026-04-01

## Preamble

This Valuation and Pricing Policy governs the pricing of the assets of the Midland Corporate Bond Fund for the calculation of its net asset value. Version 2 is a restatement of the previous version in tabular form. No requirement has been added, removed or altered; the change has been made so that the fund accounting team and the pricing function can check each valuation point against a single set of tables. The Policy is issued by Midland Asset Management and binds the portfolio manager, the fund accounting team, the pricing function and the Valuation Committee.

Prices are sourced from the Fund's primary pricing vendor, corroborated against a secondary vendor and observable dealer quotations, and selected according to the source hierarchy that the pricing function maintains for each instrument type. Where the primary source fails, the hierarchy determines the substitute and the reason is documented. The tables below set out the rules that apply once a price has been obtained.

## Table 1: Pricing rules

| Subject | Rule | Responsibility |
| - | - | - |
| Odd lot positions | Positions held in odd lots shall be valued using odd-lot prices. Institutional round-lot prices shall not be applied to odd-lot positions. | The pricing function identifies odd lot positions at each valuation point and applies the vendor's odd-lot service or a dealer quotation for the size actually held |
| Level 3 assets | Any mark applied to a Level 3 asset requires prior approval of the Valuation Committee. | The pricing function prepares a valuation memorandum covering methodology, inputs and sensitivity; the Valuation Committee approves and minutes its reasoning before the mark is used |
| Stale price | A price that has not been updated for more than 5 business days is deemed stale and must be referred to the Valuation Committee before use. | The pricing function runs the staleness report at each valuation point; the Valuation Committee confirms, substitutes or adjusts the price and documents the basis |

## Table 2: Exceptions and escalation

| Matter | Provision |
| - | - |
| Who may approve an exception to a rule in Table 1 | The portfolio manager and the Valuation Committee, jointly and in writing, before the price is used |
| Record of the exception | The reason and basis are recorded in the Valuation Committee minutes |
| Retrospective approval | Not available; an exception granted after the event does not cure a breach |
| Error discovered after the net asset value is struck | Reported to Compliance without delay; effect quantified by the fund accounting team; the Firm's error correction procedure determines any compensation; event logged in the pricing incident log |

## Descriptive note on review frequency

The Valuation Committee reviews its pricing sources approximately every 90 days. That review frequency describes the Committee's usual practice and is not a requirement of this Policy: the Committee may review sooner when circumstances call for it or allow a longer interval, and neither course is a breach. The review covers vendor accuracy, the volume of price challenges and their outcomes.

## Table 3: Roles

| Role | Responsibilities |
| - | - |
| Pricing function | Maintains the source hierarchy, obtains and corroborates prices, identifies odd lot positions, runs staleness reports, prepares Level 3 valuation memoranda and keeps the price source records |
| Fund accounting team | Calculates the net asset value from released prices and quantifies the effect of any pricing error |
| Valuation Committee | Approves Level 3 marks, resolves stale price referrals, approves exceptions jointly with the portfolio manager, reviews pricing sources and owns this Policy |
| Portfolio manager | Provides market colour on request and joins the Valuation Committee in approving any exception, but does not set prices |
| Compliance | Receives breach reports and retains breach and exception records |

## Governance

The Valuation Committee reviews this Policy periodically and after any material change in the Fund's holdings, its pricing vendors or applicable regulation. Records of price sources, staleness reports, valuation memoranda, Committee minutes and pricing incidents are retained in accordance with the Firm's record retention policy.

# Midland Corporate Bond Fund: Counterparty and Broker Policy
Version 1, effective 2025-01-01

INTERNAL MEMORANDUM

To: Portfolio Management, Dealing Desk, Treasury Operations, Risk and Compliance
From: Head of Counterparty Risk, on behalf of the Chief Investment Officer
Date: 2025-01-01
Subject: Counterparty and Broker Policy for the Midland Corporate Bond Fund

## Purpose

This memorandum sets out the Counterparty and Broker Policy (the "Policy") of the Midland Corporate Bond Fund (the "Fund"). The Policy controls the credit risk the Fund takes on the firms it deals with, whether as securities lending or repo counterparties, as banks holding the Fund's cash, or as brokers executing its trades, and it records the absolute bar on dealing with entities connected to Midland Asset Management (the "Manager"). It binds the portfolio manager, the dealing desk, treasury operations and Risk. Counterparty limits for derivatives are dealt with in the Derivatives Usage Policy and are not repeated here.

## Approved counterparties

Only firms on the approved counterparty list maintained by Risk may be used. Admission to the list follows a credit review covering financial strength, regulatory standing, operational capability and legal documentation, and each name is re-reviewed on a regular cycle and whenever its credit standing changes materially. The dealing desk may not trade with a firm that is not on the list.

## Securities lending and repo counterparties

Exposure to any single securities lending counterparty, and likewise to any single repo counterparty, shall not exceed 10% of net asset value. Exposure is measured as the market value of securities lent or cash placed with that counterparty, less the value of collateral received and held, after giving effect to the proposed transaction, and the limit continues to apply for as long as the exposure is outstanding.

## Affiliated entities

No transaction may be entered into with an entity affiliated with the Manager. An affiliated entity is any company in the Manager's group, any company under common control with the Manager and any vehicle managed by either of them. This conflict-of-interest prohibition cannot be overridden by any individual or committee. Neither the portfolio manager, nor the Chief Investment Officer, nor Compliance, nor Risk, nor the Fund Oversight Committee has authority to waive it, and the exception procedure below does not apply to it.

## Deposit banks

Cash deposits may only be placed with banks rated A- or better. Where a deposit bank is downgraded below A-, no new deposits may be placed with it, and existing deposits are withdrawn at maturity or earlier where the terms permit, with treasury operations reporting the position to Risk.

## Executing brokers

No single executing broker shall account for more than 25% of the Fund's annual traded value. The share attributable to any single executing broker is calculated by the dealing desk from traded value over the trailing year and reported to Risk, so that concentration with any single executing broker is identified before it reaches the limit.

## Context that is not a limit

Securities lending exposure to the largest counterparty is currently about 5% of net asset value. That figure is reported for information only; it is a snapshot of the position at the date of this memorandum, not a target and not a limit, and exposure to any single securities lending counterparty is constrained only by the limit stated above.

## Exceptions

An exception to the securities lending and repo limit, the deposit bank rating requirement or the executing broker limit may be granted only with the approval of the portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer and Risk, given in writing before the transaction and recording the reason. An exception lapses 30 days after approval unless renewed through the same procedure. No exception is available for the prohibition on affiliated entities.

## Breaches

Any breach is reported to Compliance without delay and recorded in the breach register with its cause and the corrective action. Where the breach arises passively, for example through a rating change or a movement in collateral values, Risk agrees a correction plan with the portfolio manager and treasury operations. Where it results from a transaction, the position is unwound promptly and the matter is escalated to the Chief Investment Officer.

## Records and review

Risk retains the approved counterparty list, credit reviews and exposure reports, and Compliance retains the breach register and exception approvals, in accordance with the Firm's record retention policy. The Fund Oversight Committee reviews this Policy periodically and after any material change in the Fund's trading activity, its counterparties or applicable regulation.

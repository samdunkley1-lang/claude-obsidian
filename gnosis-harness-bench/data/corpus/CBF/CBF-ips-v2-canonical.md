# Midland Corporate Bond Fund: Investment Policy Statement
Version 2, effective 2026-04-01

## 1. Preliminary

1.1. This Investment Policy Statement (the "Statement") is adopted by Midland Asset Management (the "Manager") for the Midland Corporate Bond Fund (the "Fund"). It takes effect on the date stated above and replaces the prior version in full.

1.2. Version 2 amends the modified duration band that applied under the prior version. No other limit, expectation or procedure has been altered, and the numbering of the clauses has been preserved so that existing references remain valid.

1.3. The Statement binds the portfolio manager, the dealing desk and every person who acts on the portfolio manager's instructions. It is to be read together with the Fund's Investment Guidelines and Restrictions and the Firm's other policies, each of which applies in addition to, and not in place of, this Statement.

## 2. Objective

2.1. The Fund seeks a total return in excess of its investment grade corporate bond benchmark over a full credit cycle, achieved principally through issuer selection and sector allocation within a controlled active risk budget.

2.2. The Fund is managed as a long-only credit portfolio. Cash is held for settlement and liquidity management and is not used as an active asset allocation position.

## 3. Cash, diversification and turnover

3.1. The Fund shall hold cash and cash equivalents of not less than 0% of net asset value.

3.2. Cash and cash equivalents shall not exceed 10% of net asset value.

3.3. The Fund shall hold a minimum of 100 distinct positions.

3.4. Annual portfolio turnover shall not exceed 100% of average net assets.

## 4. Market risk

4.1. Ex-ante tracking error against the benchmark shall not exceed 150 basis points.

4.2. Unhedged non-base-currency exposure shall not exceed 5% of net asset value.

4.3. Portfolio modified duration shall be maintained between 3.5 and 7 years.

## 5. Concentration

5.1. Exposure to the Financials sector shall not exceed 25% of net asset value.

5.2. Exposure to issuers domiciled in United States shall not exceed 50% of net asset value.

## 6. Credit quality

6.1. Holdings rated below investment grade shall not exceed 10% of net asset value.

6.2. No security rated below BB- (or equivalent) may be purchased.

## 7. Non-binding statements of expectation

7.1. In normal market conditions the Manager expects to hold cash of around 3% of net asset value. This sentence describes an expectation only. It creates no limit, and a cash level that differs from it is not a breach of the Statement provided the limits in section 3 are observed.

7.2. Historically the Fund's turnover has averaged approximately 60% per annum. This is a historical observation recorded for the information of the reader; the only turnover limit is the one stated in section 3.

## 8. Measurement

8.1. Each limit is measured on a post-trade basis, taking account of the exposure the proposed transaction would create, and continues to apply at all times thereafter.

8.2. Ratings are taken from the recognised agencies used by the Firm; where agencies differ, the Firm's rating hierarchy policy determines the rating applied.

## 9. Exceptions

9.1. An exception to any limit in section 3, section 5 or section 6 may be granted only by the portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer and Compliance acting together.

9.2. An exception to any limit in section 4 may be granted only by the portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer and Risk acting together.

9.3. Every exception must be recorded in writing before the relevant transaction is executed, must state the reason and the expected path back to compliance, and shall lapse no later than 30 days after it is granted unless renewed under the same procedure.

## 10. Breaches

10.1. Any breach, whether active or passive, shall be reported to Compliance without delay and entered in the breach register.

10.2. A passive breach shall be remedied within a timeframe agreed with Compliance and Risk, taking the interests of unitholders into account. An active breach shall be remedied promptly and escalated to the Chief Investment Officer.

## 11. Governance and review

11.1. The Fund Oversight Committee reviews this Statement periodically and whenever the mandate, benchmark or regulatory framework changes materially.

11.2. Compliance maintains the breach register, the register of exceptions and the approval records supporting each of them, in accordance with the Firm's record retention policy.

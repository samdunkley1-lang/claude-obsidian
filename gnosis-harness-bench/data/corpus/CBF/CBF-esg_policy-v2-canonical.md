# Midland Corporate Bond Fund: Responsible Investment Exclusions Policy
Version 2, effective 2026-04-01

## 1. Introduction

1.1. This Responsible Investment Exclusions Policy (the "Policy") is adopted by Midland Asset Management (the "Manager") for the Midland Corporate Bond Fund (the "Fund"). It identifies the issuers whose securities the Fund may not hold and describes how the exclusions are applied.

1.2. Version 2 tightens the revenue threshold of the thermal coal exclusion. The other provisions are carried forward from the previous version without change.

1.3. The Policy binds the portfolio manager, the dealing desk, Risk and Compliance. It applies to every security held or proposed to be held by the Fund, whether acquired in the primary or the secondary market.

## 2. Interpretation

2.1. "Issuer" includes the issuer's parent and any subsidiary whose activities are consolidated into the group's accounts, so that involvement at group level is attributed to each issuing entity.

2.2. Involvement, revenue shares and assessments of conduct are determined using the data of the Firm's appointed responsible investment research provider, supplemented where necessary by the Manager's own analysis, and are refreshed whenever the provider publishes an update.

## 3. Controversial weapons

3.1. Issuers involved in the production of controversial weapons (cluster munitions, anti-personnel mines, biological and chemical weapons) are excluded absolutely.

3.2. No exception may be granted by any person. Neither the portfolio manager, nor the Chief Investment Officer, nor Compliance, nor any committee of the Firm has the authority to waive, suspend or override this exclusion, and section 7 does not apply to it.

## 4. Thermal coal

4.1. Issuers deriving more than 20% of revenue from thermal coal are excluded, unless the security is a certified green bond whose proceeds are ring-fenced for transition projects.

4.2. Certification for the purpose of the carve-out means certification under a recognised green bond standard, evidenced by an external verification report held on file by Compliance, and ring-fencing means that the use of proceeds is contractually restricted to transition projects identified in the bond documentation. The carve-out attaches to the security; other securities of the same issuer remain excluded.

## 5. UN Global Compact

5.1. Issuers assessed as being in breach of the UN Global Compact principles are excluded.

5.2. The list of UN Global Compact violators is taken from the research provider's conduct assessment. An issuer placed on watch but not yet assessed as being in breach is not excluded under this section.

## 6. Carbon intensity ambition

6.1. The Manager aims to reduce the portfolio's weighted average carbon intensity by 30% relative to the benchmark over time.

6.2. The preceding clause expresses an ambition. It is not an exclusion, it is not a limit and it is not monitored as one; a portfolio whose carbon intensity differs from that ambition is not in breach of this Policy.

## 7. Exceptions

7.1. An exception to section 4 or section 5 may be granted only with the approval of the portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer and Compliance.

7.2. The approval must be in writing, must precede the transaction and must record the reason. It lapses 30 days after it is given unless renewed under the same procedure.

7.3. No exception is available in respect of section 3.

## 8. Implementation and breaches

8.1. Compliance loads the exclusion lists into the pre-trade compliance system and updates them whenever the research provider publishes a change.

8.2. Where a held issuer becomes excluded through a change of assessment or an amendment to the Policy, the portfolio manager agrees a disposal plan with Compliance and Risk that protects unitholders from forced selling, and the holding is recorded in the breach register until it is sold.

8.3. Where an excluded security is purchased, the trade is reported to Compliance without delay, reversed promptly and escalated to the Chief Investment Officer.

## 9. Governance

9.1. The Fund Oversight Committee reviews the Policy periodically and whenever the Fund's responsible investment commitments or the regulatory framework change materially.

9.2. Compliance retains the exclusion lists, exception approvals, verification reports and breach records in accordance with the Firm's record retention policy.

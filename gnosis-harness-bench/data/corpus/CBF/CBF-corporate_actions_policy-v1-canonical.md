# Midland Corporate Bond Fund: Corporate Actions Election Policy
Version 1, effective 2025-01-01

## 1. Purpose and scope

1.1. This Corporate Actions Election Policy (the "Policy") sets out how the Midland Corporate Bond Fund (the "Fund") responds to voluntary corporate actions affecting the securities it holds. It is issued by Midland Asset Management (the "Manager") and binds the portfolio manager, the operations team responsible for instructing the custodian, and Compliance.

1.2. Mandatory corporate actions, such as coupon payments, redemptions at maturity and compulsory exchanges, are processed by the custodian without an election and are outside the scope of this Policy. The Policy is concerned with events in which the Fund has a choice.

1.3. The Policy applies to every security held by the Fund, including securities held through the Private placement sleeve and any equity or hybrid instruments received through a restructuring.

## 2. Default elections

2.1. Scrip dividend. Scrip dividend alternatives shall be taken in cash by default. A scrip election requires a documented rationale from the portfolio manager and is not permitted for this Fund.

2.2. Rights issue. Rights issues shall be taken up by default. A decision to let rights lapse or to sell nil-paid rights requires a documented rationale from the portfolio manager.

2.3. Tender offer. Tender offers shall be declined by default. Tendering requires approval of the Chief Investment Officer.

2.4. Where taking up rights, or any other election, would cause a holding to exceed the single security limit set out in the Investment Guidelines and Restrictions, the election may proceed only if an exception under that document has been approved beforehand. The limit is not restated here and is to be read from the Investment Guidelines and Restrictions.

2.5. A documented rationale for the purpose of this section is a written note from the portfolio manager, retained by operations, explaining why the default is not in the interests of unitholders for the event in question.

## 3. Election deadline

3.1. Elections shall be instructed to the custodian no later than 2 business days before the custodian deadline.

3.2. Operations maintains a diary of open events with the custodian deadline for each, circulates it to the portfolio manager on announcement and confirms the instruction once it has been sent. Where the portfolio manager has not responded in time to meet the election deadline, operations instructs the default under section 2 and records that it did so.

## 4. Historical note

4.1. In recent years the majority of scrip alternatives offered to the Fund have been taken in cash. This clause records what has happened and is not a rule; the binding position on scrip dividends is the default in section 2.

## 5. Exceptions

5.1. An exception to a default election or to the election deadline may be granted only with the approval of the portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer and Compliance.

5.2. The approval must be in writing, must be obtained before the instruction is sent to the custodian and must record the reason. It lapses 30 days after it is given unless renewed through the same procedure.

## 6. Breaches

6.1. An election made otherwise than in accordance with this Policy, or an instruction sent after the election deadline, is reported to Compliance without delay and entered in the breach register with its cause and the corrective action.

6.2. Where a late or incorrect instruction has financial consequences for the Fund, the Firm's error correction procedure determines whether unitholders are to be compensated, and the matter is escalated to the Chief Investment Officer.

## 7. Records and review

7.1. Operations retains the event diary, custodian instructions, documented rationales and approvals, and Compliance retains the breach register, in accordance with the Firm's record retention policy.

7.2. The Fund Oversight Committee reviews this Policy periodically and after any material change in the custodian's processes, the Fund's holdings or applicable regulation.

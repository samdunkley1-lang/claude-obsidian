# Midland Corporate Bond Fund: Responsible Investment Exclusions Policy
Version 2, effective 2026-04-01

## How to use this document

The Midland Corporate Bond Fund applies a short list of issuer exclusions. This document presents them as a register so that the dealing desk, Risk and Compliance can check a proposed purchase against each row in turn. Each row of the register records what is excluded, whether the exclusion can be lifted and by whom. The text around the tables explains the mechanics and should not be read as adding further exclusions.

An issuer is assessed at group level: the parent and every consolidated subsidiary share the same status. The Firm's appointed responsible investment research provider is the primary source for involvement data, revenue shares and conduct assessments. The Manager's own research may supplement the provider's classification but may not contradict it without a documented reason accepted by Compliance.

## Exclusions register

| Exclusion | What is caught | Carve-out | Can it be lifted? | Who can approve |
| - | - | - | - | - |
| Controversial weapons | Any issuer involved in producing controversial weapons, namely cluster munitions, anti-personnel mines, biological and chemical weapons. The exclusion is absolute. | None | Never. No person or committee in the Firm can waive or override this row. | Nobody |
| Thermal coal | Any issuer that derives more than 20% of its revenue from thermal coal. | A security that is a certified green bond whose proceeds are ring-fenced for transition projects may be held even though its issuer is otherwise caught. | Yes, in writing, before the trade | portfolio manager, Chief Investment Officer, Compliance |
| UN Global Compact violators | Any issuer assessed as being in breach of the UN Global Compact principles. | None | Yes, in writing, before the trade | portfolio manager, Chief Investment Officer, Compliance |

## Lifting an exclusion

| Element | Requirement |
| - | - |
| Rows eligible | Thermal coal, UN Global Compact violators |
| Approval | Written approval of the portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer and Compliance, obtained before the trade |
| Content of approval | The reason for the exception and the intended route to disposal or to compliance |
| Duration | Lapses 30 days after approval unless renewed through the same route |
| Controversial weapons | No exception is available under any circumstances |

## An ambition that is not a rule

Separately from the exclusions, the Manager aims to reduce the portfolio's weighted average carbon intensity by 30% relative to the benchmark over time. This is an ambition rather than a limit. Risk does not test the portfolio against it, no approval is needed to depart from it, and a portfolio that has not yet reached it is not in breach of anything in this document.

## What happens when an issuer becomes excluded

Compliance keeps the register loaded in the pre-trade compliance system and refreshes it whenever the research provider changes an assessment. If an issuer the Fund already holds moves into an excluded row, the portfolio manager and Compliance, together with Risk, agree a plan to sell the holding in a way that does not force the Fund into a poor exit, and the holding sits in the breach register until it has gone. If a security in an excluded row is bought by mistake, Compliance is told without delay, the trade is reversed as soon as practicable and the Chief Investment Officer is informed.

## Oversight

The Fund Oversight Committee looks at this document on a regular cycle and whenever the Fund's responsible investment commitments or the surrounding regulation change materially. Compliance keeps the register, the approvals, the verification reports supporting any green bond carve-out and the breach records for as long as the Firm's record retention policy requires.

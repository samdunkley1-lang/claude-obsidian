# Midland Corporate Bond Fund: Investment Guidelines and Restrictions
Version 2, effective 2026-04-01

## 1. Scope

1.1. These Guidelines apply to the Midland Corporate Bond Fund (the "Fund") and are issued by Midland Asset Management (the "Manager") under the authority of the Fund Oversight Committee.

1.2. They operate beneath the Investment Policy Statement. If both instruments address the same exposure, the stricter provision governs.

1.3. Every person who trades for the Fund, instructs a trade or supervises trading is bound by these Guidelines.

## 2. Interpretation

2.1. A reference to a percentage is a reference to a percentage of the Fund's net asset value, save where a clause provides its own basis.

2.2. Compliance with a limit is assessed on the position that would result if the proposed transaction were completed, and the limit must continue to be met at all times after completion.

2.3. "Issuer" includes the issuer's subsidiaries and any entity whose obligations the issuer guarantees, so that exposures to the group are aggregated.

2.4. "Domicile" means the country of risk assigned under the Firm's country classification methodology, looking through financing vehicles to the ultimate obligor.

## 3. Holding size

3.1. The market value of any individual security held by the Fund may not be greater than 2% of net asset value.

3.2. The combined market value of every security issued by the same issuer may not be greater than 3.5% of net asset value.

3.3. Securities allocated to the Private placement sleeve are subject to a further test: within the Private placement sleeve, the market value of any individual security may not be greater than 5% of net asset value. The general holding and issuer tests in this section continue to apply to such securities.

## 4. Sector concentration

4.1. The combined market value of issuers classified to the Energy sector may not be greater than 15% of net asset value.

4.2. The combined market value of issuers classified to the Utilities sector may not be greater than 20% of net asset value.

4.3. Classification follows the scheme of the benchmark provider, and a reclassification takes effect for these purposes when the provider applies it.

## 5. Country concentration

5.1. The combined market value of issuers domiciled in United Kingdom may not be greater than 30% of net asset value.

5.2. The combined market value of issuers domiciled in Italy may not be greater than 10% of net asset value.

## 6. Restricted List

6.1. The Fund is prohibited from buying, or increasing an existing holding in, any security of an issuer that appears on the Firm's Restricted List. The prohibition applies in every circumstance.

6.2. Neither the Manager, nor the Chief Investment Officer, nor Compliance is able to waive this prohibition, and no other officer or committee of the Firm has any power to do so. Section 8 does not apply to it.

6.3. Compliance administers the Restricted List, alerts the dealing desk to each addition immediately and confirms that the pre-trade system has been updated.

## 7. Descriptive information

7.1. As a matter of practice, the Manager's opening position in a new name is usually in the region of 1% of net asset value. This is a description of habit rather than a rule; it is not monitored, and an opening position of a different size does not contravene these Guidelines provided section 3 is satisfied.

## 8. Departures

8.1. A departure from any provision of sections 3, 4 or 5 requires the prior written consent of the portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer and Compliance.

8.2. The consent must record the grounds for the departure and the manner in which the Fund will return within the limit.

8.3. Consent ceases to have effect 30 days after it is given, unless it is renewed in the same manner.

## 9. Breach management

9.1. Compliance is to be informed of any breach without delay, and the breach, its cause and the corrective action are to be entered in the breach register.

9.2. Where the breach did not result from a trade, correction follows a plan agreed between the portfolio manager, Compliance and Risk that protects unitholders from forced disposals.

9.3. Where the breach resulted from a trade, correction is immediate and the Chief Investment Officer is informed.

## 10. Review

10.1. The Fund Oversight Committee reviews these Guidelines on a regular cycle and after any material change in the Fund's mandate or in applicable regulation.

10.2. Records of breaches, consents and their supporting reasons are retained by Compliance in line with the Firm's record retention policy.

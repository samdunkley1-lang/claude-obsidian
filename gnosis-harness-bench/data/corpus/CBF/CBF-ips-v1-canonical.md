# Midland Corporate Bond Fund: Investment Policy Statement
Version 1, effective 2025-01-01

INTERNAL MEMORANDUM

To: Portfolio Management, Risk, Compliance and the Fund Oversight Committee
From: Chief Investment Officer, Midland Asset Management
Date: 2025-01-01
Subject: Investment Policy Statement for the Midland Corporate Bond Fund

## Purpose of this memorandum

This memorandum sets out the Investment Policy Statement (the "Statement") that governs the management of the Midland Corporate Bond Fund (the "Fund"). It records the objective of the Fund, the mandatory limits within which the portfolio manager must operate, the process by which an exception may be sought, and those expectations of the Manager that are not themselves limits. It replaces every earlier draft and circular on the same subject. Anyone involved in managing, dealing for, risk monitoring or compliance testing the Fund is expected to read it and to raise any question of interpretation with Compliance before acting.

## Objective and approach

The Fund seeks a total return, from income and capital combined, in excess of its investment grade corporate bond benchmark over a full credit cycle, while keeping active risk within a disciplined budget. The portfolio manager pursues that objective through bottom-up credit selection, supported by a top-down view on sector allocation, curve positioning and currency exposure. The Fund is intended to be fully invested in corporate credit; cash is held for settlement and liquidity purposes rather than as an active position.

## Binding investment limits

The limits in this section are mandatory. Each is measured on a post-trade basis, taking into account the exposure that a proposed transaction would create, and each applies continuously rather than only at the moment of dealing. Percentages refer to the Fund's net asset value unless stated otherwise.

Cash. The Fund shall hold cash and cash equivalents of not less than 0% of net asset value. Cash and cash equivalents shall not exceed 10% of net asset value.

Diversification. The Fund shall hold a minimum of 100 distinct positions.

Turnover. Annual portfolio turnover shall not exceed 100% of average net assets.

Active risk. Ex-ante tracking error against the benchmark shall not exceed 150 basis points.

Sector and country concentration. Exposure to the Financials sector shall not exceed 25% of net asset value. Exposure to issuers domiciled in United States shall not exceed 50% of net asset value.

Currency. Unhedged non-base-currency exposure shall not exceed 5% of net asset value.

Interest rate risk. Portfolio modified duration shall be maintained between 4 and 7 years.

Credit quality. Holdings rated below investment grade shall not exceed 10% of net asset value. No security rated below BB- (or equivalent) may be purchased.

## Statements of expectation

What follows is provided for context. These sentences are not limits, Risk does not monitor them as limits, and a portfolio that departs from them is not in breach of this Statement.

In normal market conditions the Manager expects to hold cash of around 3% of net asset value. Historically the Fund's turnover has averaged approximately 60% per annum.

## Exceptions

Where a binding limit is exceeded, or where the portfolio manager wishes to take an action that would cause it to be exceeded, an exception may be requested. For the cash, diversification, turnover, sector, country and credit quality limits, an exception requires the approval of the portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer and Compliance. For the tracking error, currency and duration limits, it requires the approval of the portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer and Risk. Approval must be documented before the action is taken, and no exception may remain in force for longer than 30 days without being renewed through the same process.

## Breach handling

A passive breach arising from market movement, a rating change, a redemption or a corporate action is not a disciplinary matter, but it must be reported to Compliance without delay and corrected within a period agreed with Compliance and Risk, having regard to the interests of unitholders. An active breach caused by a trade is escalated to the Chief Investment Officer and recorded in the breach register together with the remedial action taken.

## Monitoring, review and record keeping

Risk monitors the limits in this Statement through the pre-trade compliance system and through independent post-trade checks. Compliance retains the breach register, exception approvals and the reasoning behind each decision for the period required by the Firm's record retention policy. The Fund Oversight Committee reviews this Statement periodically and whenever a material change to the Fund's mandate, benchmark or regulatory environment occurs. An amendment takes effect only once approved by the Committee and communicated in writing to the addressees of this memorandum.

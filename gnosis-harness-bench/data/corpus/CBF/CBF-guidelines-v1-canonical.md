# Midland Corporate Bond Fund: Investment Guidelines and Restrictions
Version 1, effective 2025-01-01

## Purpose and status

- These Investment Guidelines and Restrictions (the "Guidelines") supplement the Investment Policy Statement of the Midland Corporate Bond Fund (the "Fund"). They set the concentration limits and the trading restrictions within which the portfolio is to be managed.
- The Guidelines bind the portfolio manager, the dealing desk and any other person who transacts for the Fund. Where the Guidelines and the Investment Policy Statement both address the same exposure, the tighter of the provisions applies.
- Unless a section states a different basis, every limit is a percentage of the Fund's net asset value and is tested after giving effect to the proposed transaction. A limit that is satisfied at the point of trade must continue to be satisfied thereafter.

## Single security and issuer limits

- No single security shall exceed 2% of net asset value.
- Aggregate exposure to any single issuer, including all securities of that issuer, shall not exceed 3% of net asset value. Securities of subsidiaries and of guaranteed entities are aggregated with those of the parent or guarantor for this purpose.
- Within the Private placement sleeve, no single security shall exceed 5% of net asset value. This sleeve limit operates alongside the general single security limit and the issuer limit rather than in place of them.

## Sector limits

- Exposure to the Energy sector shall not exceed 15% of net asset value.
- Exposure to the Utilities sector shall not exceed 20% of net asset value.
- Sector classification follows the scheme used by the Fund's benchmark provider. Where an issuer is reclassified, the new classification applies from the date the benchmark provider gives effect to it.

## Country limits

- Exposure to issuers domiciled in United Kingdom shall not exceed 30% of net asset value.
- Exposure to issuers domiciled in Italy shall not exceed 10% of net asset value.
- Domicile means the issuer's country of risk as determined under the Firm's country classification methodology, which looks through financing vehicles to the ultimate obligor.

## Restricted List

- No security of an issuer appearing on the Firm's Restricted List may be purchased or added to under any circumstances.
- This restriction cannot be waived by the Manager, the Chief Investment Officer or Compliance, and no other person or committee within the Firm has authority to waive it. The exception procedure described below does not extend to it.
- Compliance maintains the Restricted List, notifies the dealing desk of each addition without delay and confirms that the pre-trade compliance system has been updated before the notification is treated as complete.

## Context that is not a limit

- The Manager's typical initial position size is approximately 1% of net asset value. This sentence describes the Manager's usual practice and is not a requirement. An initial position that is larger or smaller than this figure does not breach the Guidelines provided the single security and issuer limits are observed.

## Exceptions

- An exception to any limit in the sections on single security, issuer, sector and country exposure may be granted only with the approval of the portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer and Compliance.
- Approval must be documented before the transaction is executed. The record must set out the reason for the exception and the intended route back within the limit.
- An exception lapses 30 days after it is granted unless renewed through the same procedure.

## Breaches

- Any breach shall be reported to Compliance without delay and entered in the breach register together with its cause and the remedial action taken.
- Where a breach arises passively, through market movement, an issuer reclassification, a redemption or a corporate action, the portfolio manager agrees a correction plan with Compliance and Risk that avoids forced selling to the detriment of unitholders.
- Where a breach arises from a trade, the position is corrected promptly and the matter is escalated to the Chief Investment Officer.

## Review and records

- The Fund Oversight Committee reviews the Guidelines periodically and following any material change to the Fund's mandate or to the regulatory framework.
- Compliance retains the breach register, exception approvals and supporting rationale in accordance with the Firm's record retention policy.

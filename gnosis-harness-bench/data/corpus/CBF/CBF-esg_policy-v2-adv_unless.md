# Midland Corporate Bond Fund: Responsible Investment Exclusions Policy
Version 2, effective 2026-04-01

This Responsible Investment Exclusions Policy governs which issuers the Midland Corporate Bond Fund may not hold. It is adopted by Midland Asset Management as manager of the Fund, and it binds the portfolio manager, the dealing desk, Risk and Compliance in respect of every purchase for the Fund, whether made in the primary or the secondary market. This second version tightens the revenue threshold of the thermal coal exclusion and otherwise restates the previous version without change.

Assessment is carried out at group level, so that a parent and its consolidated subsidiaries share the same status. Involvement in an excluded activity, the share of revenue derived from it and any assessment of conduct are taken from the Firm's appointed responsible investment research provider, whose classifications may be supplemented by the Manager's own analysis but may not be overridden without a documented reason accepted by Compliance. Exclusion lists are refreshed in the pre-trade compliance system whenever the provider publishes an update.

Issuers involved in the production of controversial weapons (cluster munitions, anti-personnel mines, biological and chemical weapons) are excluded absolutely. No exception may be granted by any person. Neither the portfolio manager, nor the Chief Investment Officer, nor Compliance, nor any committee of the Firm has authority to waive, suspend or override this exclusion, and the exception procedure described later in this Policy has no application to it.

Issuers deriving more than 20% of revenue from thermal coal are excluded. Revenue share is measured using the research provider's most recent assessment of the issuer's consolidated revenue, and an issuer whose revenue share moves above the threshold becomes excluded from the date the provider publishes the change.

Issuers assessed as being in breach of the UN Global Compact principles are excluded. The screen for UN Global Compact violators relies on the research provider's conduct assessment, and an issuer that has merely been placed on watch, without being assessed as in breach, is not excluded on that ground.

The Manager also aims to reduce the portfolio's weighted average carbon intensity by 30% relative to the benchmark over time. That sentence expresses an ambition and nothing more. It is not an exclusion, it is not a limit, Risk does not test the portfolio against it and a portfolio whose carbon intensity has not reached the ambition is not in breach of this Policy.

The thermal coal exclusion set out earlier in this Policy is subject to a qualification. A security may be held even though its issuer derives more than 20% of revenue from thermal coal, but only where that security is a certified green bond whose proceeds are ring-fenced for transition projects. Certification means certification under a recognised green bond standard, supported by an external verification report held on file by Compliance, and ring-fencing means that the bond documentation contractually confines the use of proceeds to the transition projects it identifies. The qualification attaches to the individual security; other securities of the same issuer remain excluded.

An exception to the thermal coal exclusion or to the UN Global Compact exclusion may be granted only with the approval of the portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer and Compliance. The approval must be in writing, must be obtained before the transaction and must record the reason, and it lapses 30 days after it is given unless renewed through the same procedure. No exception is available for the controversial weapons exclusion.

Compliance maintains the exclusion lists in the pre-trade compliance system. Where an issuer already held becomes excluded, through a change in the provider's assessment or an amendment to this Policy, the portfolio manager agrees a disposal plan with Compliance and Risk that protects unitholders from forced selling, and the holding is recorded in the breach register until it has been sold. Where an excluded security is purchased, the trade is reported to Compliance without delay, reversed promptly and escalated to the Chief Investment Officer.

The Fund Oversight Committee reviews this Policy periodically and whenever the Fund's responsible investment commitments or the regulatory framework change materially. Compliance retains the exclusion lists, exception approvals, verification reports and breach records for the period required by the Firm's record retention policy.

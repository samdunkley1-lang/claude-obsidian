# Midland Corporate Bond Fund: Investment Policy Statement
Version 2, effective 2026-04-01

## Reader's guide

The tables below form the operative part of the Investment Policy Statement for the Midland Corporate Bond Fund. Rather than a clause-by-clause text, the Manager has chosen to present the mandate as a register of parameters, each with its binding requirement, the measure against which it is tested and the people who can authorise a departure. The prose surrounding the tables explains how the register is to be read; it neither adds to nor subtracts from it.

Every requirement in Table A and Table B is mandatory. Compliance with a requirement is tested after giving effect to any proposed trade, and the requirement continues to apply at all times afterwards, so a limit that is met at the moment of dealing but drifts outside its bound later still counts as a breach. Percentages are percentages of the Fund's net asset value except where the table states a different basis.

This is the second version of the Statement. The only change from the previous version is a wider modified duration corridor at its lower end; everything else carries over unchanged.

## Table A: Portfolio construction

| Parameter | Binding requirement | How it is measured | Who may authorise a departure |
| - | - | - | - |
| Cash and cash equivalents, floor | Must be at least 0% of net asset value | Settled cash plus qualifying cash equivalents, post-trade | portfolio manager, Chief Investment Officer, Compliance |
| Cash and cash equivalents, ceiling | May not exceed 10% of net asset value | As above | portfolio manager, Chief Investment Officer, Compliance |
| Number of positions | Must be no fewer than 100 distinct positions | Count of distinct securities held | portfolio manager, Chief Investment Officer, Compliance |
| Portfolio turnover | May not exceed 100% of average net assets in any year | Annual turnover ratio as computed by Risk | portfolio manager, Chief Investment Officer, Compliance |
| Financials sector | May not exceed 25% of net asset value | Market value of issuers classified to Financials | portfolio manager, Chief Investment Officer, Compliance |
| Issuers domiciled in United States | May not exceed 50% of net asset value | Market value by issuer country of domicile | portfolio manager, Chief Investment Officer, Compliance |
| Below investment grade holdings | May not exceed 10% of net asset value | Market value of holdings rated below investment grade | portfolio manager, Chief Investment Officer, Compliance |
| Minimum rating at purchase | No purchase of any security rated below BB- (or equivalent) | Rating at the time of trade under the Firm's rating hierarchy | portfolio manager, Chief Investment Officer, Compliance |

## Table B: Market risk

| Parameter | Binding requirement | How it is measured | Who may authorise a departure |
| - | - | - | - |
| Ex-ante tracking error | May not exceed 150 basis points relative to the benchmark | Risk model forecast, post-trade | portfolio manager, Chief Investment Officer, Risk |
| Unhedged non-base-currency exposure | May not exceed 5% of net asset value | Net currency exposure after hedges | portfolio manager, Chief Investment Officer, Risk |
| Modified duration | Must remain between 3.5 and 7 years | Portfolio modified duration, post-trade | portfolio manager, Chief Investment Officer, Risk |

## Authorising a departure

A departure from any row of Table A or Table B is permitted only when each of the persons named in the final column of that row has approved it in writing before the trade is placed. The approval must record why the departure is needed and how the portfolio will be brought back within the requirement. An approval lapses 30 days after it is given; if the departure is still needed at that point, a fresh approval is required through the same route.

## Reference information that does not bind

The Manager also wishes readers to have the following context. It is deliberately kept out of the tables because it is not a requirement and Risk does not test against it.

Under normal market conditions the Manager expects the cash balance to sit somewhere around 3% of net asset value; a higher or lower balance within the floor and ceiling in Table A is entirely acceptable. Looking back over the Fund's history, turnover has come out at roughly 60% per annum on average, comfortably inside the ceiling in Table A, and the Manager does not treat that average as a target.

## When something goes wrong

If a requirement is found to have been exceeded, Compliance is told without delay and the event is logged in the breach register. Where the cause is a market move, a rating action, a redemption or a corporate action rather than a trade, the portfolio manager agrees a correction plan with Compliance and Risk that protects unitholders from forced selling. Where the cause is a trade, the position is corrected at once and the matter goes to the Chief Investment Officer.

## Ownership and review

The Fund Oversight Committee owns this Statement, revisits it on a regular cycle and amends it whenever the Fund's mandate, benchmark or regulatory setting changes in a way that matters. Compliance keeps the log of breaches, the file of approved departures and the supporting reasoning for as long as the Firm's retention rules require.

# Midland Corporate Bond Fund: Responsible Investment Exclusions Policy
Version 1, effective 2025-01-01

INTERNAL MEMORANDUM

To: Portfolio Management, Dealing Desk, Risk and Compliance
From: Head of Responsible Investment, on behalf of the Chief Investment Officer
Date: 2025-01-01
Subject: Responsible Investment Exclusions Policy for the Midland Corporate Bond Fund

## Purpose

This memorandum sets out the Responsible Investment Exclusions Policy (the "Policy") that applies to the Midland Corporate Bond Fund (the "Fund"). It identifies the categories of issuer whose securities the Fund may not hold, explains how each exclusion is applied and records the ambition the Manager has adopted that is not itself an exclusion. The Policy binds the portfolio manager, the dealing desk, Risk and Compliance, and it applies to every purchase for the Fund whether in the primary or the secondary market.

## Data and interpretation

Issuers are assessed at group level, so a parent and its consolidated subsidiaries share the same status. Involvement in an excluded activity, revenue shares and assessments of conduct are taken from the Firm's appointed responsible investment research provider. The Manager may supplement the provider's data with its own analysis, but may not override a provider classification without a documented reason approved by Compliance. Exclusion lists are refreshed in the pre-trade compliance system whenever the provider publishes an update.

## Controversial weapons: absolute exclusion

Issuers involved in the production of controversial weapons (cluster munitions, anti-personnel mines, biological and chemical weapons) are excluded absolutely. No exception may be granted by any person. To remove any doubt, neither the portfolio manager, nor the Chief Investment Officer, nor Compliance, nor the Fund Oversight Committee, nor any other committee of the Firm can waive, suspend or override this exclusion, and the exception procedure described later in this memorandum does not reach it.

## Thermal coal

Issuers deriving more than 30% of revenue from thermal coal are excluded, unless the security is a certified green bond whose proceeds are ring-fenced for transition projects. Certification means certification under a recognised green bond standard, supported by an external verification report held on file by Compliance, and ring-fencing means that the bond documentation contractually restricts the use of proceeds to the transition projects it identifies. The carve-out attaches to the security rather than the issuer: other securities of the same issuer remain excluded.

## UN Global Compact violators

Issuers assessed as being in breach of the UN Global Compact principles are excluded. The screen for UN Global Compact violators relies on the research provider's conduct assessment. An issuer that the provider has placed on watch, but has not assessed as being in breach, is not excluded under this heading.

## Carbon intensity: an ambition, not a rule

The Manager aims to reduce the portfolio's weighted average carbon intensity by 30% relative to the benchmark over time. This sentence records an ambition. It is not an exclusion, it is not a limit, Risk does not test the portfolio against it, and a portfolio whose carbon intensity has not reached the ambition is not in breach of the Policy.

## Exceptions

An exception to the thermal coal exclusion or to the UN Global Compact exclusion may be granted only with the approval of the portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer and Compliance. The approval must be given in writing before the transaction, must record the reason and lapses 30 days after it is given unless renewed through the same route. No exception of any kind is available for the controversial weapons exclusion.

## Implementation and breaches

Compliance maintains the exclusion lists in the pre-trade compliance system. Where an issuer already held becomes excluded, whether through a change in the provider's assessment or through an amendment to the Policy, the portfolio manager agrees a disposal plan with Compliance and Risk that protects unitholders from forced selling, and the holding is recorded in the breach register until it has been sold. Where an excluded security is purchased, the trade is reported to Compliance without delay, reversed promptly and escalated to the Chief Investment Officer.

## Governance and records

The Fund Oversight Committee reviews this Policy periodically and whenever the Fund's responsible investment commitments or the regulatory framework change materially. Compliance retains the exclusion lists, exception approvals, verification reports and breach records in accordance with the Firm's record retention policy. Questions of interpretation should be raised with the Head of Responsible Investment before a trade is placed.

# Midland Corporate Bond Fund: Investment Policy Statement
Version 2, effective 2026-04-01

Consolidated text incorporating the amendment effective 2026-04-01. The amendment altered the modified duration band. The version 1 wording of that band is reproduced next to the current clause and marked as superseded, so that the history of the provision can be traced without consulting the earlier document. Only the current clauses bind the Fund.

## 1. Application

1.1. This Investment Policy Statement (the "Statement") is adopted by Midland Asset Management (the "Manager") for the Midland Corporate Bond Fund (the "Fund") and binds the portfolio manager, the dealing desk and every person acting on the portfolio manager's instructions.

1.2. Text marked "superseded with effect from 2026-04-01" has no continuing effect and is retained for reference only. The operative provision is the clause standing next to it.

1.3. The Statement is read together with the Investment Guidelines and Restrictions and the Firm's other policies, which apply in addition to it and not in place of it.

## 2. Objective

2.1. The Fund aims to outperform its investment grade corporate bond benchmark over a full credit cycle through issuer selection and sector allocation within a controlled active risk budget. It is managed long-only, and cash is held for settlement and liquidity purposes rather than as an active position.

## 3. Cash, diversification and turnover

3.1. The Fund shall hold cash and cash equivalents of not less than 0% of net asset value.

3.2. Cash and cash equivalents shall not exceed 10% of net asset value.

3.3. The Fund shall hold a minimum of 100 distinct positions.

3.4. Annual portfolio turnover shall not exceed 100% of average net assets.

## 4. Market risk

4.1. Ex-ante tracking error against the benchmark shall not exceed 150 basis points.

4.2. Unhedged non-base-currency exposure shall not exceed 5% of net asset value.

4.3. Portfolio modified duration shall be maintained between 3.5 and 7 years.

4.4. Former clause, superseded with effect from 2026-04-01: "Portfolio modified duration shall be maintained between 4 and 7 years." This wording ceased to apply on that date and is reproduced for reference only; the operative band is the one in the clause immediately above.

## 5. Concentration

5.1. Exposure to the Financials sector shall not exceed 25% of net asset value.

5.2. Exposure to issuers domiciled in United States shall not exceed 50% of net asset value.

## 6. Credit quality

6.1. Holdings rated below investment grade shall not exceed 10% of net asset value.

6.2. No security rated below BB- (or equivalent) may be purchased.

## 7. Non-binding statements

7.1. In normal market conditions the Manager expects to hold cash of around 3% of net asset value. That is an expectation, not a limit; it is not monitored, and a cash balance that differs from it does not breach the Statement so long as section 3 is satisfied.

7.2. Historically the Fund's turnover has averaged approximately 60% per annum. The figure is descriptive. The turnover limit that binds the Fund is stated in section 3 and nowhere else.

## 8. Measurement

8.1. Every limit is tested on the position that would exist after the proposed transaction and must continue to be satisfied thereafter.

8.2. Ratings are those of the recognised agencies used by the Firm, resolved where they differ under the Firm's rating hierarchy policy.

## 9. Exceptions

9.1. An exception to a limit in section 3, section 5 or section 6 requires the written approval of the portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer and Compliance, obtained before the transaction.

9.2. An exception to a limit in section 4 requires the written approval of the portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer and Risk, obtained before the transaction.

9.3. The approval records the reason and the intended route back within the limit, and it lapses 30 days after it is given unless renewed by the same procedure.

## 10. Breaches

10.1. Compliance is informed of any breach without delay and enters it in the breach register with its cause and the corrective action.

10.2. A passive breach is corrected under a plan agreed with Compliance and Risk that protects unitholders from forced selling; an active breach is corrected promptly and escalated to the Chief Investment Officer.

## 11. Review and records

11.1. The Fund Oversight Committee reviews the Statement periodically and after any material change to the Fund's mandate, benchmark or regulatory framework.

11.2. Compliance retains the breach register, exception approvals and supporting reasoning in accordance with the Firm's record retention policy.

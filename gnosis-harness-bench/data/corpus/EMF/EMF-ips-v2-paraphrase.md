# Midland Emerging Markets Fund: Investment Policy Statement

Version 2, effective 2026-04-01

## 1. Introduction

1.1. This document is the Investment Policy Statement of the Midland Emerging Markets Fund (the "Fund"). It states the investment aim of the Fund and the portfolio parameters within which the Manager must operate.

1.2. The provisions below bind the Manager, each member of the portfolio management team and any delegate or sub-adviser appointed in respect of the Fund.

1.3. Should any provision of this Statement conflict with the Investment Guidelines and Restrictions, whichever provision is more restrictive shall prevail.

## 2. Investment aim

2.1. The Fund aims to grow capital over the long term by investing in a diversified selection of equity and equity-related securities of companies that are domiciled in emerging market economies or that derive the greater part of their economic activity from those economies.

2.2. The benchmark stated in the prospectus is used to assess performance and to measure relative risk. It does not dictate security selection except where a clause of this Statement so provides.

2.3. Sector and country attribution follows the benchmark provider's classification unless the Valuation Committee has resolved that a different attribution is appropriate for a specific holding.

## 3. Cash parameters

3.1. Cash and cash equivalents held by the Fund shall at all times be not less than 0% of net asset value; a net borrowed cash position at portfolio level is not permitted.

3.2. Cash and cash equivalents held by the Fund shall at no time exceed 8% of net asset value.

3.3. For information only, and without creating any restriction, the Manager anticipates that under ordinary market conditions the Fund's cash balance will be around 3% of net asset value.

## 4. Number of holdings and portfolio activity

4.1. The portfolio shall comprise not fewer than 45 distinct positions at all times. Where an issuer has several classes of security with differing economic rights, they are treated as distinct positions only if Compliance has confirmed that treatment.

4.2. Portfolio turnover over any rolling year shall not exceed 100% of average net assets. Turnover is measured as the lower of aggregate purchases and aggregate sales during the period, divided by average net assets for that period.

4.3. As a matter of record, and not as a restriction, the Fund's turnover has in the past averaged approximately 60% per annum.

## 5. Risk parameters

5.1. Forward-looking tracking error relative to the benchmark, as produced by the Manager's approved risk model, shall not exceed 700 basis points.

5.2. The aggregate weight of holdings classified within the Financials sector shall not exceed 30% of net asset value.

5.3. The aggregate weight of holdings in issuers domiciled in China shall not exceed 30% of net asset value.

## 6. Method of measurement

6.1. Each parameter expressed by reference to net asset value is assessed after giving effect to the proposed transaction, so that the test is applied to the existing exposure plus the change the transaction would cause.

6.2. A parameter that is exceeded solely through market movement, a corporate action or investor subscriptions and redemptions is not treated as a breach at the moment it occurs, but the portfolio manager is required to restore compliance in an orderly fashion once the position has been identified.

## 7. Exceptions

7.1. Any departure from the tracking error parameter in clause 5 requires the joint approval of the portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer and Risk.

7.2. Any departure from any other parameter in this Statement requires the joint approval of the portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer and Compliance.

7.3. An application for an exception shall be made in writing, shall explain the reason for the departure and the intended route back to compliance, and shall be recorded by Compliance in the exceptions register.

7.4. No exception shall subsist for a period exceeding 30 days.

7.5. Compliance shall report every exception granted under this clause to the Board at the Board's next scheduled meeting.

## 8. Breaches

8.1. On identifying a breach, Compliance shall promptly inform the portfolio manager and the Chief Investment Officer.

8.2. The portfolio manager shall propose a plan for remediation, and Compliance shall enter the breach, its cause and its resolution in the breach log.

8.3. Any breach that is material, or that has not been resolved within the time contemplated by its remediation plan, shall be escalated to the Board.

## 9. Ownership, review and records

9.1. The Chief Investment Officer owns this Statement, and the Board approves it and each amendment to it.

9.2. The Statement is reviewed annually and whenever a change to the prospectus, the benchmark or the regulatory status of the Fund affects a parameter set out above.

9.3. All records generated in operating this Statement are retained in accordance with the Manager's record-keeping policy.

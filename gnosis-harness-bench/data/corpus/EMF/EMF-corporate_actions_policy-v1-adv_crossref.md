# Midland Emerging Markets Fund: Corporate Actions Election Policy

Version 1, effective 2025-01-01

**INTERNAL MEMORANDUM**

To: Operations, Portfolio Management, Compliance and the Fund administrator
From: Head of Operations, on behalf of the Chief Investment Officer
Date: 2025-01-01
Subject: Corporate Actions Election Policy for the Midland Emerging Markets Fund

This memorandum sets out the Corporate Actions Election Policy (the "Policy") for the Midland Emerging Markets Fund (the "Fund"). It governs how elections on voluntary corporate actions are decided, documented and instructed. To keep the operative rules in one place, every default election and the internal deadline are set out in Schedule A at the end of this memorandum; the body below explains how those defaults and that deadline are to be applied. The Policy binds the addressees and any delegate involved in processing corporate actions, and it operates alongside the Investment Policy Statement and the Investment Guidelines and Restrictions.

**Definitions**

A voluntary corporate action is any event on which the holder is offered a choice; the events covered by this Policy are the scrip dividend, the rights issue and the tender offer. The custodian deadline is the latest time notified by the custodian for receipt of our instruction. The election deadline is the internal cut-off derived from the custodian deadline in the manner stated in Schedule A.

**Scrip dividend**

Where a scrip dividend is offered, Operations shall instruct the default set out in Schedule A unless the portfolio manager has delivered a documented rationale for electing the alternative. An election for scrip is prohibited where the post-election position would exceed the single security limit in the Investment Guidelines; Operations must confirm with Compliance, before releasing any scrip election, that the resulting position would remain within that limit.

**Rights issue**

Where a rights issue is announced, Operations shall instruct the default set out in Schedule A. A decision to depart from the default, whether by allowing the rights to lapse or by selling the nil-paid rights, requires a documented rationale from the portfolio manager, held on the corporate actions file before the instruction is released.

**Tender offer**

Where a tender offer is received, Operations shall instruct the default set out in Schedule A. A decision to tender requires the approval of the Chief Investment Officer, recorded in writing before the instruction is released.

**Timing of elections**

Every election shall be instructed to the custodian no later than the deadline in Schedule A. Operations maintains the corporate actions diary, records the custodian deadline for each event on receipt of the custodian's notice, and derives the election deadline from it using Schedule A. Where the portfolio manager has not delivered a documented decision by the election deadline, Operations instructs the default set out in Schedule A and records that the default was applied.

**Historical practice**

For context only, in recent years the majority of scrip alternatives offered to the Fund have been taken in cash. That observation describes past elections and is not a rule; the binding default for scrip dividends is the one in Schedule A.

**Documentation**

Every departure from a default must be supported by a written rationale from the portfolio manager, filed with the custodian's notice and the instruction sent. Where an election requires the approval of the Chief Investment Officer, that approval must be on file before release. Operations reconciles every instruction against the custodian's confirmation and investigates any discrepancy immediately.

**Exceptions**

An exception to any provision of this Policy may be approved only by the portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer and Compliance acting together. Requests go to Compliance in writing, with the rationale and the intended route back to compliance, and are entered in the exceptions register. No exception may remain in force for longer than 30 days.

**Breaches**

Compliance will notify the portfolio manager and the Chief Investment Officer as soon as a breach is identified, including any missed election deadline and any election instructed without the required documentation. The portfolio manager, or Operations where the breach is operational, will propose a remediation plan, and Compliance will record the breach, its cause and its resolution in the breach log. Material breaches, and any breach that results in a loss to the Fund, are reported to the Board.

**Governance and records**

The Chief Investment Officer owns this Policy and the Board approves it. It is reviewed annually and whenever a change to the custodian arrangements, the prospectus or the regulatory status of the Fund affects any provision. Notices, rationales, approvals, instructions and confirmations are retained under the Manager's record-keeping policy.

**Schedule A: Defaults and deadline**

| Item | Event or matter | Default election or deadline |
| - | - | - |
| A1 | Scrip dividend | Taken in cash by default. |
| A2 | Rights issue | Taken up by default. |
| A3 | Tender offer | Declined by default. |
| A4 | Election deadline | Elections shall be instructed to the custodian no later than 2 business days before the custodian deadline. |

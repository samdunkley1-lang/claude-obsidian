# Midland Emerging Markets Fund: Investment Guidelines and Restrictions

Version 2, effective 2026-04-01

Consolidated text. This edition incorporates the amendment effective 2026-04-01, which changed the single security limit and updated the statement of position-sizing practice. For the convenience of readers reconciling older records, the version 1 wording of each amended provision is retained immediately after the current provision and is marked "superseded with effect from 2026-04-01". Only the current provisions are binding. A superseded provision has no continuing effect and must not be applied to any transaction or review dated on or after 2026-04-01.

## 1. Application

1.1. These Investment Guidelines and Restrictions (the "Guidelines") supplement the Investment Policy Statement of the Midland Emerging Markets Fund (the "Fund") and bind the Manager, the portfolio management team and every delegate acting for the Fund.

1.2. Where these Guidelines and the Investment Policy Statement address the same matter, the more restrictive provision applies.

1.3. Limits are tested on a post-trade basis: the existing exposure plus the change that a proposed transaction would produce. Passive movements arising from prices, corporate actions or investor dealing are not breaches in themselves, but the portfolio manager must restore compliance in an orderly manner once such a movement is identified.

1.4. Sector and country classifications follow the benchmark provider's methodology unless the Valuation Committee determines otherwise for a particular security.

## 2. Single security limit

2.1. No single security shall exceed 5% of net asset value, unless the security represents more than 4% of the benchmark, in which case the limit is the benchmark weight plus 2 percentage points.

2.2. Superseded text, retained for reference: "No single security shall exceed 6% of net asset value, unless the security represents more than 4% of the benchmark, in which case the limit is the benchmark weight plus 2 percentage points." That provision is superseded with effect from 2026-04-01 and is replaced by the provision immediately above.

2.3. The alternative limit in the current provision is available only while the benchmark condition is satisfied.

## 3. Single issuer limit

3.1. Aggregate exposure to any single issuer, including all securities of that issuer, shall not exceed 8% of net asset value.

3.2. Aggregation covers ordinary shares, preference shares, depositary receipts and any other instrument whose value derives from the issuer.

## 4. Sector limits

4.1. Exposure to the Technology sector shall not exceed 30% of net asset value.

4.2. Exposure to the Materials sector shall not exceed 20% of net asset value.

## 5. Country limits

5.1. Exposure to issuers domiciled in India shall not exceed 25% of net asset value.

5.2. Exposure to issuers domiciled in Brazil shall not exceed 15% of net asset value.

## 6. Frontier markets

6.1. Within the Frontier markets sleeve, no single security shall exceed 10% of net asset value. This limit applies in addition to the single security limit in clause 2.

6.2. Aggregate exposure to frontier markets shall not exceed 10% of net asset value.

## 7. Restricted List

7.1. No security of an issuer appearing on the Firm's Restricted List may be purchased or added to under any circumstances.

7.2. This restriction cannot be waived by the Manager, the Chief Investment Officer or Compliance, and no other individual or committee has authority to override it.

## 8. Statement of practice

8.1. For information, and not as a limit, the Manager's typical initial position size is approximately 2.5% of net asset value.

8.2. Superseded text, retained for reference: "The Manager's typical initial position size is approximately 3% of net asset value." That statement is superseded with effect from 2026-04-01 and is replaced by the statement immediately above. Neither the current nor the superseded statement imposes any restriction.

## 9. Exceptions

9.1. An exception to any limit in clauses 2 to 6 may be approved only by the portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer and Compliance, acting together.

9.2. A request for an exception must be in writing, must state the rationale and the intended route back to compliance, and must be recorded by Compliance in the exceptions register.

9.3. No exception may remain in force for longer than 30 days.

9.4. No exception is available in respect of clause 7.

## 10. Breach procedure

10.1. Compliance notifies the portfolio manager and the Chief Investment Officer as soon as a breach is identified.

10.2. The portfolio manager proposes a remediation plan, and Compliance records the breach, its cause and its resolution in the breach log.

10.3. Material breaches, and any breach that remains unresolved once its remediation plan has run its course, are reported to the Board.

## 11. Governance and records

11.1. These Guidelines are owned by the Chief Investment Officer and approved by the Board.

11.2. They are reviewed annually and whenever a change to the prospectus, the benchmark or the regulatory status of the Fund affects any limit above.

11.3. Records supporting the operation of these Guidelines are retained in accordance with the Manager's record-keeping policy.

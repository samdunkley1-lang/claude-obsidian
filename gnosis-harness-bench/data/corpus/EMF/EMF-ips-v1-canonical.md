# Midland Emerging Markets Fund: Investment Policy Statement

Version 1, effective 2025-01-01

## Purpose and scope

This Investment Policy Statement records the objectives, the permitted investment universe and the portfolio-level parameters that govern the management of the Midland Emerging Markets Fund (the "Fund"). It binds the Manager, the portfolio management team and every person acting on their behalf, including delegates and sub-advisers. Where this Statement and the Investment Guidelines and Restrictions address the same matter, the more restrictive provision applies.

## Objective and benchmark

- The Fund seeks long-term capital growth through a diversified portfolio of equity and equity-related securities issued by companies domiciled in, or deriving the greater part of their economic activity from, emerging market economies.
- Performance is measured against the benchmark named in the Fund's prospectus. Sector and country classifications follow the benchmark provider's methodology unless the Valuation Committee determines otherwise for a particular security.
- The Fund is actively managed. The benchmark serves as a reference for risk measurement and is not a constraint on security selection except where this Statement expressly says so.

## Cash and cash equivalents

- The Fund shall hold cash and cash equivalents of not less than 0% of net asset value. The Fund may not run a negative cash balance at the portfolio level.
- Cash and cash equivalents shall not exceed 8% of net asset value.
- In normal market conditions the Manager expects to hold cash of around 3% of net asset value. This is a statement of expectation offered for the information of investors; it is not a limit and no breach arises from departing from it.

## Diversification and portfolio activity

- The Fund shall hold a minimum of 45 distinct positions. Securities of the same issuer that carry different economic rights are counted as separate positions only where Compliance has confirmed the classification.
- Annual portfolio turnover shall not exceed 120% of average net assets.
- Historically the Fund's turnover has averaged approximately 72% per annum. That figure describes past experience, is provided for context, and does not operate as a restriction on the portfolio manager.

## Risk parameters

- Ex-ante tracking error against the benchmark shall not exceed 700 basis points, measured using the Manager's approved risk model.
- Exposure to the Financials sector shall not exceed 30% of net asset value.
- Exposure to issuers domiciled in China shall not exceed 30% of net asset value.

## Measurement

- Every limit expressed as a share of net asset value is tested on a post-trade basis, meaning the existing exposure plus the change that a proposed transaction would bring about.
- Passive movements caused by market prices, corporate actions or subscriptions and redemptions do not themselves constitute a breach, but the portfolio manager must restore compliance in an orderly manner once such a movement is identified.
- Turnover is calculated as the lesser of purchases and sales over a rolling year, divided by average net assets over the same period.

## Exceptions

- An exception to the tracking error limit may be approved only by the portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer and Risk, acting together.
- An exception to any other limit in this Statement may be approved only by the portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer and Compliance, acting together.
- Every exception must be requested in writing, must set out the rationale and the intended path back to compliance, and may not remain in force for longer than 30 days.
- Compliance maintains the register of exceptions and reports each one to the Board at its next meeting.

## Breach procedure

- Compliance notifies the portfolio manager and the Chief Investment Officer as soon as a breach is identified.
- The portfolio manager proposes a remediation plan, and Compliance records the breach, its cause and its resolution in the breach log.
- Material breaches, and any breach that remains unresolved after the remediation plan has run its course, are reported to the Board.

## Governance and review

- This Statement is owned by the Chief Investment Officer and approved by the Board.
- It is reviewed annually, and additionally whenever the Fund's prospectus, benchmark or regulatory status changes in a manner that affects any parameter above.
- Records supporting the operation of this Statement are retained in accordance with the Manager's record-keeping policy.

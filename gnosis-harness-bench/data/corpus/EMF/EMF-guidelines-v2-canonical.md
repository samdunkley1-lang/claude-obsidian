# Midland Emerging Markets Fund: Investment Guidelines and Restrictions

Version 2, effective 2026-04-01

**INTERNAL MEMORANDUM**

To: Portfolio Management, Compliance, Risk, Trading and Operations
From: Chief Investment Officer
Date: 2026-04-01
Subject: Investment Guidelines and Restrictions, version 2, in force from today

This memorandum circulates version 2 of the Investment Guidelines and Restrictions for the Midland Emerging Markets Fund (the "Fund") and replaces version 1 with immediate effect. The Board approved the amendment at its most recent meeting. The single security limit has been tightened; the remaining provisions are unchanged in substance. Please read the whole document, because the rules below are binding on every person named above and on any delegate acting for the Fund.

**How limits are measured**

Every limit is tested on a post-trade basis: the existing exposure plus the change a proposed transaction would produce. Passive movements from market prices, corporate actions or investor dealing are not breaches in themselves, but the portfolio manager must bring the portfolio back within the limit in an orderly manner once the movement is identified. Sector and country classification follows the benchmark provider unless the Valuation Committee has ruled otherwise for a particular security. Where these Guidelines and the Investment Policy Statement both address a matter, the more restrictive provision applies.

**Security and issuer concentration**

No single security shall exceed 5% of net asset value, unless the security represents more than 4% of the benchmark, in which case the limit is the benchmark weight plus 2 percentage points. The alternative limit is available only while the benchmark condition holds; when the benchmark weight falls to or below the condition, the headline limit applies again.

Aggregate exposure to any single issuer, including all securities of that issuer, shall not exceed 8% of net asset value. Aggregation captures ordinary shares, preference shares, depositary receipts and any other instrument whose value derives from the issuer.

Within the Frontier markets sleeve, no single security shall exceed 10% of net asset value. This sleeve limit sits alongside the whole-Fund single security limit and does not replace it.

**Sector limits**

Exposure to the Technology sector shall not exceed 30% of net asset value.

Exposure to the Materials sector shall not exceed 20% of net asset value.

**Country and regional limits**

Exposure to issuers domiciled in India shall not exceed 25% of net asset value.

Exposure to issuers domiciled in Brazil shall not exceed 15% of net asset value.

Aggregate exposure to frontier markets shall not exceed 10% of net asset value.

**Restricted List**

No security of an issuer appearing on the Firm's Restricted List may be purchased or added to under any circumstances. This restriction cannot be waived by the Manager, the Chief Investment Officer or Compliance, and no other individual or committee has any authority to override it. Trading must check the Restricted List before every order is released.

**A note on position sizing**

For context, and not as a rule, the Manager's typical initial position size is approximately 2.5% of net asset value. This reflects the way the portfolio has usually been built; it is neither a floor nor a ceiling, and a position sized differently is not a breach of anything in this memorandum.

**Exceptions**

An exception to any concentration, sector, country or sleeve limit above may be approved only by the portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer and Compliance acting together. Requests go to Compliance in writing, with the rationale and the intended route back to compliance, and are logged in the exceptions register. No exception may run for longer than 30 days. There is no exception route of any kind for the Restricted List.

**Breaches**

Compliance will notify the portfolio manager and me as soon as a breach is identified. The portfolio manager will propose a remediation plan, and Compliance will record the breach, its cause and its resolution in the breach log. Material breaches, and any breach that outlives its remediation plan, will be reported to the Board.

**Review and records**

I own these Guidelines and the Board approves them. They will be reviewed annually and whenever a change to the prospectus, the benchmark or the Fund's regulatory status affects any limit. Records supporting the operation of these Guidelines are retained under the Manager's record-keeping policy.

Please direct questions to Compliance in the first instance.

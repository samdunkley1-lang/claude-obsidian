# Midland Emerging Markets Fund: Investment Policy Statement

Version 2, effective 2026-04-01

This Investment Policy Statement describes how the Midland Emerging Markets Fund (the "Fund") is to be managed. It applies to the Manager, to the portfolio management team and to every delegate or sub-adviser acting for the Fund. Version 2 replaces the previous edition in full; the principal change is to the portfolio turnover parameter, and the remaining provisions have been carried forward with editorial refinements only. Where a matter is addressed both here and in the Investment Guidelines and Restrictions, the more restrictive provision governs.

The Fund's objective is long-term capital growth. It pursues that objective through a diversified portfolio of equity and equity-related securities issued by companies domiciled in, or deriving the greater part of their economic activity from, emerging market economies. Performance is assessed against the benchmark identified in the prospectus, and sector and country classifications follow the benchmark provider's methodology unless the Valuation Committee has determined otherwise for a particular security. The Fund is actively managed; the benchmark is a reference for measuring risk rather than a template for the portfolio.

Turning to cash, the Fund shall hold cash and cash equivalents of not less than 0% of net asset value, which means that a negative cash balance at portfolio level is not permitted. At the other end of the range, cash and cash equivalents shall not exceed 8% of net asset value. Within that band the Manager retains discretion. In normal market conditions the Manager expects to hold cash of around 3% of net asset value, although this is an expression of expectation for the benefit of investors rather than a restriction, and no breach arises if actual cash differs from it.

The Fund shall hold a minimum of 45 distinct positions. For this purpose, securities of the same issuer that carry different economic rights count as separate positions only where Compliance has confirmed that classification. Annual portfolio turnover shall not exceed 100% of average net assets, calculated as the lesser of purchases and sales over a rolling year divided by average net assets for the same period. Historically the Fund's turnover has averaged approximately 60% per annum; that figure is recorded here for context and is not a constraint on the portfolio manager.

The risk parameters are as follows. Ex-ante tracking error against the benchmark shall not exceed 700 basis points, as measured by the Manager's approved risk model. Exposure to the Financials sector shall not exceed 30% of net asset value. Exposure to issuers domiciled in China shall not exceed 30% of net asset value. Each of these parameters is measured on a post-trade basis, taking the existing exposure together with the change that a proposed transaction would produce.

Passive movements, whether arising from market prices, corporate actions or investor dealing, do not by themselves amount to a breach. Once such a movement is identified, however, the portfolio manager must bring the portfolio back within the relevant parameter in an orderly manner and without disadvantaging investors.

Exceptions are permitted but tightly controlled. An exception to the tracking error parameter may be approved only by the portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer and Risk acting together. An exception to any other parameter in this Statement may be approved only by the portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer and Compliance acting together. Every exception must be sought in writing with a rationale and an intended route back to compliance, and no exception may remain in force for more than 30 days. Compliance keeps the register of exceptions and reports each one to the Board at its next meeting.

When a breach is identified, Compliance informs the portfolio manager and the Chief Investment Officer without delay. The portfolio manager then proposes a remediation plan, and Compliance records the breach, its cause and its eventual resolution in the breach log. Material breaches, together with any breach that outlives its remediation plan, are escalated to the Board.

This Statement is owned by the Chief Investment Officer and approved by the Board. It is reviewed annually and whenever a change to the prospectus, the benchmark or the Fund's regulatory status bears on any parameter described above. Supporting records are retained under the Manager's record-keeping policy.

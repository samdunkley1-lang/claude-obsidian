# Midland Emerging Markets Fund: Investment Policy Statement

Version 2, effective 2026-04-01

Consolidated text. This edition incorporates the amendment effective 2026-04-01, which changed the turnover provisions of clause 4. For the convenience of readers reconciling older records, the version 1 wording of each amended clause is retained immediately after the current clause and is marked "superseded with effect from 2026-04-01". Only the current clauses are binding; a superseded clause has no continuing effect and must not be applied to any transaction or review dated on or after 2026-04-01.

## 1. Purpose and application

1.1. This Investment Policy Statement sets out the objectives and portfolio-level parameters governing the Midland Emerging Markets Fund (the "Fund").

1.2. It applies to the Manager, the portfolio management team and every delegate or sub-adviser acting for the Fund.

1.3. Where this Statement and the Investment Guidelines and Restrictions deal with the same matter, the more restrictive provision applies.

## 2. Objective and benchmark

2.1. The Fund seeks long-term capital growth through a diversified portfolio of equity and equity-related securities of companies domiciled in, or deriving the greater part of their economic activity from, emerging market economies.

2.2. Performance is measured against the benchmark named in the prospectus. The Fund is actively managed and the benchmark is a reference for risk measurement rather than a template for construction.

2.3. Sector and country classifications follow the benchmark provider's methodology unless the Valuation Committee determines otherwise for a particular security.

## 3. Cash and cash equivalents

3.1. The Fund shall hold cash and cash equivalents of not less than 0% of net asset value.

3.2. Cash and cash equivalents shall not exceed 8% of net asset value.

3.3. In normal market conditions the Manager expects to hold cash of around 3% of net asset value. This is an expectation only and is not a limit.

## 4. Diversification and portfolio activity

4.1. The Fund shall hold a minimum of 45 distinct positions.

4.2. Annual portfolio turnover shall not exceed 100% of average net assets.

4.3. Superseded text, retained for reference: "Annual portfolio turnover shall not exceed 120% of average net assets." That clause is superseded with effect from 2026-04-01 and is replaced by the clause immediately above.

4.4. Historically the Fund's turnover has averaged approximately 60% per annum. This is a statement of past experience and does not operate as a restriction.

4.5. Superseded text, retained for reference: "Historically the Fund's turnover has averaged approximately 72% per annum." That statement is superseded with effect from 2026-04-01 and is replaced by the statement immediately above.

## 5. Risk parameters

5.1. Ex-ante tracking error against the benchmark shall not exceed 700 basis points, measured using the Manager's approved risk model.

5.2. Exposure to the Financials sector shall not exceed 30% of net asset value.

5.3. Exposure to issuers domiciled in China shall not exceed 30% of net asset value.

## 6. Measurement

6.1. Limits expressed as a share of net asset value are tested on a post-trade basis: the existing exposure plus the change a proposed transaction would produce.

6.2. Passive movements arising from market prices, corporate actions or investor dealing do not of themselves constitute a breach, but the portfolio manager must restore compliance in an orderly manner once such a movement is identified.

6.3. Turnover is calculated as the lesser of purchases and sales over a rolling year, divided by average net assets over the same period.

## 7. Exceptions

7.1. An exception to the tracking error limit in clause 5 may be approved only by the portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer and Risk, acting together.

7.2. An exception to any other limit in this Statement may be approved only by the portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer and Compliance, acting together.

7.3. Every exception must be requested in writing with a rationale and an intended route back to compliance, and may not remain in force for longer than 30 days.

7.4. Compliance maintains the exceptions register and reports each exception to the Board at its next meeting.

## 8. Breach procedure

8.1. Compliance notifies the portfolio manager and the Chief Investment Officer as soon as a breach is identified.

8.2. The portfolio manager proposes a remediation plan, and Compliance records the breach, its cause and its resolution in the breach log.

8.3. Material breaches, and any breach unresolved at the end of its remediation plan, are reported to the Board.

## 9. Governance and review

9.1. This Statement is owned by the Chief Investment Officer and approved by the Board.

9.2. It is reviewed annually and whenever a change to the prospectus, benchmark or regulatory status of the Fund affects any parameter above.

9.3. Supporting records are retained in accordance with the Manager's record-keeping policy.

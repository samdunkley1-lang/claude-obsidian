# Midland Emerging Markets Fund: Investment Guidelines and Restrictions

Version 2, effective 2026-04-01

## 1. Application

1.1. These Investment Guidelines and Restrictions (the "Guidelines") apply to the Midland Emerging Markets Fund (the "Fund") and supplement its Investment Policy Statement. They bind the Manager, the portfolio management team and every delegate acting for the Fund.

1.2. Nothing in these Guidelines shall be read as relaxing any provision of the Investment Policy Statement; where the two differ, the provision that is not the less restrictive shall apply.

1.3. Limits are tested on a post-trade basis, being the existing exposure together with the change that a proposed transaction would produce. A passive movement arising from prices, corporate actions or investor dealing is not of itself a breach, but it is not permissible for the portfolio manager to leave such a movement unremedied once it has been identified.

1.4. Sector and country classifications are those of the benchmark provider unless the Valuation Committee has determined otherwise for a particular security.

## 2. Single security limit

2.1. It is not the case that any single security may exceed 5% of net asset value; a security shall in no case be permitted to exceed that proportion, unless the security represents more than 4% of the benchmark, in which case the limit is the benchmark weight plus 2 percentage points and the position may not exceed that alternative figure.

2.2. The alternative limit is not available at any time when the benchmark condition is not satisfied.

## 3. Single issuer limit

3.1. Aggregate exposure to any single issuer, including all securities of that issuer, shall in no case be permitted to exceed 8% of net asset value; no combination of holdings in an issuer may be such that the total is not within that limit.

3.2. No security whose value derives from the issuer, whether ordinary share, preference share, depositary receipt or otherwise, is excluded from the aggregation.

## 4. Sector limits

4.1. Exposure to the Technology sector may not, in any circumstances, be permitted to exceed 30% of net asset value, nor may any transaction be executed whose effect would be that it does.

4.2. It is not permitted for exposure to the Materials sector to exceed 20% of net asset value, and no order shall be released where the post-trade exposure would not be within that limit.

## 5. Country limits

5.1. Exposure to issuers domiciled in India shall not at any time be permitted to exceed 25% of net asset value; it is not the case that any transaction may take such exposure above that level.

5.2. Exposure to issuers domiciled in Brazil shall in no case be permitted to exceed 15% of net asset value, nor shall any transaction be entered into whose result would be exposure that is not within that limit.

## 6. Frontier markets

6.1. Within the Frontier markets sleeve, no single security shall exceed 10% of net asset value. This limit applies in addition to the single security limit in clause 2.

6.2. Aggregate exposure to frontier markets shall not exceed 10% of net asset value.

## 7. Restricted List

7.1. No security of an issuer appearing on the Firm's Restricted List may be purchased or added to under any circumstances.

7.2. This restriction cannot be waived by the Manager, the Chief Investment Officer or Compliance, and no other individual or committee has authority to override it.

## 8. Statement of practice

8.1. For information, and without imposing any limit, floor or expectation of conduct, the Manager's typical initial position size is approximately 2.5% of net asset value. A position initiated at a different size is not a breach of these Guidelines.

## 9. Exceptions

9.1. An exception to any limit in clauses 2 to 6 may be approved only by the portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer and Compliance, acting together, and by no one else.

9.2. A request for an exception must be in writing, must state the rationale and the intended route back to compliance, and must be recorded by Compliance in the exceptions register.

9.3. No exception may remain in force for longer than 30 days.

9.4. No exception is available in respect of clause 7.

## 10. Breach procedure

10.1. Compliance notifies the portfolio manager and the Chief Investment Officer as soon as a breach is identified.

10.2. The portfolio manager proposes a remediation plan, and Compliance records the breach, its cause and its resolution in the breach log.

10.3. Material breaches, and any breach that remains unresolved once its remediation plan has run its course, are reported to the Board.

## 11. Governance and records

11.1. These Guidelines are owned by the Chief Investment Officer and approved by the Board.

11.2. They are reviewed annually and whenever a change to the prospectus, the benchmark or the regulatory status of the Fund affects any limit above.

11.3. Records supporting the operation of these Guidelines are retained in accordance with the Manager's record-keeping policy.

# Midland Emerging Markets Fund: Investment Guidelines and Restrictions

Version 1, effective 2025-01-01

## Preamble

These Investment Guidelines and Restrictions (the "Guidelines") supplement the Investment Policy Statement of the Midland Emerging Markets Fund (the "Fund"). They set out the concentration limits, sleeve limits and absolute restrictions that apply to the portfolio, and they bind the Manager, the portfolio management team and every delegate acting for the Fund. Where a matter is covered both here and in the Investment Policy Statement, the more restrictive provision applies.

The limits below are tested on a post-trade basis. A proposed transaction is assessed by adding the change it would cause to the existing exposure and comparing the result with the relevant threshold. Passive movements in exposure, whether from market prices, corporate actions or investor dealing, are not themselves breaches, but the portfolio manager must restore compliance in an orderly fashion once such a movement has been identified. Sector and country classifications follow the benchmark provider's methodology unless the Valuation Committee has determined otherwise for a specific security.

Each table below states the binding limit in the "Rule" column. The "Notes" column carries explanatory material only; nothing in that column creates, relaxes or extends a limit.

## Table 1: Security and issuer concentration

| Ref | Scope | Rule | Notes |
| - | - | - | - |
| A | Any single security, whole Fund | No single security shall exceed 6% of net asset value, unless the security represents more than 4% of the benchmark, in which case the limit is the benchmark weight plus 2 percentage points. | The alternative limit applies only while the benchmark condition is satisfied; once the benchmark weight falls to or below the condition, the headline limit applies again. |
| B | Any single issuer, whole Fund | Aggregate exposure to any single issuer, including all securities of that issuer, shall not exceed 8% of net asset value. | Aggregation covers ordinary shares, preference shares, depositary receipts and any other instrument whose value derives from the issuer. |
| C | Any single security, Frontier markets sleeve | Within the Frontier markets sleeve, no single security shall exceed 10% of net asset value. | Applies in addition to, and not in substitution for, the whole-Fund limit at Ref A. |

## Exceptions

An exception to any limit in Tables 1 to 3 may be approved only by the portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer and Compliance, acting together. The request must be made in writing, must set out the reason for the exception and the intended route back to compliance, and must be entered by Compliance in the exceptions register. No exception may remain in force for longer than 30 days. No exception of any kind is available in respect of the restriction in Table 4.

## Breach procedure

Compliance notifies the portfolio manager and the Chief Investment Officer as soon as a breach is identified. The portfolio manager proposes a remediation plan, and Compliance records the breach, its cause and its resolution in the breach log. Material breaches, and any breach that remains unresolved once its remediation plan has run its course, are reported to the Board.

## Governance, review and records

These Guidelines are owned by the Chief Investment Officer and approved by the Board. They are reviewed annually and whenever a change to the prospectus, the benchmark or the regulatory status of the Fund affects any limit above. Records supporting the operation of these Guidelines are retained in accordance with the Manager's record-keeping policy.

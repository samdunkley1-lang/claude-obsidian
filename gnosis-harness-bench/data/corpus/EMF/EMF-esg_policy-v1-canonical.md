# Midland Emerging Markets Fund: Responsible Investment Exclusions Policy

Version 1, effective 2025-01-01

## Purpose and scope

This Responsible Investment Exclusions Policy (the "Policy") sets out the categories of issuer that the Midland Emerging Markets Fund (the "Fund") may not hold, together with the Manager's aspirations on climate-related matters. It applies to the Manager, the portfolio management team and any delegate acting for the Fund, and it operates alongside the Investment Policy Statement and the Investment Guidelines and Restrictions. Where those documents and this Policy address the same matter, the more restrictive provision applies.

## Definitions

- "Issuer" means any company, or any entity controlled by that company, whose securities the Fund might hold directly or through a depositary receipt.
- "Excluded" means that no security of the issuer may be purchased, and that any existing holding must be disposed of in an orderly manner once the issuer has been assessed as falling within the relevant category.
- "Assessed" means determined by the Manager's responsible investment function on the basis of the screening data providers approved by the Board, supplemented by the Manager's own research where the data are incomplete.

## Absolute exclusion

- Issuers involved in the production of controversial weapons (cluster munitions, anti-personnel mines, biological and chemical weapons) are excluded absolutely.
- No exception to this exclusion may be granted by any person. Neither the portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer, Compliance, Risk, the Valuation Committee nor the Board has authority to waive or override it.
- The controversial weapons screen is applied before any purchase is released and is re-run whenever the screening data are refreshed.

## Activity-based exclusion

- Issuers deriving more than 30% of revenue from thermal coal extraction or thermal coal power generation are excluded.
- Revenue attribution follows the most recent audited financial statements as interpreted by the approved screening provider. Where an issuer's revenue split is not disclosed in sufficient detail, the responsible investment function must make a documented determination before the issuer is treated as eligible.

## Conduct-based exclusion

- UN Global Compact violators are excluded: any issuer assessed as being in breach of the UN Global Compact principles on human rights, labour, environment and anti-corruption may not be held.
- An issuer flagged for potential breach but not yet assessed as a violator may be held while the assessment is in progress, subject to a note in the responsible investment log.

## Climate aspiration

- The Manager aims to reduce the portfolio's weighted average carbon intensity by 30% relative to the benchmark over time.
- This is an aspiration, not a limit. It does not create a threshold against which transactions are tested, and no breach arises from the portfolio's carbon intensity at any point in time. Progress is reported to the Board for information.

## Exceptions

- No exception is available in respect of the absolute exclusion of controversial weapons.
- An exception to the thermal coal exclusion or the UN Global Compact exclusion may be approved only by the portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer and Compliance, acting together.
- A request must be in writing, must explain the rationale and the intended route back to compliance, and must be recorded by Compliance in the exceptions register.
- No exception may remain in force for longer than 30 days.

## Breach procedure

- Compliance notifies the portfolio manager and the Chief Investment Officer as soon as a breach is identified.
- The portfolio manager proposes a remediation plan for the orderly disposal of the affected holding, and Compliance records the breach, its cause and its resolution in the breach log.
- Any holding of a controversial weapons issuer is escalated to the Board immediately; other material breaches are reported to the Board at its next meeting.

## Governance, review and records

- This Policy is owned by the Chief Investment Officer and approved by the Board.
- It is reviewed annually and whenever the approved screening providers, the Fund's prospectus or applicable regulation change in a manner that affects any provision above.
- Screening outputs, determinations of the responsible investment function and exception records are retained in accordance with the Manager's record-keeping policy.

# Midland Emerging Markets Fund: Valuation and Pricing Policy

Version 1, effective 2025-01-01

**INTERNAL MEMORANDUM**

To: Fund Accounting, Portfolio Management, Compliance, Risk and the Valuation Committee
From: Chair of the Valuation Committee
Date: 2025-01-01
Subject: Valuation and Pricing Policy for the Midland Emerging Markets Fund

This memorandum sets out the Valuation and Pricing Policy (the "Policy") for the Midland Emerging Markets Fund (the "Fund"). Its purpose is to ensure that every position is carried at a fair, consistent and independently verifiable value, so that the net asset value struck on each dealing day treats subscribing, redeeming and continuing investors alike. The Policy binds Fund Accounting, the portfolio management team, the Fund administrator and any delegate involved in pricing the Fund. It operates alongside the Investment Policy Statement and the Liquidity Risk Management Policy.

**Pricing hierarchy**

Positions are valued using the pricing sources approved by the Valuation Committee, in the order of preference set out in the approved source list. Where a primary source is unavailable, Fund Accounting moves to the next approved source and records the substitution. Where no approved source produces a usable price, the position is escalated to the Valuation Committee under the fair value procedure described below.

**Rule on odd lot positions**

Positions held in odd lots shall be valued using odd-lot prices. Institutional round-lot prices shall not be applied to odd-lot positions. Fund Accounting identifies each odd lot position from the custodian's holdings file and confirms that the price applied reflects the odd-lot market rather than the round-lot market. Any position for which an odd-lot price is not available from an approved source is escalated to the Valuation Committee.

**Rule on level 3 assets**

Any mark applied to a level 3 asset requires prior approval of the Valuation Committee. A level 3 asset is one whose fair value depends on inputs that are not observable in an active market. Fund Accounting may not release a net asset value that incorporates a new or revised level 3 mark unless the Valuation Committee's approval has been minuted in advance.

**Rule on stale prices**

A stale price is a price that has not been updated for more than 5 business days. Such a price is deemed stale and must be referred to the Valuation Committee before use. Fund Accounting runs the stale price report on every dealing day, and a stale price may not be carried forward into the net asset value without the Committee's written decision on whether to retain, adjust or replace it.

**Review frequency of pricing sources**

For information, the Valuation Committee's review frequency for its pricing sources is approximately every 90 days. This statement describes the Committee's working practice and is not a requirement; a review falling earlier or later than that interval does not constitute a breach of this Policy.

**Exceptions**

An exception to any rule in this memorandum may be approved only by the portfolio manager and the Valuation Committee acting together. A request must be made in writing, must set out the rationale and the proposed alternative valuation approach, and must be recorded by Compliance in the exceptions register. Every exception is reported to the Board at its next meeting.

**Breach procedure**

Compliance notifies the portfolio manager and the Chair of the Valuation Committee as soon as a breach is identified. Fund Accounting determines whether any net asset value already struck requires correction, and Compliance records the breach, its cause and its resolution in the breach log. A breach that has affected a published net asset value is reported to the Board and, where required, to the Fund's regulator.

**Governance, review and records**

This Policy is owned by the Valuation Committee and approved by the Board. It is reviewed annually and whenever the approved pricing sources, the Fund's prospectus or applicable accounting standards change in a manner that affects any provision above. Pricing source records, Committee minutes, stale price reports, odd lot confirmations and exception records are retained in accordance with the Manager's record-keeping policy.

Please direct questions to the secretary of the Valuation Committee.

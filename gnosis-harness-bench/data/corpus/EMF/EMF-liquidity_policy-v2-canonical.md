# Midland Emerging Markets Fund: Liquidity Risk Management Policy

Version 2, effective 2026-04-01

## Preamble

This Liquidity Risk Management Policy (the "Policy") governs how the Midland Emerging Markets Fund (the "Fund") manages the risk of being unable to meet redemptions or to rebalance its portfolio without disadvantaging investors. Version 2 replaces version 1 in full. The Board approved a strengthening of the portfolio-level liquidity floor; the other requirements are unchanged in substance. The Policy binds the Manager, the portfolio management team, Risk and any delegate acting for the Fund, and it sits alongside the Investment Policy Statement and the Investment Guidelines and Restrictions. Where those documents and this Policy address the same matter, the more restrictive provision applies.

A holding is classified as illiquid where Risk has determined, using the Manager's approved liquidity model and its own judgement, that the holding could not be realised at or near its carrying value within a reasonable period under normal market conditions. A holding is treated as realisable within a stated number of business days where the model indicates that the entire position could be sold within that period at a participation rate that would not be expected to move the market price materially. Classifications are refreshed whenever model inputs are updated and whenever Risk considers that market conditions have changed.

Every limit in the tables below is tested on a post-trade basis, meaning the existing exposure plus the change a proposed transaction would produce. A limit that is crossed through reclassification or market movement rather than through a transaction is not a breach at the moment it is crossed, but the portfolio manager must restore compliance in an orderly manner once Risk has notified the change.

| Ref | Requirement | Binding rule | Measured by |

## Exceptions

An exception to any requirement in Tables 1 to 3 may be approved only by the portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer and Risk acting together. The request must be in writing, must set out the rationale and the intended route back to compliance, and must be entered by Compliance in the exceptions register. No exception may remain in force for longer than 30 days.

## Breach procedure

Compliance notifies the portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer and Risk as soon as a breach is identified. The portfolio manager proposes a remediation plan, and Compliance records the breach, its cause and its resolution in the breach log. Material breaches, and any breach that remains unresolved once its remediation plan has run its course, are reported to the Board.

## Governance, review and records

This Policy is owned by the Chief Investment Officer, operated day to day by Risk and approved by the Board. It is reviewed annually and whenever a change to the prospectus, the dealing arrangements or the regulatory status of the Fund affects any requirement above. Liquidity model outputs, classification decisions, swing pricing calculations and exception records are retained in accordance with the Manager's record-keeping policy.

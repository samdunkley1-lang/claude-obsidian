# Midland Emerging Markets Fund: Responsible Investment Exclusions Policy

Version 1, effective 2025-01-01

**INTERNAL MEMORANDUM**

To: Portfolio Management, Research, Trading, Compliance and Risk
From: Head of Responsible Investment, on behalf of the Chief Investment Officer
Date: 2025-01-01
Subject: Exclusions applying to the Midland Emerging Markets Fund

This memorandum restates, in plain terms, the exclusion rules that apply to the Midland Emerging Markets Fund (the "Fund") and the climate aspiration that sits alongside them. The rules are binding on everyone in the addressee groups and on any delegate acting for the Fund. They operate together with the Investment Policy Statement and the Investment Guidelines and Restrictions, and where those documents and this memorandum cover the same ground the stricter provision wins.

**What "excluded" means**

When an issuer is excluded, no security of that issuer may be bought, and any holding already in the portfolio must be sold down in an orderly way once the issuer has been assessed as falling within the excluded category. Assessment is carried out by the responsible investment function using the screening providers approved by the Board, supplemented by our own research where the data are patchy. The word "issuer" covers the company itself and any entity it controls, whether we hold the shares directly or through a depositary receipt.

**Rule 1: controversial weapons, without exception**

Any issuer involved in producing controversial weapons is excluded outright. For this purpose controversial weapons means cluster munitions, anti-personnel mines, and biological and chemical weapons. There is no exception route. No person can grant a waiver: not the portfolio manager, not the Chief Investment Officer, not Compliance, not Risk, not the Valuation Committee and not the Board. The screen runs before every purchase is released and again each time the screening data are refreshed.

**Rule 2: thermal coal**

An issuer is excluded where thermal coal extraction and thermal coal power generation, taken together, generate more than 30% of its revenue. Revenue is attributed on the basis of the latest audited accounts as read by the approved screening provider. If the revenue split is not disclosed in enough detail to apply the test, the responsible investment function must record a reasoned determination before the issuer can be treated as eligible.

**Rule 3: UN Global Compact violators**

Issuers assessed as UN Global Compact violators, meaning those found to be in breach of the UN Global Compact principles covering human rights, labour standards, the environment and anti-corruption, are excluded. An issuer that has merely been flagged for possible breach, and has not yet been assessed, may continue to be held while the assessment runs, provided a note is entered in the responsible investment log.

**Climate aspiration, which is not a rule**

Separately from the exclusions, the Manager aims to reduce the portfolio's weighted average carbon intensity by 30% relative to the benchmark over time. Please treat this as exactly what it says: an aim. It sets no threshold against which trades are checked, and the portfolio's carbon intensity on any given day cannot give rise to a breach. We report progress to the Board for information.

**Exceptions**

Rule 1 admits no exception of any kind. For Rule 2 and Rule 3, an exception may be granted only by the portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer and Compliance acting together. Requests go to Compliance in writing, with the rationale and the intended route back to compliance, and are entered in the exceptions register. An exception lapses after 30 days at the latest.

**If something goes wrong**

Compliance will tell the portfolio manager and the Chief Investment Officer as soon as a breach comes to light. The portfolio manager will propose a plan for selling down the affected holding in an orderly way, and Compliance will log the breach, its cause and its resolution. Any holding in a controversial weapons issuer goes to the Board immediately; other material breaches are reported at the Board's next meeting.

**Ownership and review**

The Chief Investment Officer owns this Policy and the Board approves it. It is reviewed annually and whenever the approved screening providers, the prospectus or applicable regulation change in a way that affects any of the rules above. Screening outputs, determinations and exception records are retained under the Manager's record-keeping policy.

Questions should be directed to the responsible investment function in the first instance.

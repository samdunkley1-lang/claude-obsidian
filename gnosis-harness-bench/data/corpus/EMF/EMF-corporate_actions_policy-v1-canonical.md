# Midland Emerging Markets Fund: Corporate Actions Election Policy

Version 1, effective 2025-01-01

## Purpose and scope

This Corporate Actions Election Policy (the "Policy") sets out how elections on voluntary corporate actions are to be made for the Midland Emerging Markets Fund (the "Fund"). It applies to the Manager, the portfolio management team, Operations, the Fund administrator and any delegate involved in processing corporate actions. The Policy operates alongside the Investment Policy Statement and the Investment Guidelines and Restrictions, and where those documents and this Policy address the same matter the more restrictive provision applies.

## Definitions

- "Voluntary corporate action" means any event on which the holder is offered a choice, including a scrip dividend, a rights issue and a tender offer.
- "Default" means the election that Operations must instruct where no documented decision to the contrary has been made in accordance with this Policy.
- "Custodian deadline" means the latest time notified by the custodian for receipt of the Fund's instruction on an event.
- "Election deadline" means the internal deadline set by this Policy for delivery of the Fund's instruction to the custodian.

## Default elections

- Scrip dividend: scrip dividend alternatives shall be taken in cash by default. A scrip election requires a documented rationale from the portfolio manager and is prohibited where the post-election position would exceed the single security limit in the Investment Guidelines.
- Rights issue: rights issues shall be taken up by default. A decision to let rights lapse or to sell nil-paid rights requires a documented rationale from the portfolio manager.
- Tender offer: tender offers shall be declined by default. Tendering requires approval of the Chief Investment Officer.

## Election deadline

- Elections shall be instructed to the custodian no later than 2 business days before the custodian deadline.
- Operations maintains the corporate actions diary, records the custodian deadline for every event on receipt of the custodian's notice, and calculates the election deadline from it.
- Where the portfolio manager has not delivered a documented decision by the election deadline, Operations instructs the default election and records that the default was applied.

## Historical practice

- In recent years the majority of scrip alternatives offered to the Fund have been taken in cash. This is an observation about past elections and is not a rule; the binding default for scrip dividends is stated above.

## Documentation

- Every departure from a default must be supported by a written rationale from the portfolio manager, held on the corporate actions file together with the custodian's notice and the instruction sent.
- Where an election requires the approval of the Chief Investment Officer, that approval must be recorded in writing before the instruction is released.
- Operations reconciles every instruction against the custodian's confirmation and investigates any discrepancy immediately.

## Exceptions

- An exception to any provision of this Policy may be approved only by the portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer and Compliance, acting together.
- A request must be in writing, must set out the rationale and the intended route back to compliance, and must be recorded by Compliance in the exceptions register.
- No exception may remain in force for longer than 30 days.

## Breach procedure

- Compliance notifies the portfolio manager and the Chief Investment Officer as soon as a breach is identified, including any missed election deadline and any election instructed without the required documentation.
- The portfolio manager, or Operations where the breach is operational in nature, proposes a remediation plan, and Compliance records the breach, its cause and its resolution in the breach log.
- Material breaches, and any breach that results in a loss to the Fund, are reported to the Board.

## Governance, review and records

- This Policy is owned by the Chief Investment Officer and approved by the Board.
- It is reviewed annually and whenever a change to the custodian arrangements, the prospectus or the regulatory status of the Fund affects any provision above.
- Corporate action notices, rationales, approvals, instructions and confirmations are retained in accordance with the Manager's record-keeping policy.

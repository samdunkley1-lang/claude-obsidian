# Midland Diversified Growth Fund: Corporate Actions Election Policy

Version 2, effective 2026-04-01

## Purpose and status

This Policy sets out how Midland Asset Management Limited (the "Manager") makes elections on corporate actions affecting securities held by the Midland Diversified Growth Fund (the "Fund"). Version 2 is a restatement of the previous version for clarity; no default, deadline or approval requirement has changed. The Policy has been approved by the Board of the Fund and is binding on the Manager, its operations team and any agent instructing the custodian on the Fund's behalf.

## Scope

A corporate action for the purposes of this Policy is any issuer-initiated or third-party event that requires or permits the Fund to make a choice. The custodian notifies each event through the corporate actions platform, and the operations team logs the event, the applicable default, any departure from the default and the final instruction. The defaults in the tables below are binding rules; an instruction that departs from a default without the required documentation or approval is a breach.

## Default elections

Each row of the table is a binding rule. The default applies unless the conditions in the "Departure from default" column are satisfied and documented before the instruction is given.

| Ref | Event type | Default election | Departure from default |
| - | - | - | - |
| 1 | Scrip dividend alternative | The alternative shall be taken in cash | A scrip election requires a documented rationale from the portfolio manager, and is prohibited where the post-election position would exceed the single security limit in the Investment Guidelines |
| 2 | Rights issue | Rights shall be taken up | A decision to let rights lapse or to sell nil-paid rights requires a documented rationale from the portfolio manager |
| 3 | Tender offer | The offer shall be declined | Tendering, in whole or in part, requires approval of the Chief Investment Officer |

Before releasing any scrip instruction the operations team must confirm the projected post-election position against the single security limit in the Investment Guidelines and retain the confirmation with the election record.

## Instruction timing

| Ref | Requirement | Deadline |
| - | - | - |
| 4 | Election deadline: elections shall be instructed to the custodian no later than the stated period before the custodian deadline | 2 business days before the custodian deadline |

The operations team tracks the custodian deadline for every event, obtains the portfolio manager's decision in good time and confirms that the custodian has acknowledged the instruction. An instruction released after the internal deadline is a breach of this Policy even where the custodian accepts it; a missed instruction results in the custodian applying its own default, and the consequences are reported to the Board.

## Descriptive note

The following statement is provided for context and is not a rule: in recent years the majority of scrip alternatives offered to the Fund have been taken in cash. It describes the outcome of past elections and does not alter the default or the departure conditions in row 1.

## Exceptions

An exception to any default in rows 1 to 3, or to the election deadline in row 4, may be granted where the circumstances of a specific event make the standard process inappropriate. An exception requires the joint written approval of the portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer and Compliance, must record the reasons for it, and lapses 30 days after approval unless renewed through the same process. No exception may be granted retrospectively to cure an instruction that has already been given in breach of this Policy.

## Monitoring and breach handling

Compliance reviews the corporate actions log on each business day for events approaching the custodian deadline without an instruction, for departures from a default lacking the required documentation or approval, and for instructions released late. Each finding is reported to the portfolio manager and the Chief Investment Officer on the day of identification and entered in the breach log. Where the Fund has suffered a loss, or where the same failing recurs, the matter is escalated to the Board with the remedial action taken. Notifications, decisions, approvals and instructions are retained in accordance with the Manager's record keeping policy.

## Review

The Board reviews this Policy periodically and whenever the custodian's processing arrangements or the Fund's asset mix change materially. Amendments take effect on the date stated by the Board and supersede all earlier versions.

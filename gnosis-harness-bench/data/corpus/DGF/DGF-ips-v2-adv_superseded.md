# Midland Diversified Growth Fund: Investment Policy Statement

Version 2, effective 2026-04-01

Consolidated text incorporating the amendment effective 2026-04-01. Clauses marked "superseded with effect from 2026-04-01" reproduce the wording of Version 1 for reference only and have no continuing effect; the clause immediately preceding each superseded clause states the rule currently in force.

## 1. Interpretation and application

1.1. This Statement is adopted by the Board of the Midland Diversified Growth Fund (the "Fund") and binds Midland Asset Management Limited (the "Manager") as investment manager.

1.2. References to a percentage are to a percentage of the net asset value of the Fund, measured immediately after giving effect to the proposed transaction.

1.3. Where a clause is expressed as a blocking limit, a transaction that would result in the limit being exceeded may not be executed. Where a clause is expressed as a monitored limit, an excess gives rise to a formal warning and a hold on further deterioration but does not of itself prevent execution.

## 2. Objective

2.1. The Fund seeks long-term capital growth through a diversified multi-asset portfolio of global equities, sovereign bonds, corporate credit and cash, measured against the composite benchmark described in the Prospectus.

## 3. Liquidity and diversification

3.1. The Fund shall hold cash and cash equivalents of not less than 2% of net asset value. This is a blocking limit.

3.2. Cash and cash equivalents shall not exceed 25% of net asset value. This is a blocking limit.

3.3. The Fund shall hold a minimum of 60 distinct positions. This is a monitored limit.

3.4. Annual portfolio turnover shall not exceed 200% of average net assets. This is a monitored limit.

## 4. Active risk, sector, country and currency

4.1. Ex-ante tracking error against the benchmark shall not exceed 600 basis points. This is a blocking limit.

4.2. Exposure to the Technology sector shall not exceed 20% of net asset value. This is a blocking limit.

4.3. Exposure to issuers domiciled in United States shall not exceed 50% of net asset value. This is a blocking limit.

4.4. Unhedged non-base-currency exposure shall not exceed 30% of net asset value. This is a blocking limit.

## 5. Fixed income

5.1. Portfolio modified duration shall be maintained between 2 and 7 years. This is a blocking limit and is the clause in force.

5.2. Superseded with effect from 2026-04-01: "Portfolio modified duration shall be maintained between 2 and 8 years." This former clause is reproduced from Version 1 for the record and confers no authority to operate outside the band stated in the preceding clause.

5.3. Holdings rated below investment grade shall not exceed 12% of net asset value. This is a blocking limit and is the clause in force.

5.4. Superseded with effect from 2026-04-01: "Holdings rated below investment grade shall not exceed 15% of net asset value." This former clause is reproduced from Version 1 for the record and confers no authority to hold below investment grade securities in excess of the ceiling stated in the preceding clause.

5.5. No security rated below B (or equivalent) may be purchased. This is a blocking limit.

## 6. Statements of practice

6.1. The following statements describe practice and history. They are not limits, are not monitored as limits and are incapable of being breached.

6.2. In normal market conditions the Manager expects to hold cash of around 3% of net asset value.

6.3. Historically the Fund's turnover has averaged approximately 120% per annum.

## 7. Exceptions

7.1. An exception to any limit in sections 3, 4 or 5, other than the tracking error, unhedged currency and duration limits, requires the joint written approval of the portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer and Compliance.

7.2. An exception to the tracking error, unhedged currency or duration limit requires the joint written approval of the portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer and Risk.

7.3. Every exception shall be documented with its rationale and expected duration and shall lapse 30 days after approval unless renewed through the same approval process. No exception may be granted in respect of a superseded clause, which has no operative content.

## 8. Monitoring, breaches and review

8.1. Compliance shall test each limit on every business day against the official valuation and report any excess to the portfolio manager and the Chief Investment Officer without delay.

8.2. A breach arising from market movement shall be remedied in an orderly manner having regard to the interests of investors; a breach arising from a transaction shall be escalated to the Board with an analysis of cause and remedy.

8.3. The Board shall review this Statement periodically and on any material change to the Fund's objective, benchmark or investor base. Amendments take effect from the date stated in the amending resolution and supersede all earlier text, and the superseded clauses retained above are included solely so that the history of the limits can be traced.

# Midland Diversified Growth Fund: Investment Policy Statement

Version 2, effective 2026-04-01

**To:** The Board of Directors, Midland Diversified Growth Fund

**From:** Chief Investment Officer, Midland Asset Management Limited

**Date:** 2026-04-01

**Subject:** Restated Investment Policy Statement, Version 2

This memorandum sets out the restated Investment Policy Statement of the Midland Diversified Growth Fund (the "Fund") as approved by the Board. It replaces the previous version in its entirety from the effective date shown above. The restatement narrows the permitted duration band and lowers the ceiling on holdings rated below investment grade; every other limit is carried forward unchanged. Please treat the rules below as binding on the Manager from the effective date and circulate this memorandum to Compliance, Risk and the fund administrator.

## Objective

The Fund pursues long-term capital growth through a diversified multi-asset portfolio of global equities, sovereign bonds, corporate credit across the rating spectrum and cash. Performance is judged against the composite benchmark in the Prospectus. All percentages in this memorandum refer to the net asset value of the Fund, measured immediately after any proposed trade.

## Liquidity and diversification

The Fund shall hold cash and cash equivalents of not less than 2% of net asset value at all times. Cash and cash equivalents shall not exceed 25% of net asset value. The Fund shall hold a minimum of 60 distinct positions; the diversification requirement is a monitored limit, and a shortfall triggers a formal warning and a hold on further consolidation rather than an outright block.

## Turnover and active risk

Annual portfolio turnover shall not exceed 200% of average net assets. This is likewise a monitored limit whose breach results in a warning to the Board. Ex-ante tracking error against the benchmark shall not exceed 600 basis points; a proposed trade that would take ex-ante tracking error above this level is blocked.

## Sector, country and currency

Exposure to the Technology sector shall not exceed 20% of net asset value. Exposure to issuers domiciled in United States shall not exceed 50% of net asset value. Unhedged non-base-currency exposure shall not exceed 30% of net asset value. Each of these is a blocking limit.

## Fixed income

Portfolio modified duration shall be maintained between 2 and 7 years. Holdings rated below investment grade shall not exceed 12% of net asset value. No security rated below B (or equivalent) may be purchased; where rating agencies differ, the Manager's documented rating methodology determines the applicable rating.

## Context for the Board

For the Board's information, and without creating any additional limit, I note that in normal market conditions the Manager expects to hold cash of around 3% of net asset value, and that historically the Fund's turnover has averaged approximately 120% per annum. Both figures sit comfortably inside the binding ranges above and are offered as context rather than as commitments.

## Exceptions

An exception to the cash floor, the cash ceiling, the minimum number of positions, the turnover limit, the Technology sector limit, the United States limit, the below investment grade limit or the minimum purchase rating requires the joint approval of the portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer and Compliance. An exception to the tracking error limit, the unhedged currency limit or the duration band requires the joint approval of the portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer and Risk. Every exception is documented with its rationale and expires 30 days after approval unless renewed through the same process.

## Monitoring and breach handling

Compliance tests each rule on every business day against the official valuation. A passive breach caused by market movement is reported to me and to the portfolio manager immediately and remedied in an orderly fashion that protects investors. An active breach caused by a trade is escalated to the Board at its next meeting together with a root cause analysis. All breaches, exceptions and their resolution are retained in the compliance record.

## Review

This Statement will be reviewed by the Board periodically and on any material change in the Fund's objective, benchmark or investor profile. Until amended and re-issued, the rules in this memorandum are the sole authoritative statement of the Fund's investment limits.

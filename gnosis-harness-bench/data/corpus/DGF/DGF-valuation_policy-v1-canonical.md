# Midland Diversified Growth Fund: Valuation and Pricing Policy

Version 1, effective 2025-01-01

**To:** Board of Directors, Midland Diversified Growth Fund; Valuation Committee; Fund Administrator

**From:** Head of Valuation Oversight, Midland Asset Management Limited

**Date:** 2025-01-01

**Subject:** Valuation and Pricing Policy for the Fund

## Purpose of this memorandum

This memorandum records the Valuation and Pricing Policy that the Board has approved for the Midland Diversified Growth Fund (the "Fund"). It sets out the pricing hierarchy, the specific rules that govern odd lots, Level 3 assets and stale prices, and the responsibilities of the Valuation Committee. The rules in the sections headed "Binding requirements" are mandatory for the Manager, the fund administrator and every member of the Valuation Committee from the effective date.

## Pricing hierarchy

The Fund is valued at fair value on each dealing day. Listed securities are priced at the official closing price of the primary exchange, or at the last traded price where no official close is published. Fixed income instruments are priced from the composite evaluated price supplied by the Fund's primary pricing vendor, with a secondary vendor used for challenge. Over-the-counter derivatives are valued using the administrator's models with market observable inputs. Where an instrument cannot be priced by any of these routes it is classified as a Level 3 asset and falls within the requirement described below.

## Binding requirements: lot size

Positions held in odd lots shall be valued using odd-lot prices. Institutional round-lot prices shall not be applied to odd-lot positions. The administrator must identify each fixed income position whose size falls below the institutional round lot for that instrument and source an odd-lot price for it; where the vendor supplies only a round-lot price the position must be referred to the Valuation Committee for a documented odd-lot adjustment before the price is used.

## Binding requirements: Level 3 assets

Any mark applied to a Level 3 asset requires prior approval of the Valuation Committee. The Manager may propose a mark together with its supporting model, inputs and sensitivity analysis, but no mark may be entered into the valuation until the Valuation Committee has approved it and recorded the basis of its approval. An unchanged carrying value is itself a mark for this purpose and must be reaffirmed at each Valuation Committee meeting.

## Binding requirements: stale prices

A price that has not been updated for more than 5 business days is deemed stale and must be referred to the Valuation Committee before use. The administrator maintains a stale price report showing, for each position, the date on which its price last changed. Any position appearing on that report may not be carried at the stale price in a published net asset value until the Valuation Committee has either confirmed the price or directed an alternative valuation.

## Information for the Board

For information only and without creating any requirement, I note that the Valuation Committee reviews its pricing sources approximately every 90 days. That review frequency reflects current practice and may vary with circumstances; it is not a limit and no breach arises if a review falls outside it.

## Exceptions

Any departure from the odd-lot rule, the Level 3 approval requirement or the stale price rule must be approved in advance by the portfolio manager and the Valuation Committee acting together, and the reasons recorded in the Valuation Committee minutes. No other person or body may authorise a departure. An exception applies only to the position and the valuation date to which it relates.

## Monitoring, escalation and records

The administrator reports each business day to Valuation Oversight on odd-lot positions, Level 3 assets and stale prices. Valuation Oversight escalates any pricing that appears to contravene this Policy to the Chief Investment Officer and Compliance on the day it is identified, and the affected net asset value is corrected and, where material, restated in accordance with the Fund's error correction procedure. Minutes of the Valuation Committee, pricing challenges, model documentation and exception records are retained in line with the Manager's record keeping policy.

## Review

The Board reviews this Policy periodically and whenever the Fund acquires a new asset class or the administrator changes its pricing methodology. Amendments take effect on the date stated by the Board and supersede all earlier versions of this memorandum.

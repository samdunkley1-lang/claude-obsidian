# Midland Diversified Growth Fund: Responsible Investment Exclusions Policy

Version 1, effective 2025-01-01

## 1. Application

1.1. This Policy is adopted by the Board of the Midland Diversified Growth Fund (the "Fund") and binds Midland Asset Management Limited (the "Manager") in respect of every security acquired or held for the Fund.

1.2. The Manager shall apply the exclusions in sections 2 to 4 to direct holdings and, wherever the necessary data is available, on a look-through basis to holdings in collective investment schemes.

1.3. Identification of excluded issuers relies on the Fund's appointed responsible investment research provider, supplemented by the Manager's own analysis where coverage is incomplete. Compliance shall load the resulting exclusion list into the order management system so that purchases of excluded issuers are prevented before execution.

## 2. Weapons exclusion

2.1. The Fund shall not hold, purchase or otherwise obtain exposure to any issuer involved in the production of controversial weapons. Controversial weapons means cluster munitions, anti-personnel mines and biological or chemical weapons.

2.2. The exclusion in this section is absolute. No person, whether the portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer, Compliance, the Board or any committee, has power to grant an exception to it, waive it or defer its application, and section 5 does not apply to it.

2.3. Should a holding be found to fall within this section, the Manager shall dispose of it as soon as market conditions reasonably permit and shall report the circumstances to the Board.

## 3. Thermal coal exclusion

3.1. The Fund shall not purchase or add to any issuer that derives more than 25% of its revenue from the extraction of thermal coal or from the generation of electricity from thermal coal.

3.2. Revenue shall be assessed on the latest audited annual accounts available to the research provider. Where an issuer discloses thermal coal revenue only within a wider segment, the Manager shall adopt a conservative estimate and record the basis for it.

3.3. An existing holding that comes to exceed the revenue threshold shall be sold in an orderly manner and shall not be increased while the exclusion applies.

## 4. Global norms exclusion

4.1. The Fund shall not purchase or add to any issuer falling within the category of UN Global Compact violators, being issuers assessed by the research provider as acting in breach of the UN Global Compact principles concerning human rights, labour standards, the environment and anti-corruption.

4.2. Engagement with such an issuer during the disposal period is permitted, but engagement is not of itself a ground for retaining or enlarging the position.

4.3. An issuer placed on the research provider's watch list without a confirmed violation is not excluded under this section; the Manager shall note the concern and reconsider it at each portfolio review.

## 5. Exceptions

5.1. An exception to section 3 or section 4 is valid only where the portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer and Compliance have each approved it in writing before the relevant purchase, stating why the holding remains consistent with the Fund's responsible investment commitments.

5.2. An exception lapses 30 days after approval unless renewed in the same manner.

5.3. For the avoidance of doubt, no exception is available under this section, or otherwise, in respect of section 2.

## 6. Carbon intensity aspiration

6.1. The Manager aims to reduce the portfolio's weighted average carbon intensity by 30% relative to the benchmark over time.

6.2. The aspiration in this section is a statement of intent and not a restriction. It is not monitored as a limit by Compliance, a portfolio whose carbon intensity exceeds the aspiration is not in breach of this Policy, and no exception is required to depart from it. The Manager shall report progress towards it to the Board in its regular responsible investment reporting.

## 7. Monitoring, breaches and records

7.1. Compliance shall screen the portfolio against the exclusion list on each business day and shall screen every proposed purchase before execution.

7.2. A holding identified as excluded shall be reported to the portfolio manager and the Chief Investment Officer on the day of identification, recorded in the breach log and monitored until disposed of.

7.3. The Board shall receive a summary of exclusion events and exceptions at each regular meeting. Research, approvals and breach records shall be retained in accordance with the Manager's record keeping policy.

## 8. Review

8.1. The Board shall review this Policy periodically, and in any case following a material change in the research provider's methodology or in the Fund's responsible investment commitments.

8.2. A revised Policy takes effect on the date the Board specifies and supersedes all earlier versions.

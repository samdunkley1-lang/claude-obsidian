# Midland Diversified Growth Fund: Responsible Investment Exclusions Policy

Version 1, effective 2025-01-01

This Policy sets out the exclusions that Midland Asset Management Limited (the "Manager") applies when investing on behalf of the Midland Diversified Growth Fund (the "Fund"), together with the single climate objective that the Board has asked the Manager to pursue. It has been approved by the Board of the Fund and forms part of the investment restrictions that bind the Manager. It applies to every direct holding of the Fund and, so far as the Manager has the information to apply it, to the underlying holdings of any collective investment scheme in which the Fund invests.

The Manager relies on an external research provider to identify issuers that fall within an exclusion, supplemented by its own analysis where the provider's coverage is incomplete. Compliance maintains the resulting exclusion list in the order management system so that no purchase of an excluded issuer can be executed. Where an issuer already held by the Fund is subsequently identified as excluded, the Manager must dispose of the holding in an orderly manner and may not add to it in the meantime.

The most stringent provision concerns controversial weapons. Issuers involved in the production of controversial weapons, being cluster munitions, anti-personnel mines, and biological and chemical weapons, are excluded absolutely. This exclusion is a hard restriction: no exception may be granted by any person, and neither the portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer, Compliance, the Board nor any committee of the Firm has authority to waive it, suspend it or approve a departure from it. Any position acquired in contravention of it shall be sold as soon as market conditions permit and the transaction reported to the Board.

The Policy also addresses climate-related exclusions in the energy sector. Issuers deriving more than 25% of revenue from thermal coal extraction or thermal coal power generation are excluded. Revenue is measured on the most recent audited annual figures available to the research provider, and where an issuer reports thermal coal revenue only as part of a broader segment the Manager shall make a conservative estimate and document it. The thermal coal exclusion is a binding restriction on purchases and additions, and a holding that crosses the revenue threshold after acquisition must be exited in an orderly manner.

Issuers assessed as UN Global Compact violators, that is, issuers found by the research provider to be in breach of the UN Global Compact principles on human rights, labour, environment and anti-corruption, are excluded. The Manager may engage with such an issuer while a holding is being exited, but engagement does not by itself justify retaining or increasing the position. Where the research provider places an issuer on a watch list rather than confirming a violation, the issuer is not excluded but the Manager shall record the concern and review it at each portfolio review.

Alongside these exclusions the Board has set an aspiration rather than a rule. The Manager aims to reduce the portfolio's weighted average carbon intensity by 30% relative to the benchmark over time. This objective describes a direction of travel; it is not a limit, it is not tested by Compliance, and a portfolio whose carbon intensity sits above the aspiration is not in breach of this Policy. The Manager reports progress against the aspiration to the Board as part of its regular responsible investment reporting.

Exceptions are available only in respect of the thermal coal exclusion and the UN Global Compact exclusion. An exception to either requires the joint written approval of the portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer and Compliance, must be documented with the reasons why the holding is consistent with the Fund's responsible investment commitments, and lapses 30 days after approval unless renewed through the same process. No exception of any kind is available in respect of the controversial weapons exclusion.

Compliance screens the portfolio against the exclusion list on each business day and screens every proposed purchase before execution. A holding found to be excluded is reported to the portfolio manager and the Chief Investment Officer on the day it is identified, entered in the breach log and monitored until it has been sold. The Board receives a summary of all exclusion events and exceptions at each of its regular meetings, and the Manager retains the underlying research and approvals in accordance with its record keeping policy.

The Board reviews this Policy periodically, and in any event when the research provider materially changes its methodology or when the Fund's responsible investment commitments to investors are revised. A revised Policy takes effect on the date stated by the Board and replaces all earlier versions from that date.

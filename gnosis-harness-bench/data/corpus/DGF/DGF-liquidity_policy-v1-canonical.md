# Midland Diversified Growth Fund: Liquidity Risk Management Policy

Version 1, effective 2025-01-01

## Purpose

This Policy describes how Midland Asset Management Limited (the "Manager") manages the liquidity risk of the Midland Diversified Growth Fund (the "Fund") so that redemption requests can be met in accordance with the Prospectus without disadvantaging remaining investors. It establishes binding liquidity limits, a swing pricing requirement and the governance around them. It has been approved by the Board of the Fund and is binding on the Manager.

## Classification of holdings

Each holding is assigned a liquidity classification by Risk using observed trading volumes, bid-offer spreads, the depth of the dealer market and, for funds and unlisted instruments, the redemption terms available to the Fund. A holding is classified as illiquid where Risk is not satisfied that it could be realised at or close to its carrying value within a reasonable period under normal market conditions. Classifications are refreshed on each business day and reconsidered by Risk whenever market conditions change materially.

## Binding liquidity limits

The table below sets out the liquidity limits that apply at all times. Percentages refer to the net asset value of the Fund, measured immediately after giving effect to a proposed transaction. A blocking limit prevents execution of any transaction that would cause it to be exceeded; a monitored limit results in a formal warning and a hold on further deterioration.

| Ref | Requirement | Threshold | Type |
| - | - | - | - |
| 1 | Holdings classified as illiquid shall not exceed the threshold | 15% of net asset value | blocking |
| 2 | At least the threshold proportion of net asset value shall be realisable within 5 business days under normal market conditions | 75% of net asset value | blocking |
| 3 | No single position shall require more than the stated number of days to liquidate at 25% of average daily volume | 5 business days | monitored |

Realisability for the purposes of row 2 is assessed by Risk using the same volume-based methodology as row 3, applied position by position and aggregated across the Fund.

## Swing pricing

| Ref | Requirement | Trigger | Type |
| - | - | - | - |
| 4 | Swing pricing shall be applied on any dealing day on which net flows exceed the trigger level | net flows of 1% of net asset value | blocking |

The swing factor applied on a triggered day is determined by the fund administrator in accordance with the swing pricing procedure approved by the Board, reflecting estimated dealing costs and market impact. The Manager may not instruct the administrator to omit the swing adjustment on a day when the trigger has been met.

## Descriptive information

The following statement is provided for context and is not a limit: at the last review illiquid holdings represented approximately 4.5% of net asset value. That figure reflects the composition of the portfolio on a particular date and is not tested by Compliance as a threshold.

## Exceptions

An exception to any limit in rows 1 to 4 requires the joint written approval of the portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer and Risk. Each exception must record its rationale and the expected route back within the limit, and lapses 30 days after approval unless renewed through the same process. No exception may be granted after the event to regularise a transaction executed in breach of this Policy.

## Monitoring and breach handling

Risk calculates the illiquid proportion, the realisable proportion and the days to liquidate for every position on each business day and reports the results to the portfolio manager and Compliance. An excess arising from market movement or from a change in classification is reported to the Chief Investment Officer as soon as it is identified and corrected in an orderly manner having regard to the interests of investors. An excess arising from a transaction is recorded in the breach log, investigated for root cause and reported to the Board with the remedial action taken. Stress testing of the liquidity profile under adverse redemption scenarios is performed as part of the Manager's wider risk framework and its results are presented to the Board.

## Review

The Board reviews this Policy periodically and whenever the Fund's dealing frequency, investor concentration or asset mix changes materially. Amendments take effect on the date stated by the Board and supersede all earlier versions.

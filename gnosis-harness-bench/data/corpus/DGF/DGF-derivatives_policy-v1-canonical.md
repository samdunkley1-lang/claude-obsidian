# Midland Diversified Growth Fund: Derivatives Usage Policy

Version 1, effective 2025-01-01

## Purpose

This Policy governs the use of derivative instruments by Midland Asset Management Limited (the "Manager") on behalf of the Midland Diversified Growth Fund (the "Fund"). It sets binding limits on leverage, counterparty exposure and collateralisation, and it describes how those limits are monitored and how exceptions are approved. It should be read together with the Investment Policy Statement and the Counterparty and Broker Policy.

## Permitted purposes and instruments

- Derivatives may be used for efficient portfolio management, for hedging currency, interest rate, credit and equity market risk, and for taking investment positions consistent with the Fund's objective.
- Permitted instrument types are exchange-traded futures and options, forward foreign exchange contracts, interest rate and inflation swaps, credit default swaps on indices and single names, and total return swaps on indices.
- The Manager may not use any instrument type not listed above without the prior written approval of the Board.

## Leverage and exposure limits

- Gross notional exposure through derivatives shall not exceed 100% of net asset value. Gross notional is the sum of the absolute notional values of all open derivative positions without netting or offset.
- Uncollateralised derivatives exposure shall not exceed 5% of net asset value. Exposure is uncollateralised to the extent that the positive mark-to-market owed to the Fund by a counterparty is not covered by collateral received under the applicable credit support arrangement.
- A proposed transaction that would cause either limit to be exceeded is blocked in the order management system and may proceed only under an approved exception.

## Counterparty requirements

- Mark-to-market exposure to any single derivatives counterparty shall not exceed 20% of net asset value, measured as the net positive replacement value across all transactions with that counterparty after taking account of enforceable netting.
- Derivatives may only be transacted with counterparties rated A- or better by at least one recognised rating agency. Where a derivatives counterparty is downgraded below A- after a trade has been executed, no new transactions may be entered into with that counterparty and the Manager shall present a plan to Risk for reducing the existing exposure.
- Every over-the-counter counterparty must have executed a master agreement and credit support annex with the Fund before the first trade.

## Historical context

- The following statement is provided for information only and is not a limit: over the last three years gross notional derivatives exposure has typically been around 40% of net asset value. This reflects the Manager's practice under normal conditions and does not constrain the Manager within the binding limits above.

## Exceptions

- An exception to the gross notional limit, the uncollateralised exposure limit, the single counterparty exposure limit or the minimum counterparty rating requires the joint written approval of the portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer and Risk.
- Each exception must record its rationale and the expected route back within the limit, and lapses 30 days after approval unless renewed through the same process.
- No exception may be granted after the event to regularise a transaction that was executed in breach of this Policy.

## Monitoring and breach handling

- Risk calculates gross notional, uncollateralised exposure and counterparty exposure on each business day using the official valuation and reports the results to the portfolio manager and Compliance.
- An excess arising from market movement is reported to the Chief Investment Officer as soon as it is identified and corrected in an orderly manner having regard to the interests of investors.
- An excess arising from a transaction is recorded in the breach log, investigated for root cause and reported to the Board together with the remedial action taken.
- All approvals, exception records and breach reports are retained in the compliance archive for the period required by the Fund's record keeping policy.

## Review

- The Board reviews this Policy periodically and whenever the Fund's use of derivatives changes materially in scale or nature.
- Amendments take effect on the date stated by the Board and supersede all earlier versions.

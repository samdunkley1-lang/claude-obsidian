# Midland Diversified Growth Fund: Investment Guidelines and Restrictions

Version 1, effective 2025-01-01

## 1. Scope and construction

1.1. These Guidelines bind Midland Asset Management Limited (the "Manager") in the management of the Midland Diversified Growth Fund (the "Fund") and supplement the Investment Policy Statement. Nothing in these Guidelines shall be read as permitting what the Prospectus forbids.

1.2. No percentage in these Guidelines is to be construed otherwise than as a proportion of the net asset value of the Fund, measured immediately after the proposed transaction would take effect.

1.3. Unless an exception has been approved under section 6, the Manager is not permitted to execute any transaction the effect of which would be that a limit in sections 2 to 4 was no longer satisfied.

## 2. Security and issuer concentration

2.1. It is not the case that any single security may exceed 4% of net asset value; no such security shall in any circumstances be permitted to represent more than 4% of net asset value.

2.2. Aggregate exposure to any single issuer, taking together all securities of that issuer, shall in no case be permitted to exceed 5% of net asset value, nor shall the Manager treat securities of entities under common control otherwise than as securities of a single issuer for this purpose.

2.3. Within the Alternatives sleeve, no single security shall exceed 10% of net asset value. This limit does not displace the fund-wide limit stated earlier in this section; a security that falls within both is tested against both.

## 3. Sector concentration

3.1. Exposure to the Energy sector shall not, under any circumstances, be permitted to exceed 15% of net asset value; it is not open to the Manager to hold Energy exposure above 15% of net asset value on any basis other than an approved exception.

3.2. Exposure to the Healthcare sector may not at any time be allowed to exceed 20% of net asset value, and no transaction shall be executed that would cause Healthcare exposure to be other than at or below 20% of net asset value.

3.3. Sector classification is not to be determined otherwise than by the classification standard adopted in the Manager's portfolio management system.

## 4. Country concentration

4.1. Exposure to issuers domiciled in Japan shall in no event be permitted to exceed 20% of net asset value; the Manager may not add to Japan exposure unless, after the addition, it would remain no more than 20% of net asset value.

4.2. It is not permitted for exposure to issuers domiciled in United Kingdom to exceed 20% of net asset value, nor for the Manager to execute a transaction whose effect would be that United Kingdom exposure was no longer at or below 20% of net asset value.

4.3. Domicile is not to be determined otherwise than by the country of risk assigned by the Manager's data provider, or by country of incorporation where none is assigned.

## 5. Restricted List

5.1. No security of an issuer appearing on the Firm's Restricted List may be purchased or added to under any circumstances.

5.2. This restriction cannot be waived by the Manager, the Chief Investment Officer or Compliance, and no other person or committee has authority to override it. No exception under section 6 may be sought or granted in respect of it.

5.3. Compliance maintains the Restricted List and notifies the Manager of each change without delay. An existing holding in an issuer added to the list may be retained or sold but may not be increased.

## 6. Exceptions

6.1. An exception to a limit in section 2, 3 or 4 is of no effect unless approved in writing by the portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer and Compliance before the transaction to which it relates.

6.2. No exception shall remain in force for longer than 30 days from approval unless renewed through the same process, and no exception shall be granted without a documented rationale and an expected route back within the limit.

## 7. Statement of practice

7.1. The Manager's typical initial position size is approximately 2% of net asset value. This is a description of practice and not a limit; it is not monitored as a limit and cannot be breached.

## 8. Monitoring, breaches and review

8.1. Compliance tests every limit on each business day against the official valuation and operates pre-trade controls in the order management system so that no transaction contrary to sections 2 to 4 is executed without an approved exception.

8.2. A passive excess arising from market movement is reported to the portfolio manager and the Chief Investment Officer as soon as it is identified and corrected in an orderly manner; an active excess arising from a transaction is recorded in the breach log and escalated to the Board.

8.3. The Board reviews these Guidelines periodically and whenever the Investment Policy Statement is amended. No earlier version retains any effect once a successor is in force.

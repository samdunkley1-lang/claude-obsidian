# Midland Diversified Growth Fund: Investment Guidelines and Restrictions

Version 1, effective 2025-01-01

## 1. Purpose and application

1.1. These Investment Guidelines and Restrictions (the "Guidelines") supplement the Investment Policy Statement of the Midland Diversified Growth Fund (the "Fund") and set out the concentration limits and prohibitions that Midland Asset Management Limited (the "Manager") must observe in managing the portfolio.

1.2. The Guidelines are binding on the Manager, its delegates and every individual with dealing authority over the Fund. In the event of conflict between these Guidelines and the Prospectus, the more restrictive provision prevails.

1.3. Unless stated otherwise, a percentage refers to the net asset value of the Fund measured immediately after giving effect to the proposed transaction, and exposure is measured on a look-through basis where the Manager has the information to do so.

## 2. Single security and issuer limits

2.1. No single security shall exceed 4% of net asset value.

2.2. Aggregate exposure to any single issuer, including all securities of that issuer, shall not exceed 5% of net asset value. For this purpose securities issued by entities under common control are aggregated with those of the parent.

2.3. Within the Alternatives sleeve, no single security shall exceed 10% of net asset value. The Alternatives sleeve comprises the holdings designated as such in the Manager's portfolio management system, and this clause applies in addition to, and not in place of, the fund-wide single security limit in this section where a security falls within both.

## 3. Sector limits

3.1. Exposure to the Energy sector shall not exceed 15% of net asset value.

3.2. Exposure to the Healthcare sector shall not exceed 20% of net asset value.

3.3. Sector classification follows the industry classification standard adopted in the Manager's portfolio management system.

## 4. Country limits

4.1. Exposure to issuers domiciled in Japan shall not exceed 20% of net asset value.

4.2. Exposure to issuers domiciled in United Kingdom shall not exceed 20% of net asset value.

4.3. Domicile is determined by the country of risk assigned by the Manager's data provider, with country of incorporation used where no country of risk is assigned.

## 5. Restricted List

5.1. No security of an issuer appearing on the Firm's Restricted List may be purchased or added to under any circumstances.

5.2. The restriction in the preceding clause cannot be waived by the Manager, the Chief Investment Officer or Compliance, nor by any other person or committee. No exception process applies to it, and any transaction executed in contravention of it shall be reversed at the Manager's expense.

5.3. Compliance maintains the Restricted List and notifies the Manager of additions and removals without delay. An existing holding whose issuer is added to the Restricted List may be retained or sold but may not be increased.

## 6. Statements of practice

6.1. The Manager's typical initial position size is approximately 2% of net asset value. This statement describes practice only; it is not a limit, is not monitored as a limit and does not give rise to any breach.

## 7. Exceptions

7.1. An exception to any limit in sections 2, 3 or 4 requires the joint written approval of the portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer and Compliance, recorded before the transaction to which it relates is executed.

7.2. Each exception shall state its rationale and the expected path back within the limit and shall lapse 30 days after approval unless renewed through the same process.

7.3. No exception is available in respect of section 5.

## 8. Monitoring and breaches

8.1. Compliance shall test every limit in these Guidelines on each business day against the official valuation, and pre-trade checks in the order management system shall prevent execution of any transaction that would exceed a limit in sections 2 to 4 without an approved exception.

8.2. A passive excess arising from market movement shall be reported to the portfolio manager and the Chief Investment Officer as soon as identified and remedied in an orderly manner consistent with the interests of investors. An active excess arising from a transaction shall be recorded in the breach log and escalated to the Board.

## 9. Review

9.1. The Board reviews these Guidelines periodically and whenever the Investment Policy Statement is amended. Amendments take effect from the date stated by the Board and supersede all earlier versions.

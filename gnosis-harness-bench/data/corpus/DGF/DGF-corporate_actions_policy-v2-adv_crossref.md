# Midland Diversified Growth Fund: Corporate Actions Election Policy

Version 2, effective 2026-04-01

**To:** Portfolio Managers; Investment Operations; Compliance

**From:** Chief Operating Officer, Midland Asset Management Limited

**Date:** 2026-04-01

**Subject:** Corporate Actions Election Policy, restated as Version 2

## Purpose

This memorandum restates the Corporate Actions Election Policy for the Midland Diversified Growth Fund (the "Fund") as approved by the Board. Nothing of substance has changed from the previous version; the text has been reorganised so that the operative defaults and the instruction deadline now sit together in Schedule A at the end of this memorandum, where they can be checked at a glance. The body of this memorandum explains how the defaults are applied, who may depart from them and how departures are documented. The body and Schedule A together constitute the Policy and are binding on the Manager, Investment Operations and any agent instructing the custodian.

## How the defaults work

For each type of event listed in Schedule A, the Fund's instruction to the custodian shall follow the default set out in Schedule A unless a departure has been documented or approved in the manner described below. Investment Operations records every notification received from the custodian, identifies the applicable default from Schedule A, and prepares the instruction accordingly. A default is a binding rule, and an instruction that departs from it without the required documentation or approval is a breach of this Policy.

## Scrip dividend alternatives

Where an issuer offers a scrip dividend alternative, the Fund shall elect the default set out in Schedule A. A scrip election, being a departure from that default, requires a documented rationale from the portfolio manager setting out why receiving shares serves investors better than the default. A scrip election is prohibited where the post-election position would exceed the single security limit in the Investment Guidelines; Investment Operations must confirm the projected position against that limit before any scrip instruction is released, and the limit itself is not restated here.

## Rights issues

On a rights issue the Fund shall follow the default set out in Schedule A. A decision to let rights lapse, or to sell the nil-paid rights in the market, requires a documented rationale from the portfolio manager recorded before the instruction is given. The rationale should address the terms of the issue, the effect on the holding and any concentration consequences of following the default in full.

## Tender offers

On a tender offer the Fund shall follow the default set out in Schedule A. Tendering any part of a holding requires approval of the Chief Investment Officer, given in writing after considering the portfolio manager's recommendation, the offer terms and the position of holders who do not tender.

## Election deadline

Every election, whether it follows the default or departs from it, shall be instructed to the custodian no later than the deadline in Schedule A, measured against the custodian's own deadline for the event. Investment Operations tracks the custodian deadline for each event, obtains the portfolio manager's decision in good time and confirms that the custodian has acknowledged the instruction. An instruction released after the deadline in Schedule A is a breach of this Policy even where the custodian accepts it.

## Exceptions

An exception to any default in Schedule A, or to the deadline in Schedule A, requires the joint written approval of the portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer and Compliance. The exception must record its reasons and lapses after the validity period stated in Schedule A unless renewed through the same process. An exception cannot be granted retrospectively to cure an instruction already given in breach of this Policy.

## Monitoring and records

Compliance reviews the corporate actions log on each business day for events approaching the custodian deadline without an instruction, for departures from a default lacking documentation or approval, and for late instructions. Findings are reported to the portfolio manager and the Chief Investment Officer on the day of identification and entered in the breach log; where the Fund has suffered a loss the matter is escalated to the Board with the remedial action taken. Notifications, decisions, approvals and instructions are retained under the Manager's record keeping policy.

## Review

The Board reviews this Policy periodically and whenever the custodian's processing arrangements change materially. Amendments take effect on the date stated by the Board and supersede all earlier versions.

## Schedule A: defaults, deadline and exception validity

| Item | Event or parameter | Default or value |
| - | - | - |
| A | Scrip dividend | Cash |
| B | Rights issue | Take up |
| C | Tender offer | Decline |
| D | Election deadline | 2 business days before the custodian deadline |

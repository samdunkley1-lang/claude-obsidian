# Midland Diversified Growth Fund: Counterparty and Broker Policy

Version 2, effective 2026-04-01

## Purpose

This Policy sets the standards that Midland Asset Management Limited (the "Manager") must meet when selecting and dealing with counterparties, deposit banks and executing brokers on behalf of the Midland Diversified Growth Fund (the "Fund"). Version 2 tightens the ceiling on exposure to individual securities lending and repo counterparties; the remaining provisions are carried forward from the previous version without change. The Policy is approved by the Board and binds the Manager and its delegates from the effective date. Derivatives counterparties are addressed separately in the Derivatives Usage Policy, which applies alongside this document.

## Scope and measurement

- The Policy covers securities lending, repurchase transactions, cash deposits, trade execution and any other arrangement under which the Fund bears credit or performance risk on a third party.
- Percentages refer to the net asset value of the Fund measured immediately after a proposed transaction, except for the broker concentration limit, which is measured against annual traded value.
- No counterparty, bank or broker may be used unless Risk has approved it following credit and operational due diligence and placed it on the approved counterparty list.

## Securities lending and repo

- Exposure to any single securities lending counterparty, or to any single repo counterparty, shall not exceed 15% of net asset value.
- Exposure is measured as the market value of securities lent or cash placed, less the value of collateral received that is enforceable under the governing agreement.
- All such transactions shall be fully collateralised, marked daily and documented under a master agreement approved by Risk.
- A proposed loan or repo that would cause the ceiling to be exceeded is blocked and may proceed only under an approved exception.

## Affiliated entities

- No transaction may be entered into with an affiliated entity of the Manager, meaning any entity that controls, is controlled by or is under common control with the Manager.
- This conflict-of-interest prohibition cannot be overridden by any individual or committee. The portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer, Risk, Compliance and the Board are each without authority to waive it, and no exception may be granted in respect of it.
- Any transaction found to contravene this prohibition shall be unwound at the Manager's expense and reported to the Board.

## Cash deposits

- Cash deposits may only be placed with a deposit bank rated A- or better by a recognised rating agency at the time the deposit is placed.
- If a deposit bank is downgraded below A- while a balance is outstanding, no new deposits may be placed with it and the balance shall be withdrawn at the earliest date the deposit terms allow.

## Executing brokers

- No single executing broker shall account for more than 25% of the Fund's annual traded value; exposure to any single executing broker is assessed by traded value over the rolling annual period.
- This is a monitored limit. An excess generates a formal warning to the Chief Investment Officer and Compliance and a hold on directing further flow to that broker until concentration has fallen back within the limit.
- Broker selection and allocation follow the Manager's best execution policy.

## Descriptive note

- For information only: securities lending exposure to the largest counterparty is currently about 10% of net asset value. This describes the position at the date of this Policy, is not a limit and cannot be breached.

## Exceptions

- An exception to the securities lending and repo ceiling, the deposit bank rating requirement or the broker concentration limit requires the joint written approval of the portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer and Risk, recorded before the relevant transaction.
- Each exception must state its rationale and the expected route back within the limit and lapses 30 days after approval unless renewed through the same process.
- No exception is available in respect of the affiliated entity prohibition.

## Monitoring and breaches

- Risk calculates counterparty and deposit exposures on each business day; Compliance tracks broker concentration against rolling annual traded value.
- An excess arising from market movement or collateral revaluation is reported to the portfolio manager and the Chief Investment Officer as soon as identified and corrected in an orderly manner.
- An excess arising from a transaction is recorded in the breach log and escalated to the Board with an analysis of cause and remedy.
- Approvals, exception records and breach reports are retained in accordance with the Manager's record keeping policy.

## Review

- The Board reviews this Policy periodically and whenever the Fund's use of securities lending, repo or deposit facilities changes materially.
- Amendments take effect on the date stated by the Board and supersede all earlier versions.

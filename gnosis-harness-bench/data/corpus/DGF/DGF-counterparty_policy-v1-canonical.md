# Midland Diversified Growth Fund: Counterparty and Broker Policy

Version 1, effective 2025-01-01

## 1. Purpose and application

1.1. This Policy governs the selection of, and exposure to, the counterparties, banks and brokers with which Midland Asset Management Limited (the "Manager") deals on behalf of the Midland Diversified Growth Fund (the "Fund"). It is approved by the Board of the Fund and is binding on the Manager and its delegates.

1.2. It applies to securities lending and repurchase transactions, cash deposits, trade execution and any other arrangement under which the Fund bears credit or performance risk on a third party. Derivatives counterparties are governed by the Derivatives Usage Policy, which applies in addition to this Policy.

1.3. Percentages in this Policy refer to the net asset value of the Fund, measured immediately after giving effect to a proposed transaction, except where a different measure is expressly stated.

## 2. Approval of counterparties

2.1. No counterparty, bank or broker may be used unless it has been approved by Risk following credit and operational due diligence and entered on the approved counterparty list maintained by Risk.

2.2. Risk reviews the approved list periodically and removes any entity whose creditworthiness or operational standing no longer satisfies the requirements of this Policy.

## 3. Securities lending and repo counterparties

3.1. Exposure to any single securities lending counterparty, or to any single repo counterparty, shall not exceed 20% of net asset value. Exposure is measured as the market value of securities lent or cash placed, less the value of collateral received and enforceable under the relevant agreement.

3.2. All securities lending and repurchase transactions shall be fully collateralised on a daily marked basis under a master agreement approved by Risk.

## 4. Affiliated entities

4.1. No transaction may be entered into with an affiliated entity of the Manager, meaning any entity that controls, is controlled by or is under common control with the Manager, in any capacity covered by this Policy.

4.2. The prohibition in this section is a conflict-of-interest safeguard and cannot be overridden by any individual or committee. Neither the portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer, Risk, Compliance nor the Board may waive it, and no exception under section 7 is available in respect of it.

## 5. Cash deposits

5.1. Cash deposits may only be placed with a deposit bank rated A- or better by a recognised rating agency at the time the deposit is placed.

5.2. Where a deposit bank is downgraded below A- while a deposit is outstanding, no further deposits may be placed with it and the Manager shall withdraw the outstanding balance at the earliest date permitted by the deposit terms.

## 6. Executing brokers

6.1. No single executing broker shall account for more than 25% of the Fund's annual traded value. For the avoidance of doubt, exposure to any single executing broker under this clause is measured by traded value over the rolling annual period rather than by net asset value.

6.2. The limit in this section is a monitored limit: an excess results in a formal warning to the Chief Investment Officer and Compliance and a hold on directing further flow to the broker concerned until the concentration has been reduced.

6.3. Broker selection shall follow the Manager's best execution policy, and the allocation of trading between brokers shall be reviewed under that policy.

## 7. Exceptions

7.1. An exception to section 3, section 5 or section 6 requires the joint written approval of the portfolio manager, the Chief Investment Officer and Risk, recorded before the transaction to which it relates.

7.2. Each exception shall state its rationale and the expected route back within the limit and shall lapse 30 days after approval unless renewed through the same process.

7.3. No exception is available in respect of section 4.

## 8. Statement of current position

8.1. For information only, securities lending exposure to the largest counterparty is currently about 10% of net asset value. This statement describes the position at the date of this Policy and is not a limit; it is not monitored as such and does not give rise to any breach.

## 9. Monitoring, breaches and review

9.1. Risk calculates counterparty exposures on each business day, and Compliance tracks broker concentration on a rolling basis against annual traded value.

9.2. An excess arising from market movement or collateral valuation is reported to the portfolio manager and the Chief Investment Officer as soon as it is identified and corrected in an orderly manner. An excess arising from a transaction is recorded in the breach log and escalated to the Board with an analysis of cause and remedy.

9.3. The Board reviews this Policy periodically and whenever the Fund's use of securities lending, repo or deposit facilities changes materially. Amendments take effect on the date stated by the Board and supersede all earlier versions.

## 1. What they are actually buying

The posting language (verified via search index; direct page blocked) is unusually explicit about the shape of the person:

> The role holder **partners with senior business, technology, risk, and operations leaders** to define and execute a strategic AI transformation roadmap **aligned to Santander Group priorities and UK business objectives**. Accountable for **embedding AI-driven decision-making into core processes, accelerating value creation, and ensuring robust governance, ethical standards, and regulatory compliance** across all AI initiatives… **translating cutting-edge AI capabilities into tangible business outcomes**, fostering a culture of innovation, and **positioning Santander UK as a leader in responsible AI adoption**.

Decoded, that is four distinct jobs in one role. Your application has to show evidence against all four, because the screener will be scoring them separately:

| What the JD says | What they mean | What proof looks like |
|---|---|---|
| "Partners with senior business, technology, risk and operations leaders" | You have no direct authority over the P&Ls you must change. This is influence-without-control at ExCo-adjacent level. | Named a transformation you drove across functions you did not own. |
| "Strategic AI transformation roadmap aligned to Group priorities **and** UK objectives" | Santander UK sits under a Madrid-set global agenda. You will be reconciling a Group mandate with a UK regulatory and commercial reality. | Experience in a matrixed multinational, or local-entity-vs-group tension. |
| "Embedding AI-driven decision-making into core processes, accelerating value creation" | **Adoption and P&L, not pilots.** This is the make-or-break criterion. | A number: £ or % value delivered, and the process you changed to get it. |
| "Robust governance, ethical standards, and regulatory compliance" | UK financial services. Model risk, Consumer Duty, explainability, audit trail. | Evidence you can ship AI *through* a second line, not around it. |

The JD also signals culture in a way worth mirroring: the organisation values **"bold thinking, innovation, and the courage to challenge what's possible"** alongside **"a proactive and responsible approach toward risk management."** Both halves. An application that is all boldness reads as a compliance risk; all-governance reads as a blocker. Pair them in every answer.

> [!tip] The single highest-leverage line in your whole application
> An AI transformation lead who cannot quote a realised value number is indistinguishable from a strategy consultant. Lead your CV summary and your supporting statement with one: *"delivered £Xm of realised benefit from N production AI use cases across M business units."* If your number is small, use the ratio instead — pilots moved to production, or adoption rate.

## 2. Santander intelligence brief

Use these. Very few applicants will, and it is the fastest way to read as someone who has already started the job. All figures below came from search results dated to 2026; **verify any number before you quote it in a live interview** — I could not open the primary pages to confirm them directly.

**The Group AI position**
- Santander has moved to an explicitly **"AI-first"** strategy and has extended AI tool access to **all ~185,000 employees** group-wide — around **15,000 in the UK**.
- The Group expects **more than €200m in business value** (revenue plus cost) from AI by the **end of 2026**.
- Wider **"ONE Transformation"** programme targets **~€1bn of value by 2028**.
- There is a **collaboration with OpenAI** underpinning the rollout.
- The 2026–27 roadmap is **scaling agentic AI**, transforming front- and back-office processes, and **fully conversational banking**.
- **17,000+ people** were reported working with agentic AI in software development as of May 2026.

**The platform substrate — Gravity**
- **Gravity** is Santander's home-grown cloud-native core banking platform, migrating core banking off the mainframe. At completion it is projected to handle **>1 trillion technical executions/year**.
- Original Gravity delivered around **16% savings** via simplification and standardisation.
- **Gravity 2.0** is live in the payments hub, connects Santander banks across markets, and is projected at **up to 45% savings** by removing duplication and driving integration cost toward zero.

**The UK specifics**
- Santander UK is targeting **~240,000 calls — roughly 40% of annual voice volume** — for AI-assisted self-service.
- **AI copilots already support 40%+ of contact centre interactions.**
- Reported plans to cut costs by **over £430m** under the AI strategy.
- **Unity Place, Milton Keynes**: >£150m investment, 6,000+ employees. Santander Technology UK is **2,500+ people** across Milton Keynes, London, Leicester and Bootle.
- **Openbank** (Group digital bank) runs AI over **100,000 AML alerts/year**, cutting investigations from hours to minutes.

**The regulatory frame you are expected to know**
- **PRA SS1/23** — Model Risk Management principles for banks, **effective 17 May 2024**. Five principles: model identification & risk classification; governance; development/implementation/use; independent validation; risk mitigants. It **explicitly extends to AI/ML** modelling techniques. This is the single most important acronym in the room; being fluent in it separates AI-in-banking from AI-in-general.
- **FCA Consumer Duty** — good outcomes, foreseeable harm, vulnerable customers. Any customer-facing AI decisioning must evidence it.
- **SMCR / Conduct Rules** — an S5 Head of role may be a Certification Function. Expect fit-and-proper screening.
- **EU AI Act** — matters at Group level even post-Brexit, because Santander is a Spanish-headquartered group and the UK entity will inherit Group standards.

> [!note] The strategic insight to deploy at interview
> Santander's published numbers are **enablement** numbers (185,000 seats, 17,000 developers, €200m) — deployment and access metrics. The hard part of this job is the **denominator**: what proportion of those seats produce measurable, repeatable, audited business value. Positioning yourself as the person who converts access into realised P&L — and who can defend every use case to the second line — is exactly the gap in the story their own press releases reveal. Say that. It demonstrates you read past the headline.

### 4.3 "How would you approach the first 90 days?"

This is largely answerable from public information, so I have drafted it fully. Adjust the emphasis to your strengths.

> **Days 1–30 — establish the denominator.** Santander UK has enabled AI broadly; the first question I would answer is what proportion of that enablement is producing measured value. I would inventory live and in-flight AI use cases across the UK business, map each to a named business owner and a benefit line, and honestly classify them: realised, projected, or unevidenced. In parallel I would meet the second line early — model risk, compliance, internal audit — because their appetite sets the real delivery ceiling, and arriving after a governance surprise is a bad first impression.
>
> **Days 31–60 — align the two roadmaps.** The role sits between Group priorities and UK objectives, and those are not automatically the same. I would produce a single view of where the Group agenda — agentic AI, conversational banking, the Gravity platform direction — maps cleanly onto UK commercial priorities, and where it does not. The gaps are where a Head of AI Transformation earns their grade: either the UK case gets made to Group, or the Group capability gets adapted to a UK regulatory reality.
>
> **Days 61–90 — commit to a defensible portfolio.** A prioritised roadmap with named owners, benefit cases signed by Finance rather than asserted by me, and an agreed control framework mapping each use case to SS1/23 classification and Consumer Duty outcome-testing. I would rather commit to fewer use cases with owned, Finance-validated numbers than publish a broad roadmap that cannot survive its first benefits review.

## 6. Interview preparation

Likely question set for an S5 Data & AI role in a UK bank, and the trap in each:

| Question | The trap | The move |
|---|---|---|
| "How do you prioritise AI use cases?" | Reciting a 2×2 of value vs. feasibility. | Add the third axis they care about: **regulatory defensibility**. A high-value, high-feasibility use case that cannot pass model validation is worth zero. |
| "Tell me about an AI initiative that failed." | Choosing a fake failure. | Pick a real one, own the decision — not the circumstances — and state the mechanism you changed so it could not recur. |
| "How do you handle a business unit that won't adopt?" | "I'd get executive sponsorship." | Diagnose *why* first. Non-adoption is usually a rational response to a tool that does not fit the workflow. Then the sponsorship. |
| "What's your view on agentic AI in banking?" | Enthusiasm without controls. | Santander is scaling agentic AI in fraud, AML and payments. Agentic systems break the model-risk frame because the action space is not enumerable in advance. Say that, then say what you would do about it: constrained tool access, human-in-the-loop on irreversible actions, full action-level audit trail. |
| "How do you measure success?" | Deployment and access metrics. | Realised, Finance-validated benefit. Explicitly distinguish it from enablement metrics — this is the whole thesis of your application. |
| "Group wants X, UK needs Y. What do you do?" | Picking a side. | Make the UK case *to* Group with evidence, and where Group standards are non-negotiable, adapt delivery rather than litigate. Show you can do both. |

**Questions to ask them** — these signal that you have modelled the job:

- Of the AI value in the UK plan to end-2026, how much is already Finance-validated versus projected?
- Does this role own a benefits line, or influence business lines that own theirs?
- Where does the second line currently sit on agentic AI with irreversible actions?
- How does the UK AI roadmap sequence against the Gravity core migration — does AI wait for the platform, or run alongside it?

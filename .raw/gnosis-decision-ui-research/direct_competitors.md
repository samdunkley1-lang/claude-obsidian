## Key Question 1: Who are the vendors? Profiles of each direct and near competitor

### Takeaway
The "buy-side decision-quality" market splits into (a) two or three specialist behavioral/decision-analytics firms (Alpha Theory, Essentia Analytics, Inalytics, and Cabot inside FactSet) that measure decision skill after the fact and nudge position sizing, (b) a larger research-management-system (RMS) tier (Bipsync, FactSet IRN, Verity/MackeyRMS, AlphaSense with the absorbed Sentieo/Tegus, Backstop/ION, Dynamo, Allvue) that captures research and audit trails but not structured decision lifecycles, and (c) portfolio-intelligence/analytics vendors (SEI Novus, Investment Metrics/Confluence) that are adjacent at most. No vendor found publicly describes an event-sourced, bitemporal decision ledger with hard policy gates, chained approvals and precedent matching; "Vestlab" does not exist as a buy-side product.

### Inferences
- Approvals/four-eyes: none of the profiled vendors advertises maker-checker approval chains for investment decisions in the material found. Backstop's "standardized workflows" and Dynamo's automation are due-diligence pipeline workflows for allocators, not decision approval gates. (Absence of evidence in public pages; unverified against product demos.)
- Immutable/sealed records: the RMS vendors' "versioning and archiving" satisfy books-and-records retention but are document-level; no vendor claims an append-only event store or a sealed commit with a substantive action set.
- Point-in-time / bitemporal reconstruction: not claimed by any vendor found. Essentia and Inalytics reconstruct decision history from trade data ex post, which is a different thing (they can show what was held when, not what was known/approved as of a date).
- Precedent matching: the nearest analogues are (a) Essentia/Cabot's decision-type taxonomies (buy/sell/size/add/trim) used for statistical skill scoring and (b) chat-over-notes retrieval. Nothing resembling gnosis's five matchers (structural, trace-pattern over event chains, graph-embedding, hybrid, semantic) was found.
- Hard non-overridable gates (info barriers, conflicts) live today in OMS/pre-trade compliance engines and surveillance tools, not in any vendor in this competitive set; gnosis's admin-proof gates are therefore a differentiator against this set but will be compared by buyers to their existing compliance engine.

## Key Question 6: What is the state of the category in 2025-2026: growth, consolidation, AI pivots?

### Takeaway
The category has been consolidating since 2021 (Cabot to FactSet, Novus to SEI, Backstop to ION, Investment Metrics to Confluence, Sentieo and Tegus to AlphaSense, Northern Trust into Essentia) and the pace continued through 2025-2026 (Verity to TMX Group, Peak to UiPath, Pyramid to ServiceNow), while every surviving RMS vendor shipped GenAI chat/summarization features between Nov 2024 and Nov 2025 and AlphaSense scaled to $600M+ ARR and a $7.5B valuation with an IPO in preparation. Independent behavioral-analytics specialists (Alpha Theory, Essentia, Inalytics) remain small and founder-led, with Essentia's own benchmark showing only 39% of active PMs adding value through decisions.

### Inferences
- Three structural trends: (1) RMS is being absorbed into larger data/terminal platforms (FactSet, AlphaSense, TMX, SEI, ION, Confluence), so a standalone decision ledger will face "why not use what is in our terminal" objections; (2) GenAI over internal notes is commoditized, so the defensible layer is structured, gated, replayable decision data, which nobody in the set has; (3) the specialist behavioral-analytics firms are small and have not visibly raised growth capital since 2021, suggesting the "measure decisions after the fact" segment is niche and consulting-heavy.
- The remaining white space that gnosis occupies is the pre-decision and at-decision layer (typed proposals, policy/gate evaluation, chained approvals, sealed commit, per-fund implications) plus bitemporal replay. Buyers most likely to value it are compliance/COO functions reacting to SEC recordkeeping enforcement, and multi-fund CIOs needing consistent decision governance across PMs.
- Probable near-term competitive responses: RMS vendors adding "decision" object types with approval steps, and OMS vendors extending pre-trade compliance to pre-decision gating. Neither was evidenced in 2025-2026 materials found.
